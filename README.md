# Campus Ambassador Programs, open 3 November 2026

A verified hunt for every campus, student and ambassador program a student at Manipal
University Jaipur can apply to, filtered to what was actually open on 3 November 2026.

## Read this first

- `REPORT.md`: the ranked answer. Apply-this-week list, open programs, opens-soon, closed
  with next windows, every AI lab checked, chapter-only programs, method.
- `EVIDENCE.md`: verbatim quotes with URLs and fetch dates.
- `data/registry.csv`: 59 rows with status, deadline, apply URL, grade and the quote.
- `data/universe.csv`: 112 candidate URLs used for the bulk liveness sweep.
- `data/sweep.json`: what each URL returned (HTTP status, keywords, links).
- `scope/SCOPE.md`: the filters, written before research.

## Headline

15 programs were open and India-eligible on 3 November 2026. The shortest clock is
SmartBridge, closing 8 November 2026. Google Gemini Student Ambassador Semester 2 is open.
AWS runs its Student Builder Campus Leader program in India and is on rolling intake.
Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub, Cursor Campus, Notion and Internshala are closed for this cycle.

## Method

1. Enumerate names from directories, not category keywords.
2. Verify at the program's own page, record the quote, the URL and the date.
3. Grade every claim V1 (vendor page), V2 (directory or organiser post), V3 (lead).
4. Treat waitlist, coming soon and applications closed as disqualifying.

Checked 2 to 4 November 2026. Spots can close without a page change.
