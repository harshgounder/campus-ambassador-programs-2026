## What MUJ changes

- IIT-only or NIT-only programs are filtered out. Nothing above requires an IIT tag.
- Google Gemini Ambassador has a hard rule: do not graduate before 31 Dec 2026. First, second
  and third years qualify. Final years do not.
- SPHINX at MNIT Jaipur is a 20 minute commute and Jaipur students get preference in practice.
  This is the highest odds application on the list.
- AWS Campus Leader spots are per campus, so apply before another MUJ student takes the slot.
- SmartBridge has 2 seats per campus, one male and one female. If you are male, you are
  competing for one seat. Apply today, not on the 8th.

## Method

1. Enumerated names from directories, not category keywords: the Interndock 45-program
   tracker, pran.sh ambassador index, geshan's developer ambassador list, the OpenAI and
   Anthropic pages, and X posts from Sep to Nov 2026.
2. Verified at the vendor page with a fetch on 2 to 4 Nov 2026. Quotes are in `EVIDENCE.md`.
3. Graded every claim V1, V2 or V3. Only V1 rows are called OPEN.
4. Treated waitlist, coming soon and applications closed as disqualifying.
5. Chinese-language programs were checked in Chinese (校园大使), because those exist but
   are domestic.

Limits: a few sites are JavaScript-only and could not be read as text (Intel, Replit app,
Techfest portal). Those are marked UNKNOWN or "portal live" rather than OPEN. Spots can
close without a page change, so apply early.
