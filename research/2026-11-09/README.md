## How to use the records

Treat a row as a research record with a source trail, not as an instruction to apply. Check the exact role, organizer authority, observed date, current intake, country and campus rules, age and student criteria, deadline, workload, pay, and remaining uncertainty. A live form or a search result does not alone prove an open cohort. Keep confirmed open, open-appearing, rolling, undated route, future, closed, restricted, unknown, and adjacent cases distinct.

The candidate profile used for checks is 18+, second-year B.Tech Electrical and Computer Engineering at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation in 2029. The search is worldwide. India is an eligibility check for this applicant, not the search boundary.
