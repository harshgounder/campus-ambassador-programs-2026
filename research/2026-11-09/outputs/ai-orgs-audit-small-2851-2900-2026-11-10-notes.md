## Scope and method

Fifty census names received three distinct exact-name searches each (150 variations, 50 calls, zero failures). Queries targeted official student ambassador, campus representative with India context, and official academic partner/advocate/chapter routes. Results are aggregated per name. Up to two snippets per name are preserved, each no longer than 180 characters. X MCP was not used.
