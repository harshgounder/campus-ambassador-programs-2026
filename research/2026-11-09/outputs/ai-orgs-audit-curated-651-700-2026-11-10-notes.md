# AI organization census audit: rows 651–700

Cutoff used: 2026-11-09. Census source: `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; rows 651–700 are 50 organization records (header excluded). The audit retains each exact name, category and row index and does not modify the central ledgers or submit forms/contact organizers.

## Main actionable and near-actionable findings

- **Baidu / ERNIE Ambassadors:** Baidu's official program page explicitly welcomes developers, creators, organizers and student leaders and describes a rolling pipeline. Stated benefits are ERNIE API credits, premium access, event support, amplification, recognition and rewards for successful content; no fixed pay, age threshold, country list, student status proof, workload or specific close date appears on the landing page. The student and global-country fit therefore merits application-page review, but India eligibility cannot yet be called confirmed. [Official ERNIE Ambassadors page](https://ernie-ambassadors.baidu.com/)
- **Snowflake:** Snowflake Squad accepts a range of community contributors, including Builder EDU students, and expects monthly participation; current benefits are recognition and private community access, with no cash stated. The separate Data Superheroes program is global and has 2027 applications open through 2026-12-18, but seeks demonstrated expertise/contributions rather than being a student-only route. Neither page publishes an age or degree-year requirement or confirms India-specific eligibility. [Snowflake Squad](https://www.snowflake.com/en/snowflake-squad/) · [Data Superheroes](https://www.snowflake.com/en/data-superheroes/)
- **Hugging Face (HuggingFaceTB account):** the official Student Ambassador call found is historical, with a 2022 application deadline. Past requirements included active enrollment at an accredited college, validated student status, an ML/data-science course and Hugging Face Hub/library use; benefits were non-cash support, workshops, merchandise and recognition. No current cohort verified. [Official 2022 call](https://huggingface.co/blog/ambassadors)
- **LM Studio community:** search results surfaced an LM Studio Hub account named `ambassador`, but no formal, current public program rules/application were confirmed. Treat it as a weak community lead only. [LM Studio community](https://www.lmstudio.ai/community) · [Hub ambassador profile](https://www.lmstudio.ai/ambassador)
