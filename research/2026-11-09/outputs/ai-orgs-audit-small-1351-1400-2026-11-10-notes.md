## Cutoff, fit and limits

Research date: Nov 10, 2026. No dated official evidence located in this pass establishes a qualifying route open on Nov 9, 2026. Current search/page observations do not establish cutoff-day status. For any hypothetical qualifying route, worldwide access and India eligibility, age 18+, second-year status, BTech ECE, 2029 graduation, deadlines, duties, weekly workload, and cash/non-cash benefits remain unknown unless explicitly established by an official program source. No inference of eligibility or exclusion was made from silence.

Method: exact census GitHub namespace URLs were mapped for all 50 rows. Targeted public web searches were run for selected potentially organization/lab/project-like names and terms covering student, ambassador, community, hiring and research; surfaced official or first-party evidence was recorded for Pipelex and selected project/lab contexts. The central `ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv` was scanned for exact label overlap. No X MCP was used. No personal data was entered and no applications, messages, joins, or submissions were made.

This is a bounded screen, not a full official-domain, historical archive, multilingual/local-forum, or parent-company/community crawl for every namespace. Search can miss private, unindexed, local-language and differently named opportunities. Exact-name deduplication does not resolve aliases. No central ledger changes were made.
