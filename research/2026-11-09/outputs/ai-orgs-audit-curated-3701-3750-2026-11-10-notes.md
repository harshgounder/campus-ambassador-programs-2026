Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row number, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Used exact-name/topic screening and source-ledger URLs as discovery leads, then checked likely official program/identity pages. Screened for student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative or creator pathways. A broad search per name is screening, not exhaustive research.
- Required evidence that the route is owned by the exact census entity. Distinguished student-leader intake from ordinary contribution, event attendance, community membership, university-wide programs, commercial programs and third-party opportunities.
- No X MCP, forms, applications, personal data entry, accounts or outreach were used.
