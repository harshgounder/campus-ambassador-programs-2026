# AI organization census audit: rows 401–450

Audit date: 2026-11-10. Status cutoff: 2026-11-09.

## Scope and row alignment

The source was `outputs/census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`. The header was excluded when numbering data rows. The source contains 5,745 data rows; row 401 is Arize and row 450 is Lambda Cloud. The output contains exactly 50 records for data rows 401–450, preserving each source name and row ID. The list covers observability/evaluation, AI safety/security, data labeling and AI compute hardware/cloud. It does not treat ordinary hiring as an ambassador or volunteer program.

## Method

A bounded official-source web search was performed across the listed categories, then relevant prior output CSV/notes were scanned for exact-name mentions to flag likely duplicates. The research surfaced official Arize Phoenix Ambassador pages, Google Cloud student learning information, Scale AI university careers, Handshake AI program/help pages, and Oracle Academy information. These were represented with their distinct status and eligibility boundaries. Formal organization-specific routes were not independently verified for the remaining rows. Search-result absence is not evidence that a route does not exist; the CSV uses “not verified in this bounded pass.”

No application, contact, or form submission was made. No X MCP was used. No application form contents, current seat availability, or live role queues were inspected. Thus open versus closed status is asserted only where the reviewed official page supported it; undated invitations and task availability are marked accordingly. No fixed hours, stipend, age, or major requirement is inferred where not published in the evidence reviewed. India eligibility is only claimed when an official page establishes it or supports a carefully bounded inference; lack of a country statement is retained as unverified.

## Evidence and limitations

- Arize’s official Phoenix Ambassador page names community events/meetups, provides an apply link, and lists an ambassador in Bengaluru. It does not establish a deadline or country-by-country application terms: https://arize.com/phoenix-ambassadors/ and https://arize.com/phoenix-ambassador-join.
- Google Cloud’s official student page describes student learning, credits, badges and developer student clubs: https://cloud.google.com/edu/students.
- Handshake AI’s official program page describes flexible paid AI work and its official help page states US-based work authorization eligibility: https://joinhandshake.com/ai/ and https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/31620022060055-Introduction-to-the-Handshake-AI-program.
- Scale AI’s university page is a careers/internship route, not a qualifying ambassador route: https://scale.com/careers/university.
- Oracle Academy’s official page describes an institutional/educator education program and says its ambassadors are Oracle employees: https://academy.oracle.com/en/oa-partner.html.

This pass is intentionally conservative and is not an exhaustive legal or recruitment eligibility review. “No exact-name match” means no duplicate was found in the relevant reviewed output files, not that the organization is absent from every historical artifact. Hardware/cloud suffix variants can share identity with base-name organizations; their source-row identity is preserved, and duplicates are called out where detected.
