# AI organization exact-name audit — source rows 1351–1400

Prepared 2026-11-10. Opportunity-status cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for relevance: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur, India, graduating 2029.

## Method and limits

Ran one exact-name Exa search for each of the 50 census labels, targeting official student/campus ambassador, developer advocate, community champion, representative, and student-leader opportunities. Searches were run in ten sequential batches of five (never more than five concurrent searches) to reduce rate-limit risk. All 50 searches returned successfully; no query failure was treated as a no-hit. Search results served discovery only. Followed surfaced primary/official sources for the strongest leads and checked the supplied candidate profile against published criteria where available.

A single exact-name search is bounded and can miss differently named, non-English, poorly indexed, or unindexed programs. A non-hit means no qualifying route was confirmed in this pass, not proof of absence. Generic campus program links mixed into results were excluded unless they were for the exact organization. Internships and salaried jobs, research, hackathons, courses, ordinary open-source contributions, affiliate/referral programs, and generic memberships are not counted as formal student leadership opportunities.

No forms were opened for submission, accounts or outreach were not made, no shared central ledger was edited, and no X MCP was used.

## Findings

No new, exact-entity student/campus ambassador or student-leader opportunity was confirmed in this slice.

- **AAIF / Goose, row 1369:** The official AAIF Ambassador Program page covers advocacy for AAIF projects including Goose, MCP, and AGENTS.md. It asks ambassadors for one public project-based contribution each month. The official site describes rolling applications for a January 2027 intake and no student-only restriction. Direct cash compensation is not stated; public recognition, private community access, leadership/maintainer briefings, and event-related perks are described. This is a global developer/community program rather than a campus appointment. India is not separately discussed, with no country restriction found on the page. The candidate’s hands-on AAIF project experience is unknown. This is an exact duplicate of central AI/ML priority verification ledger row 235, so it is not a new route.
- **Meilisearch, row 1359:** Search surfaced a closed project issue proposing a “Champions program.” The issue is a historical internal concept/discussion, last updated in February 2023; it does not establish active recruitment, a current program, or an application route.
- **FlowiseAI, row 1371:** The official Flowise affiliate page offers referral commissions. This is commercial affiliate participation, not an appointed student/campus role.
- **ClickHouse, row 1386:** Search surfaced a salaried Developer/Community Advocate job listing for the Americas that states applications are no longer accepted and describes frequent travel and a major-airport proximity requirement. This is a professional job, not a campus program; its official-employer status and precise closure date were not independently verified.
- **Apache, row 1398:** Search returned an individual profile describing past Campus Ambassador activity with the Apache Software Foundation. The ASF official community resources page lists local communities, contribution pathways, and GSoC but no current campus ambassador intake. The individual affiliation is not evidence of an official current route.
- **Deepfakes, row 1364:** Search found the Aster Ellis Campus Advocate Program from the Digital Identity Foundation. The program addresses deepfake abuse, but the sponsor is not the census entity “deepfakes”; topic overlap alone is not an exact-entity program and was excluded.
- **Upstash, row 1351:** Search found an official full-time Open Source and Developer Relations Engineer position. Its community-facing duties and event budget do not change its status as salaried professional employment rather than a student appointment. The page is undated, so availability on the cutoff date is unverified.

The remaining exact-name searches did not confirm a qualifying official route. Many returned generic campus programs, third-party opportunities, past individual affiliations, or routine OSS/community contribution paths; these are excluded from the findings rather than treated as exact-entity routes.
