## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row ID, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Used exact-name/topic screening and the source URLs as discovery leads, with official-page checks for plausible matches. Screened for student/campus ambassador, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative and creator programs. This is a bounded screen rather than exhaustive per-country research.
- Required exact-entity program ownership. Generic listings, namesake programs, ordinary contributions, event participation, recruitment/career pages and university-wide programs not owned by a census entity are not counted as qualifying student-leader routes.
- No X MCP, applications, forms, personal data entry, accounts or outreach were used.
