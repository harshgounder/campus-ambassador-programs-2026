## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows and preserved the exact census name, tier, and category from `/home/liebert511/campus-ambassador-hunt/data/ai-orgs-census.csv`.
- Used exact-handle and grouped targeted searches for student/campus ambassador, community champion/advocate, fellows, chapters, representatives, creators, and developer/software community routes. Official organizer pages were used for the two attributable leads below. A search non-hit is not treated as proof that no opportunity exists.
- Exact GitHub profile URLs are identity locators, not proof that every handle denotes a formal organization. Parent companies and unrelated initiatives are kept separate from exact census entities.
- Compared names against the central AI ledgers and prior curated audit slices. Census/catalog rows are not counted as route duplicates.
