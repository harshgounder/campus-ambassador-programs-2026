# AI organization census audit — source rows 4451–4500

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive entries from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row ID, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Screened exact census identifiers against student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative and community-leader terms. Census URLs were discovery leads, not proof of ownership. Likely program pages received first-party checks. This is a bounded screen, not exhaustive research.
- Kept formal student-leader programs separate from university recruiting, developer communities, commercial partners, sponsorship, open-source contribution, accelerators and generic community access.
- No X MCP, forms, applications, personal data entry, messages or outreach occurred.

## Exact-entity findings and mismatch checks

- **robocasa (row 4452):** The official [Robot Framework Ambassadors page](https://robotframework.org/ambassador/) describes an active rolling-quarter ambassador program that supports students and learners. It is operated by the Robot Framework community, not RoboCasa; no relationship is established. Thus it is not attributed to this row.
- **kubb-labs (row 4500):** The census links Open Science Labs (OSL). OSL’s first-party [Ambassadors Program](https://opensciencelabs.org/partnership/ambassadors/) is a formal global program connecting universities, research groups, students and open-source communities. Its current page says the application link is coming soon; applicants should be active OSL contributors, normally with at least three months’ involvement, and at least 18. The program is volunteer and has a 12-month term. OSL is separate from Kubb Labs, with no relationship established, so the route is not attributed to row 4500. Current page wording is not backdated to Nov 9.
- **ai-infra-curriculum (row 4490):** The listed [Arduino & Qualcomm Ambassador Program](https://www.arduino.cc/ambassador-program/) is first-party to Arduino/Qualcomm. Its current page describes a global developer ambassador community, minimum age 18, and countries/territories subject to restrictions. It is not owned by ai-infra-curriculum; no relationship is established. The current terms do not prove Nov 9 availability or India-specific fit.
- **edyoda (row 4495):** The census URL is a third-party Internshala campus ambassador listing. EdYoda’s current first-party site presents online education/training services but no reviewed first-party confirmation of that ambassador intake. The historical listing is not counted as an active route; its dated availability and eligibility remain unverified.
- **OpenAdaptAI (row 4456):** The first-party [OpenAdapt partner page](https://openadapt.ai/partners) describes commercial workflow/product embedding partnerships. It is not a student ambassador or community advocate intake.
- **Mcp-Brasil (row 4468):** The Agentic AI Foundation ambassador opportunity in the census links is run by a separate organizer. No evidence ties that program to MCP Brasil.
- **OpenOSINT (row 4472):** Its first-party project pages provide sponsorship support routes; no student ambassador intake was confirmed. Sponsorship is not student leadership.
- **generative-computing (row 4477):** The cited CMU AI Accelerator is a venture accelerator, and TAMU and marimo ambassador pages are separate organizers. No exact-entity student route was established.
- **beam-cloud (row 4483):** The Apache Beam community/ambassador material is not evidence of a Beam Cloud-owned ambassador program; no relationship between the two identities is established.
- **NORLAB / norlab-ulaval (row 4486):** The cited university page recruits research students; it does not describe an ambassador or community representative role.
- **Other entries:** Generic directories and programs from unrelated providers—including Claude, EDAD, SwytchCode, Solana, Technovation, Pearson, Microsoft, Devsinc, Arduino and other universities—were excluded unless exact-entity ownership could be established. Project contribution, sponsorship, a generic developer community or an event listing was not treated as an ambassador intake.

Exact identity/ownership, cutoff availability, location and academic criteria, application steps, selection, term, workload, compensation and benefits remain unresolved unless directly supported above.
