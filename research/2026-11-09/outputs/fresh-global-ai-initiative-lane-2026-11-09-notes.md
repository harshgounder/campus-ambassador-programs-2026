## Status vocabulary used

“Rolling” means the organizer states registrations or contributions remain open over time. “Open/time-bounded” denotes an active participant route with a stated publication cutoff, while noting later contributions are accepted. “Adjacent” means relevant participation but not an ambassador/advocacy role. India/global eligibility is only marked where source text supports it; otherwise it remains unconfirmed.
