## Limitations and handling

“Open-appearing” means the organizer currently presents a form or invitation but does not give a dated open/close notice. It is weaker evidence than explicit “applications open” or “year-round.” A global roster or global programme description is not treated as proof of unrestricted citizenship/residency eligibility. Missing country rules are marked unconfirmed. Reward amounts, internships, event prizes, or hiring preference are not described as guaranteed pay. No forms were filled, submitted, or entered; organizers were not contacted.

The search is a bounded follow-up, not an exhaustive census of every worldwide SWE/cyber program. A few additional source hits were excluded or left in gaps when current intake was unclear, the relevant cohort was already selected, or a page referred to a different kind of program (e.g., fellowships, events, or general community signup).
