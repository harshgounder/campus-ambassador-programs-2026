# Computer vision, OCR, speech/audio/music, and dataset communities — 9 November 2026

## Scope and method

This is a bounded follow-up for the documented profile (18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur, India, graduating 2029). It targets computer vision and pattern recognition, OCR/document intelligence, speech/audio/music AI, and open datasets/benchmarks. Discovery used Parallel and Exa search; status and criteria were checked against owner or organizer pages, official project documentation, and the project repositories themselves. Localized discovery included Chinese-language PaddleOCR documentation and searches for Japanese and Korean OCR/CV communities. No X MCP was used, no forms were opened or submitted, and no organizer was contacted.

The CSV deliberately keeps three kinds of record distinct:

1. **Ongoing contributor/community paths:** Mozilla Common Voice, AI4Bharat's IndicNLP Catalog, PaddleOCR, OpenCV, IEEE Computer Society TCPAMI, and the niche Manga OCR repository.
2. **Dated team opportunity:** DCASE 2027 task-organizer proposals, with an intent deadline of 10 December and proposal deadline of 15 December 2026.
3. **Conditional or weak discovery routes:** IEEE ASLI 2027 challenge-organizer proposals (deadline 3 December), the volunteer-run AI Music Embassy network, and PHOCR's public project repository. The last two are not formal student intakes; PHOCR's recent activity was not verified.

## Best-supported routes

- **Indic speech/data contribution:** Common Voice has current, ongoing voice, validation, sentence-writing, and transcription routes and links language communities. Its language directory was updated on 8 November 2026. The Terms require parent/guardian consent and supervision for contributors aged 19 or younger, which matters if the user is 18 or 19. Contributions are CC0; this is voluntary participation, not an appointed role.
- **Indic datasets and benchmarks:** AI4Bharat's official IndicNLP resource catalog explicitly asks people to propose new resources by issue or pull request and says contributors will be acknowledged. It is a practical contribution route for adding well-documented Indic speech/OCR resources. It does not constitute recruitment by AI4Bharat.
- **OCR code/docs:** PaddleOCR publishes current community-contribution guidance covering features, bug fixes, documentation/translations, and multilingual corpora. This is a public open-source route; the documentation sets no nationality or student rule and does not promise India-specific eligibility.
- **Computer vision:** OpenCV has an active public technical forum and code repository contribution instructions. The forum showed activity on 6 November, including OCR preprocessing discussion. It is a project community, not a formal campus ambassador program; its own forum distinguishes GSoC coordination from forum participation.
- **Field society:** IEEE Computer Society TCPAMI is explicitly free and open to all and covers computer vision, pattern recognition, and image processing. IEEE CS describes separate conference and local-chapter volunteer routes, but this research did not verify an active TCPAMI vacancy or a role at MUJ. The chapter locator lists numerous India student chapters, including some with a displayed open leadership position; that does not establish a MUJ position.
- **Near-term benchmark organizing:** DCASE invites 2027 acoustic-scene/event challenge task proposals. It encourages collaborative bids and provides its mailing list/Slack for team formation. The 10 December intent deadline and 15 December proposal deadline are close; the work involves preparing data, instructions, scoring, results, and a workshop presentation, so it is research-team-scale rather than a lightweight ambassador application.
- **Near-term speech/audio organizing:** IEEE ASLI 2027 accepts challenge proposals across audio, speech, and language intelligence until 3 December 2026. The proposal needs named organizers and affiliations, challenge data/baselines/evaluation, and a scientific committee. It is a conditional route for a student who can assemble a research team; the call says nothing specific about India-based applicants.

## Language and region coverage

The sources provide direct community paths for Indic resources and Chinese/multilingual OCR through AI4Bharat and PaddleOCR, and a Japanese OCR-specific repository in Manga OCR. PHOCR's owner-maintained repository advertises Chinese, Japanese, and Korean support and welcomes contributions, but this pass could not establish a recent maintenance cadence; it stays a weak discovery lead. Localized Japanese/Korean searches did not surface a verifiable student-representative or campus-lead intake. No India-specific eligibility should be inferred from an English-language public repository unless the organizer states it.

## Limits

This is not an exhaustive inventory of all CV/OCR/speech/audio/music groups, student societies, datasets, conferences, or language communities. Public contribution guidance is evidence of an available route, not acceptance, active mentorship, or selection. Public chapter lists and repositories do not prove a vacancy at the user's campus. Geographic rules are silent for most routes; the notes preserve that silence rather than claiming universal eligibility. No open formal student ambassador/representative intake specific to CV/OCR/speech/music was verified in this pass. All statuses are a 9 November 2026 snapshot.
