## Scope and limits

Five targeted Exa web searches covered the five organizations above, prioritizing official project pages and dated sources. The other 45 rows were not independently researched in this pass: their source-ledger URLs are retained in the CSV as **unreviewed discovery leads**, not as verified identity or program evidence. For those rows, role existence, cutoff status, geography, student eligibility, local-language channels, deadlines, compensation, and time commitments remain unassessed. No-hit claims are not made for them.

The user environment did not have the `agent-reach` command installed (`agent-reach doctor --json` returned `command not found`). Search continued through the connected Exa MCP fallback documented by the available research workflow. No Exa call reported an API error. Search output was bounded; no full-site crawl or archived-state check was performed. Chinese/local channels were only partially represented by official English/Chinese web pages; WeChat, Douyin, Bilibili, Weibo, and account-gated content were not accessed. No X MCP was used.

Cutoff claims are based only on dates explicitly visible in first-party material. Nov 10 page observations are not treated as proof of Nov 9 availability. Open-appearing, closed/limited, restricted, adjacent, and no-hit states remain distinct; the other 45 rows are marked unassessed rather than no-hit.

No forms were submitted, accounts created, personal information entered, or organizations contacted. No source repository, source ledger, or source row was edited.
