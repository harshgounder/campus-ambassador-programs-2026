# AI Africa and MENA priority pass — 9 November 2026

## Scope and method

Focused on AI-first ambassador, campus lead, chapter, and community leadership opportunities in African and MENA ecosystems. Included civic-tech/public-sector AI and regional youth community routes where a meaningful campus/community-lead function was explicit. Used English, French, and Arabic organizer-source discovery. Social posts/directories were discovery only unless published by the organizer; no X MCP was used. No forms were submitted and no organizers were contacted.

## Search coverage

- Search queries: 11 total across the pass (9 English, 1 French, and 1 Arabic).
- Languages searched: English, French, Arabic.
- Pages opened in the browser reader: 5 URLs attempted; 3 returned substantive page text (Udara program page, NSK AI organizer LinkedIn post, AIforGhana student page), one official Gebeya page returned no readable lines, and the Jadeel Arabic page was inaccessible to the reader. Exa returned extracted organizer-page text for Jadeel and AI4African Girls, but that is weaker than a full direct-page inspection.
- Sources written to the CSV: organizer-owned pages and one organizer-country-director LinkedIn announcement.

## Results and exclusions

- Added three new records: Jadeel Arabic campus ambassadors (intake and India eligibility unknown); AIforGhana Campus Ambassadors (track exists, but intake and India eligibility unknown); and NSK AI/Udara Campus Lead (Africa-only 2026 call closed October 31; program page says the Build phase concluded).
- Deduped without re-adding existing records: Dala Studio/Gebeya, Edad AI, AI for African Girls, AMLD Africa, Global AI Youth Network, and GAIN SSA campus organizing circles. These already appear in earlier priority or regional outputs.
- Excluded ITU/AI for Good AI and Robotics Youth Training Programme as a school-age training and teacher-support program, not a student ambassador/campus lead role. Its current five-country pilots include Côte d’Ivoire, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, and South Africa; it does not provide an individual university ambassador opening.
- Excluded AI Sprouts Africa because the surfaced ambassador call is from September 2025 and no current organizer-owned intake/status was verified.
- Excluded Rural AI Ambassador Programme (RTIH/Head Held High) from this Africa/MENA pass because the reviewed opportunity is a location-specific Andhra Pradesh rural immersion program, not a regional African/MENA ecosystem. It is already in the separate AI education/chapter priority pass.

## Limitations

This is a focused priority pass, not an exhaustive census of African or MENA AI organizations. Several regional programs publish broad role descriptions without dates, application criteria, country coverage, compensation, or workload. A live Apply/Join button or general contact line was not treated as proof of an open selection. Missing geography was left unknown; India eligibility was not inferred. Arabic page wording was extracted from the organizer's page but could not be independently opened in the browser reader, so its interpretation should be treated cautiously.
