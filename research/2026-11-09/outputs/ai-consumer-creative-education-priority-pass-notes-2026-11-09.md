# AI consumer, creative, and education priority pass — 2026-11-09

## Scope and method

Fresh web discovery for AI consumer apps, creative/design/video/image/voice products, AI tutors/education products, and vertical AI companies with campus ambassador, creator partner, or community-leader opportunities. Discovery queries included:

- `AI student ambassador program creative AI design video image official student ambassador program`
- `AI tutor student ambassador program education app official campus ambassador AI tutor`
- `AI consumer app student creator ambassador program official AI voice image generator ambassador`
- `AI productivity app student ambassador campus program official 2026`
- Focused follow-ups for `Akili campus ambassadors`, `Sovi.AI student ambassador`, `SOIAA campus ambassador`, and `EaseLearn AI campus ambassador`.

The prescribed Agent Reach check could not run because `agent-reach` was not installed/available in this environment. Search discovery therefore used the available web search tool. No X connector/MCP was used. Official organizer pages were opened and read for the three included rows; search-result snippets were treated only as discovery, not as proof.

## Organizer-page checks

- SOIAA: `https://www.soiaa.com/en/campus/` — page says `PROGRAM OPEN`, university students only, but also describes the service as invite-only and requires an emailed code. It states activity fees/rewards and token/point benefits without defining amounts, conversion, or currency. Footer identifies DEEPCON Inc. in Seoul; page does not say participation is Korea-only.
- EaseLearn AI: `https://easelearn.ai/hiring` and `https://easelearn.ai/` — hiring page says remote, takes college/university/department details, requires a Product Hunt upvote, and advertises subscription commissions, Premium access, and an internship certificate. It gives no country/age/year limits, deadline, payout rate, or workload. Search also surfaced an `easelearn.in` India K-12 brand; relation to `easelearn.ai` was not established.
- Sovi.AI: `https://mysovi.ai/apps/ugc/` and `https://mysovi.ai/apps/career/?skip_mask=true` — live creator application page explicitly limits creators to US-based people ages 18–24 who use iOS. Per-batch pay and contingent view bonuses are listed. The page calls for an agreement granting worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free usage rights for campaign videos. India-based user is outside the stated geography.

## Dedupe and exclusions

- Searched existing `outputs/*.csv` for SOIAA, EaseLearn, and Sovi.AI; no matching program rows were found.
- Excluded Akili because `outputs/ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv` already contains its Cohort 1 student ambassador row.
- Excluded Microsoft Copilot Student Ambassador and Grammarly Campus Collective because reviewed ledger outputs already contain both; official pages narrow eligibility to US schools for Grammarly and affiliated campuses for Microsoft, so this pass did not duplicate them.
- Excluded Retriever AI because it is already present in the prior AI model-products pass and limits applications to US universities.
- Excluded Luma AI Student Ambassador as already recorded in `outputs/ai-model-companies-agent-delta-2026-11-09.csv` as future-interest only.
- Excluded OpenAI Student Collective and Claude Campus because they are already covered by prior AI education and AI product-company rows.

## Limitations

This is a focused candidate pass, not a census of every AI product. Small or region-specific programs may not be indexed. `OPEN` means the organizer displays an application route/status, not that a place is guaranteed or that the applicant meets unpublished eligibility rules. For SOIAA and EaseLearn, international eligibility remains unstated; compensation details for both are incomplete. Sovi.AI is a valid new program lead but not an eligible lead for this India-based user under its posted US-only condition. No forms were filled or submitted, and no organizers were contacted.
