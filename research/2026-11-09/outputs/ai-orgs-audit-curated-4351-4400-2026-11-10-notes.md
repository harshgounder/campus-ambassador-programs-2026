# AI organization census audit — source rows 4351–4400

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive entries from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row ID, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Screened exact identifiers against student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative and community-leader terms. Census URLs were discovery leads only. Reviewed likely first-party matches; this is a bounded screen, not exhaustive research.
- Separated formal student-leader routes from commercial partnerships, professional recruitment, general developer networks, open-source contributions, accelerator participation, and unrelated providers’ programs.
- No X MCP, forms, applications, personal data entry, messages or outreach occurred.

## Exact-entity findings and mismatches

- **Qualcomm results vs `bottenderjs` (row 4359) and `evloghq` (row 4387):** Qualcomm’s official [Ambassador Program](https://www.qualcomm.com/developer/qualcomm-ambassador-program) expressly includes “Student Ambassadors,” describes a global developer community and states age 18+ and worldwide availability except listed restricted countries. A separate official [Qualcomm Student Ambassador Program](https://www.qualcomm.com/research/university-relations/qualcomm-student-ambassador-program) says student selection is invite-only, based on prior participation in Qualcomm-affiliated activities, and has additional compliance checks for non-US students. These are meaningful Qualcomm leads, but neither census row is Qualcomm and no ownership/affiliation evidence connects the exact source identifiers to Qualcomm. They are excluded from row-level qualifying routes. The reviewed pages do not establish whether either Qualcomm program was open/available on Nov 9, nor do they explicitly resolve India eligibility for an application.
- **julius-speech (row 4355):** The census includes a `julius.ai/ambassador` lead. Julius AI’s current first-party changelog describes its public affiliate and creator program; this is a separate Julius AI product/company, and no evidence ties it to the exact `julius-speech` identifier. A Julius AI program is not attributed to this row. FIRE Campus Scholars is also an unrelated organizer.
- **feathr-ai (row 4351):** The official [Feathr Partner Program](https://www.feathr.co/partner-program) is a commercial referral, co-marketing and integration program for nonprofits/associations and business partners. It is not a student ambassador route. Feathr’s relation to the census label `feathr-ai` as a precise entity name is not fully established.
- **Tuya (row 4376):** Tuya’s first-party [developer ecosystem partner page](https://developer.tuya.com/en/partner) provides commercial software/hardware/service/integration partner pathways. Its developer platform and general community are open technical participation routes. The reviewed pages do not describe a campus ambassador or student champion intake; partner eligibility is not treated as student eligibility.
- **BCG-X-Official (row 4357):** BCG X’s [Social Impact Accelerator](https://www.bcg.com/x/social-impact-accelerator/about) supports ventures and initiatives through an accelerator. It is not a student ambassador or campus representative program.
- **Other explicit adjacent leads:** Bilibili activity listings (row 4352), uTensor and GPflow open-source work (rows 4362 and 4377), Tuya and other developer communities (row 4376), AWS Student Builder Campus Leaders (row 4392), Stanford general opportunities and GDG on-campus communities (row 4399), and Open Mercato’s unrelated Solana/Claude/Devin links (row 4395) are not attributed as exact-entity student-leader routes.
- **`visual-layer` (row 4382):** The census lists Massing Labs and MemMachine ambassador pages. No ownership or affiliation with Visual Layer was established; these external routes are excluded.
- **Remaining entries:** Bounded exact-name screening did not confirm a student/campus/community ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter or student representative intake owned by the exact census entity. Generic directories and unrelated providers’ programs (including IncuXai, Claude, EDAD, SwytchCode, Solana, PyTorch, Technovation, and college/university programs) were not counted absent evidence of ownership.
