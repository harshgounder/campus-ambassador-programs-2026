# AI organization census audit — source rows 1451–1500

Prepared: 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for relevance: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur (India), graduating 2029.

## Method and limits

Ran one exact-name Exa web search for each of the 50 source labels. Searches were issued serially in batches of at most five, with a delay between requests. Every query returned a result payload; none of the exact-name queries was treated as a no-hit because of a failed search. Exact-name searches were discovery evidence, not exhaustive web coverage. They may miss differently named, poorly indexed, non-English, or unlisted opportunities, and common names/handles can yield unrelated results. A no-confirmation result means no qualifying route was confirmed in this bounded pass; it does not prove absence.

Screened the returned sources for official ownership and for role fit. Followed primary pages for Alibaba Cloud and SigNoz. Reviewed the Alibaba Cloud Academic Empowerment Program and its distinct 2022 Indonesia student-ambassador campaign, plus SigNoz's dated program announcement and repository program document. Reviewed prior AI audit and verification outputs for duplicate actionable routes, including the core AI/ML priority delta and earlier curated batches. A Qwen result on the Alibaba search was a distinct program already recorded elsewhere and was not attributed to the Alibaba entity label in this census row. No X MCP was used.

## Findings

Two official role/program leads were confirmed:

- **Alibaba Cloud Student Ambassador / student advocate listing, row 1485:** Alibaba Cloud's current live [Academic Empowerment Program page](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/academy/university?_p_lc=1) describes student support for students at member institutions and lists a Student Ambassador component with additional resources for student advocates and invitations to local community events. The page is undated and does not specify an ambassador selection route, criteria, duties, term, hours, or cash compensation; the page therefore establishes a listed program feature, not an open recruitment as of Nov 9. The candidate's eligibility is unresolved because the page does not identify Manipal University Jaipur as a member institution and does not state ambassador-specific India, age, year, or major criteria. Alibaba Cloud's separate [Indonesia Student Ambassador campaign](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/campaign/student-ambassador-idn-2022?_p_lc=9) required final-year students in semester 5 or later and GPA above 3.5, with a three-month campus promotion/event-planning/representative term, mentoring, training/certification, merchandise, and a possible subsequent six-month paid internship. Its stated application deadline was Feb 25, 2022; it is historical and does not fit the supplied second-year profile.

- **SigNoz Community Advocate Program, row 1498:** SigNoz's official [announcement](https://signoz.io/blog/community-advocate-program/) is dated July 29, 2025, and its [repository program document](https://github.com/SigNoz/signoz/blob/main/ADVOCATE.md) confirms the route. Duties include helping users in Slack and GitHub Discussions, explaining self-hosted implementations, sharing observability knowledge through content/forums, hosting regional meetups, and giving product feedback or beta testing. Benefits listed are official recognition, welcome hamper, swag in the first three months, optional website feature, early access, direct team access, and product influence; no cash payment, workload, or term is stated. The announcement says the program is invite-only and directs interested people to join Slack and express interest; it does not give a public application or selection criteria. No age, student, degree, or country restriction is published, but India eligibility is not explicit. The program was publicly described by the cutoff; no evidence found that invite-only status changed by Nov 9. Candidate fit remains unconfirmed because selection is invite-only and geography/profile terms are unstated.

Searches for other source labels returned unrelated organizations/programs, historical material, hiring, personal profiles, ordinary contribution paths, general community membership, or generic third-party campus-ambassador pages. These were excluded unless an official source connected the exact source entity to a qualifying role. For example, the X Developer Student Ambassadors page surfaced for `xitu` but no connection to the Xitu entity was verified; TabbyML surfaced developer-advocate hiring, which is employment rather than a student/representative program. No qualifying exact-entity route was confirmed for the other 48 rows in this bounded search pass.
