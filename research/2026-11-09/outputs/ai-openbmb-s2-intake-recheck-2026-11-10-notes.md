I did not request edit access.

## Fit and requirements

The user's B.Tech ECE background maps well to the program's open-source AI and on-device AI work, and no age, nationality, major, or academic-year restriction appeared in the checked sources. The official FAQ says applicants do not need to have used MiniCPM before. However, the application form requires examples of MiniCPM community contributions, technical background, community influence, and planned activities. The user's current profile does not establish those portfolio or community gates, so selection fit remains conditional.

The S2 post lists technical tutorials, applications, code contributions, feedback, and developer community work. It mentions visibility, team access, deployment help, 1,000 API credits, event kits or funding, a badge, and a certificate. It does not state cash pay, a weekly workload, or a term.
