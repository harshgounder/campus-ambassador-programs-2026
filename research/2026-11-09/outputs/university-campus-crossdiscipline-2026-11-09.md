# University and campus ambassador search — 9 November 2026

## What I checked

I reviewed fresh official-page/search-result leads for university and campus representative roles across civic engagement, science, education, climate, and international student support. I checked the existing reviewed ledgers before adding rows. The CSV contains nine distinct source-page records that were not already recorded as the same opportunity in the reviewed program ledgers; it deliberately includes closed and institution-restricted examples so status and eligibility are not mistaken for global access.

## Potentially relevant leads

- **An Taisce Climate Ambassador 2027** is the only promising new open lead with apparently broad language. Its official programme page says applications are open; a TU Dublin official page says “Everyone can apply,” but groups and activities are Ireland-based. Residency, remote participation, and the deadline remain unknown, so this is not a confirmed worldwide opportunity.
- **NTU Spring 2027 Welcome Ambassador** has an open application through Nov 22, 2026 and minimum-wage hourly pay. The announcement does not state clearly whether applicants must be NTU students, while it requires strong Chinese and English and a valid work permit for international students. It is on-site in Taiwan, so MUJ eligibility is unresolved and likely impractical without NTU affiliation and work authorization.
- **EU Careers Student Ambassadors 2027/28** is a future, not-yet-open call. The official page says applications begin in Feb/March 2027 but requires EU citizenship and enrollment at a participating university; it does not fit MUJ on known facts.

Other records are formally open or recent but require enrollment at the named institution or Hong Kong, so they are not available to an MUJ student as currently described. APS is an explicit worldwide student program, but the 2026–27 cohort is selected and applications are closed.

## Coverage limit

This is a targeted discovery pass, not an exhaustive inventory. Search snippets helped identify dated calls, but direct official-page opens failed or timed out for some sites (notably EOC Hong Kong and An Taisce/TU Dublin); those limitations are explicitly recorded in the CSV. No application was submitted and no account was created.
