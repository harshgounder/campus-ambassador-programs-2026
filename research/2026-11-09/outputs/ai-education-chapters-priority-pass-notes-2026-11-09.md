# AI education and campus leadership priority pass — 2026-11-09

## Scope and method

- Goal: identify current AI education, student chapter, youth/community, campus organizer, ambassador and AI ethics/policy leadership opportunities with direct fit notes for an 18+ second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, graduating 2029.
- Discovery used the connected Exa web-search tool: 37 successful query calls, each requesting up to five results. Queries covered global programs, India, US/Europe, Africa and Asia; English queries predominated, with Spanish/French-language discovery attempted. Search result yield varied. Exa Agent Reach CLI was unavailable in the shell (`agent-reach: command not found`); connected Exa MCP search/fetch worked.
- 11 official pages were fetched for detailed review. Search result pages were treated only as discovery; official publisher pages were used for the program facts/status in the CSV.
- Date cut-off: 2026-11-09 (Asia/Kolkata). No applications or interest forms were submitted. No one was contacted. No X MCP or social account research was used.
- Coverage is a priority pass, not an exhaustive global registry. Search coverage favored English-indexed official pages; multilingual depth and country-by-country completeness are limited. A program page that omits a geography, age, or degree requirement is marked unclear rather than assumed global/open.

## Included status treatment

- OpenAI Student Collective and Claude Campus are genuine AI campus-lead programs, but their 2026 intakes had closed by the census date; OpenAI's future-interest route is distinguished from a live cohort application.
- Pathfinder Spring 2027 is listed as a future opportunity because the official page says applications open 2026-11-10; it was not open yet on the census date.
- RTIH Rural AI Ambassador page explicitly displays “Open Call,” but dates are undated and delivery is a seven-day, location-bound rural Andhra Pradesh program; “open call” is not treated as proof of remaining capacity.
- AAAI and ACM SIGAI are chapter-organizing routes, not personal ambassador roles. Global scope, membership requirements, local chapter overlap and ongoing group commitments are distinguished.
- Women in AI and Responsible by Design pages establish program descriptions/rosters, but not a verifiable current open intake. Their rows say intake unclear.
- GitHub Campus Experts and AI4ALL Ignite are adjacent tech-community/AI education programs, not AI ambassador positions. GitHub's annual July cycle is closed by the cut-off; AI4ALL's published US enrollment rule makes it ineligible for the India-based student.

## Exclusions and uncertainty

- Google Gemini Student Ambassador 2026: discovered through university/community/user-generated posts and a Google Help forum thread, but no official Google program application page or eligibility notice was verified. Excluded as an opportunity; do not treat the third-party dates/region claims as official.
- AI Council of India ambassador tracks: official site advertises ambassador roles, but requires approved member verification before applying and the student membership page displays fee tiers; exact fees, current cohort status and independent validation of program terms were not established in this pass. Excluded pending a focused legitimacy/terms review.
- UNESCO Youth Hackathon: official 2026 activity concerned media and information literacy rather than AI-specific student leadership; not included as an AI program.
- General Microsoft Student Ambassadors: current Microsoft Student Hub presents a broad technology learner community and is AI-adjacent, but not an AI-specific ambassador opportunity; excluded from this AI-focused priority CSV.
- GDG on Campus was kept separate from general Google Developer Groups organizer requirements. Current campus-specific selection window and precise eligibility could not be verified; the CSV row is the general GDG community-organizer route, clearly labeled adjacent and not student-only.
- Discovery results referencing programs in social posts or third-party opportunity lists were not promoted to verified open opportunities.
