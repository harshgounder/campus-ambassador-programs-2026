# Methods and limits: rows 351–400

Audit date: 2026-11-10. Evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. The audited source was `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; data-row numbering excluded the header. Alignment was checked before research: row 351 is Beam AI and row 400 is LangSmith. Exactly 50 rows are included.

## Method

For each name, an individual Exa/Agent Reach web search targeted official student, campus ambassador, developer contributor, community, challenge, and volunteer routes. Official organization, product, documentation, repository, community, and program pages were retained as identity/context URLs or leads. Search snippets were screened for apparent namesakes and unrelated programs. Existing output CSVs were scanned for exact organization-name mentions. All 50 naturally reappear in census/search discovery ledgers; those name-only discovery repetitions are not treated as duplicate actionable student routes. The duplicate field indicates no exact actionable student-route duplicate was confirmed in the reviewed rows.

## Interpretation

“No verified route” means this bounded search did not verify a formal student ambassador/campus route; it does not establish nonexistence. General open-source contribution, community participation, meetups, product documentation, employment, and hackathons are recorded as leads where relevant and are not promoted to formal student routes. The Rabbit internship forum item, Portkey 2024 Hacktoberfest, LangChain ambassador listing, Cloudflare-wide community program, and PyTorch Foundation cohort references are not evidence of an open organization-specific student intake. Search surfaced likely namesake collisions for Beam, Comet, and Martian; these were not counted as routes.

No verified route in this pass yields no verified intake status, India eligibility, age/student/major rules, hours, or benefits/cash. “Not stated / unconfirmed” is not a claim of ineligibility. This audit did not contact organizations or submit applications. It did not use X MCP. Search result coverage and surfaced official pages are incomplete; status labels are limited to what was verifiable by the cutoff.
