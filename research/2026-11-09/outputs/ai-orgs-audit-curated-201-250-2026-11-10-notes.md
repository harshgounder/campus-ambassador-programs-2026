# Methods and limits: curated census rows 201–250

**Audit date:** 2026-11-10  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-11-09  
**Range:** Exactly census rows 201–250, preserving the names, tiers, and categories from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`.

## Method

- Checked official organizer/product pages and current search excerpts for student/campus/ambassador, creator, developer community, open-source, competition, volunteer, or closely adjacent routes.
- Used previously prepared official-source research in `ai-consumer-creative-education-priority-pass-2026-11-09.csv`, `ai-hardware-robotics-priority-pass-2026-11-09.csv`, and `ai-nonllm-modalities-official-program-sweep-2026-11-09.csv` as cross-checks where relevant.
- Added official home/product pages as identity/source anchors for organizations with no qualifying route found. These anchors do not prove an absence of programs.
- Classified student discounts and creator programs as adjacent leads rather than student leadership programs; company careers and generic product communities were not promoted to ambassador routes.
- Cross-checked duplicate mentions against existing shared program research artifacts. The new output is a slice only; no shared central ledger was edited.
- No application, organizer contact, form submission, or X MCP use was performed.

## Main route findings

- **Adobe Firefly / Adobe:** The official Adobe Express Ambassador page describes a global creator, entrepreneur, and marketer community with an apply action, but it is not Firefly-specific and does not make student status a requirement. The separate Adobe NEXT// campus leader page describes a paid current-student program for film/design students in the UK and US; this is a real student route but only adjacent to Firefly. India is not listed for this NEXT// cohort.
- **Canva:** Official pages describe Campus Canvassadors and an India-specific Canva Champs Student Marketing path. The Campus Canvassadors page has rolling applications “through August” without an unambiguous year, while Canva’s help center says the distinct Canvassador affiliate program is currently closed. These are separate pathways; 2026-11-09 live status of the India student path remains unconfirmed.
- **Runway:** Official help content offers a 25% student subscription discount, with university/college student verification and applications accepted anywhere except China. This is not an ambassador or leadership opportunity; India is included only geographically, subject to individual verification.
- **Luma AI:** Official Creative Partner Program page says it is reviewing applications for a next creator cohort and accepts interest forms for later dates. It is not student-specific. It describes monthly credit grants, biweekly meetings, early access, awards/travel/speaking opportunities, and a requirement to share 2–3 pieces monthly. No country exclusion is published, but India is not explicitly guaranteed.
- Other organizations in this slice had no formal student route verified within this bounded official-source sweep. Rows retain official homepage/source anchors and explicitly state that unlocated programs may exist.

## Interpretation limits

- The cutoff is 2026-11-09, although this audit was carried out on 2026-11-10. Undated pages were viewed after the cutoff and are labelled as undated/currently observed rather than treated as dated proof of Nov 9 availability.
- “No formal route verified” is a bounded search result, not a conclusion that no program exists. Search can miss invite-only cohorts, non-English/local pages, private communities, social posts, or programs not indexed by the search source.
- India eligibility is reported only where an official page explicitly names India or gives a geography rule that includes/excludes it. General global language and lack of a restriction are not treated as an India guarantee.
- Several brands are product names, aliases, research projects, or potentially ambiguous entities (notably Sora/OpenAI Sora, Veo/Google Veo, Genie/Dreamer, GAIA, Odyssey, and the various China-based video products). The census labels were retained; precise corporate/product ownership or program operator identity was not independently resolved for every alias.
- Benefits, student/age/major rules, workload and dates are recorded only where the reviewed official pages expose them. Unpublished terms remain unresolved rather than inferred.
- Duplicate column distinguishes organization mention in the baseline census/research files from a confirmed duplicate opportunity. An organization appearing in a search/title-candidate artifact is not evidence that a formal route was already verified.
