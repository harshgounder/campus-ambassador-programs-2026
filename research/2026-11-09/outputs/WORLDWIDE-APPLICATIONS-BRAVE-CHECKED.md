# Worldwide ambassador applications checked in Brave

Working research as of 9 November 2026 for a student at Manipal University Jaipur, second-year B.Tech Electrical and Computer Engineering, age over 18. This list is partial and is not a claim of complete worldwide coverage. Form visibility is not the same as a successful submission or acceptance. No application was submitted.

## Student programs

- **[BMLSIA Ambassador Class of 2027](https://www.bmlsia.org/ambassadors):** Organizer says Round 1 is open to students in every field, anywhere in the world. Its form lists India. Work centers on health technology, clubs, and outreach. No deadline or pay established. Later form steps were not inspected.
- **[Penseum Student Ambassador](https://www.penseum.com/ambassador):** Organizer says applications are open to students everywhere, without experience required. Its linked form describes high-school and undergraduate applicants, and opens to questions about name, school, year, and location. Stipends are mentioned but terms are not established.
- **[SwytchCode Campus Ambassador](https://www.swytchcode.com/campus-ambassadors):** Organizer explicitly welcomes college students worldwide, including international students. Basic coding familiarity and about 5–10 hours a week expected. The linked form was opened in Brave and offered sophomore and beginner choices.
- **[Cheaper Inference Student Ambassador](https://api.cheaperinference.com/students/ambassadors):** Early applications open while the program is being developed. The form accepts a free-text country and asks for institution, degree, graduation, a PDF resume, and four short answers. India is not excluded in the displayed text, but country eligibility and benefits are not explicitly stated.

## Broader community programs students may apply to

- **[Baidu ERNIE Ambassadors](https://ernie-ambassadors.baidu.com/program.html):** Organizer says applications are rolling. The application includes a student-leader option, free-text country, institution, portfolio and community plan; India is not specifically excluded in the displayed material. Seeks technical ability and evidence of contribution. An honorarium is mentioned without a stated amount.
- **[Adaption Ambassadors](https://adaptionlabs.ai/blog/ambassador-program):** Organizer's April 2026 announcement says worldwide applications are reviewed weekly, with a six-month commitment. Its form loads and asks for role, organization and country. The organizer specifically discusses university-based applicants; the form's handling of a student entering an institution as organization has not been confirmed. Event grants are not a promised salary.
- **[Parallax Ambassador](https://networkparallax.com/ambassador-info):** Organizer's October 29 X post announced applications open, but the post and current website disagree on weekly versus monthly caps. The site requires five training modules before application access and a matching app-account email. The current cap, country eligibility, and final form have not been verified. Compensation includes token and conditional stablecoin rewards.

See `global-brave-verification.csv` for observations and limits. Other worldwide programs are being researched in the separate global developer, company, and community files; not all entries have been verified in Brave.

- **[Planetary Health Campus Ambassador 2027](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/MFBCXZM):** Applications close November 27, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. The form welcomes undergraduates from all disciplines and geographies, lists India, and requires enrollment throughout 2027 with graduation no earlier than December 2027. Your expected 2029 graduation fits that condition.
- **[Young Tinker Global Ambassador](https://youngtinker.org/ambassador/):** College applications close November 13, 2026. Applicants aged 13 or older start a club where one does not already exist. A free account is required; whether a Manipal Jaipur club exists and the full application are unverified.

- **[plud Campus Ambassadors](https://plud.app/ambassador):** Rolling applications are open to university students worldwide, any subject. About 3–4 hours weekly and one campus activity per month; first step requires a school email and recent photo. Referral rewards and product access are not guaranteed salary.

## Additional community programs

- **[ETC Student Ambassador](https://etcgrantsdao.io/en/ambassadorProgram):** The organizer says it is recruiting worldwide; its linked form has a student ambassador option. The role involves public support for Ethereum Classic, campus organizing and contribution reports. Rewards are activity-based ETC tokens after probation, not a stated salary. The form was opened but not submitted.
- **[Soneium Ambassador](https://soneium.org/en/blog/soneium-ambassador-program/):** An August announcement welcomes applications from any location, while prioritizing the US, Japan, Korea and China. The linked form opens. Community experience is relevant; age rules and current acceptance beyond the form welcome screen are unverified.
- **[Eazo Community Ambassador](https://eazo.ai/ambassadors):** The organizer invites applications and the linked form is visible; it asks about country, school or organization, Eazo app links and community plans. India is not ruled out in the material inspected, but the intake is not explicitly dated and detailed eligibility is unknown.
- **[ChainReaction Ambassador](https://chainreactionglobal.com/ambassadors):** The general application is visible and can be used when a campus isn't listed; it welcomes students and nonstudents. Several listed campus roles show 2025 deadlines, so current acceptance for the general route remains unconfirmed.

- **[HackerRank Campus Crew](https://www.hackerrank.com/campuscrew):** The organizer invites college students interested in coding and community to apply; its visible form lists India. It says the role is unpaid, takes roughly 4–6 hours weekly, and selects one ambassador per campus. Manipal Jaipur availability and final submission acceptance are unverified.

- **[FlutterFlow Student Ambassadors](https://www.flutterflow.io/student-ambassadors):** The official program page welcomes university students and lists ambassadors in India, but the linked application form says it is closed.

- **[DigiKey University Ambassador](https://www.eetech.com/press-releases/digi-key-university-ambassador):** EETech seeks enrolled EE/ECE students with campus involvement and describes paid activities; the linked application form loaded and requests a résumé and graduation date. India eligibility, current intake, and pay rates are not specified.
- **[Twilio Champions](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/champions):** The 2027 cohort is advertised as open through February 28, 2027. Its application lists India and submission implies marketing-email consent. It values developer or community contributions; the exact student eligibility requirements remain unconfirmed.

### Dewesoft student ambassador — preregistration lead

[Dewesoft’s official program page](https://dewesoft.com/academy/academia/student-ambassador) welcomes candidates from all countries but currently prioritizes Europe. Technical students need 2-star Dewesoft PRO training or equivalent knowledge; the page estimates 2–3 hours weekly and mentions hourly compensation without a rate. [Preregistration](https://dewesoft.com/academy/academia/student-ambassador/registration) is the first step; selection and current capacity for India remain unconfirmed.

### Correction: IncuXai intake uncertain

[IncuXai’s page](https://campusambassador.incuxai.com/) says registrations are open, but also gives a last application date of 30 July 2026 and a launch date of 15 August 2026. The accessible application form does not resolve this contradiction; treat the current intake as unconfirmed.

### IBM Z Student Ambassador, 2026–27 — India terms eligible; current country capacity unclear

The administering partner's [2026–27 terms](https://www.yourbigyear.com/ibm-z-terms-conditions) were updated September 23 and state rolling application periods through March 2027; India is listed among eligible residence countries. The [live Monday application form](https://forms.monday.com/forms/3725234c3e9df998545ebe06f6b2976c?r=use1) loaded in Brave, but warns that openings are currently available only in some countries. Its country menu includes India; a browser control timeout prevented checking whether choosing India currently reveals an available slot. Treat country capacity as unconfirmed. Requirements include current college/university enrollment, age 18+, intermediate English, IBM Z Xplore registration and, if accepted provisionally, a Concepts badge within two weeks. Terms say no participation fee and do not promise pay. The form expressly distinguishes IBM Z from IBM SkillsBuild. No fields were completed or submitted.
