# AI science verticals and non-LLM research communities: student-pathways delta

Research date: 2026-11-09. Scope: remaining AI-for-science and non-LLM technical communities, excluding deep prior lanes for biomedical/bioinformatics, protein and medical imaging, chemistry/materials, climate/weather/Earth/geospatial, astronomy/physics, agriculture/ecology, and computational neuroscience. Student profile used for fit: 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE, Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected 2029 graduation.

## Findings

One distinct current pathway was verified: ISCA Student Advisory Committee community volunteering/contribution. This is a general academic community pathway, not an ambassador program and not explicitly AI-specific. Official ISCA student-community guidance says new members are welcome at any time and explains volunteer/contributor roles. It does not explicitly establish undergraduate eligibility or name India; those details remain unconfirmed. No email or application was sent.

No distinct live/upcoming ambassador, campus chapter, representative, or fellowship call in the targeted CV/pattern recognition, speech/audio, reinforcement learning/control, planning/KR, fuzzy/evolutionary/soft computing, and related technical society searches met the evidence threshold in this bounded pass. Closed/dated or generic academic routes were not promoted as open ambassador programs. CVPR 2027 official pages were checked for current conference opportunities; its career posting page explicitly says it is not accepting postings. The CVPR research-paper CFP is an academic submission opportunity, but not a student pathway and requires original research, so it was not included as an individual program lead.

## Limits

This is a narrow follow-through, not a global census or proof no other pathways exist. Query coverage was English-language and focused on official society/conference pages; local-language communities, individual labs, GitHub projects and additional regional societies remain undersearched. The one pathway’s undergraduate and India eligibility are unresolved because official pages do not expressly state them. Do not imply application certainty or worldwide completeness.
