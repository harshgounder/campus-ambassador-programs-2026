# Chinese-language AI student and developer community lane

**Cutoff:** 2026-11-09. Research was performed on 2026-11-10. A live page fetched after the cutoff is not treated as evidence that a route was accepting applicants on Nov. 9 unless the page supplies dated status evidence.

## Validated fresh records

The companion CSV records three organizer-sourced paths that were not found in exact-name scans of the China/localized files and AI-ledger records reviewed:

- **MoonBit Regional Ambassador** is the strongest technical pathway. MoonBit's official post embedded in its official account announcement says the role is remote and location-independent, accepts students, and pays monthly compensation; the Juejin announcement is dated June 20, 2025 and describes recruitment as ongoing. Applicants must have real coding experience and at least one publicly verifiable MoonBit Package. Because no 2026 update or cutoff-date evidence was found, status stays **unverified at cutoff**. For the MUJ candidate, India is not named as excluded, but fit requires the published package prerequisite and Chinese-language participation. Do not infer current acceptance from the older “ongoing” wording.
- **1111 AI Campus Ambassador** in Taiwan appears to have a live 2026 program page and AI-related coursework/content tasks, but the official FAQ limits participation to students enrolled at Taiwanese tertiary institutions (including international students studying there). The MUJ candidate is not eligible under the published location rule. This is marketing-oriented and adjacent to technical AI community work.
- **SZCCF Student Chapter** has a dated 2026 Greater Bay Area membership and leadership call. It includes AI and embodied-AI programming and officer roles, but membership is geographically limited to Shenzhen and nearby institutions. It is not a route for an India-based student.

## Chinese and regional discovery

Used Exa web search/fetch plus Parallel search, with Simplified and Traditional Chinese and English queries. Search phrasing included `高校 AI 社区负责人 招募`, `AI开源社区 高校大使`, `校园大使 人工智能`, `地区大使 MoonBit`, `新加坡 AI 学生社区`, `香港 AI 学生社群`, `台湾 AI 校园大使`, and specialist terms for embodied AI, GPU/kernel, open source, and developer communities. Checked organizers/platforms and local community surfaces including SZCCF, Datawhale, OpenI, MoonBit, Singapore Youth AI, PolyU AISCO, 1111 Taiwan, SEMI Taiwan, and related community pages. Search snippets and non-organizer pages were discovery aids; candidate status and criteria rely on the organizer-controlled page when available.

Exact-name duplicate checks covered existing China/local-language files, prior AI community/ecosystem passes, curated AI organization audit outputs, and the central AI ledger. Existing OpenAI Student Collective, MiniCPM/OpenBMB, Qwen, ModelScope, Huawei, Tencent, MindSpore Evangelist/MSG, Meitu, Triton China, DeepSeek and other previously listed records were not duplicated. OpenAI's Chinese-language Student Collective page appeared in results but is already represented and its India 2026 intake closed before cutoff.

## Search gaps and screened results

- Singapore Youth AI (SYAI) presents a youth AI community and youth-led outreach/research programs, but the official homepage does not publish an open student ambassador or community-lead application. PolyU's AISCO is a student AI community, but it is specific to PolyU students; its 2026 AI-agent program was closed for registration on Oct. 30 and is not an ambassador role.
- OpenI's university “启梦之旅” campus-leader recruitment page is dated 2022 and historical. No current intake was verified.
- SEMI Taiwan's 2026 Campus Ambassador intake is completed; its official page lists selected students and recruitment May 4–31, 2026. It is a semiconductor-industry exposure role rather than an AI community program.
- Datawhale surfaced as a local employer's paid “campus community specialist” job in Tianjin, not a general student ambassador intake; it was excluded from actionable student opportunities.
- WeChat official-account history, private groups, QR forms, Baidu Tieba/Gitee/Nowcoder dynamic search, and account-gated posts are incompletely indexed. The bounded web searches do not establish that no other local opportunities exist. No accounts were created, groups joined, QR codes scanned, applications submitted, or organizers contacted. X MCP was not used.
