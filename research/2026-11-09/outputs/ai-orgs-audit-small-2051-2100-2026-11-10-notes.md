For each entity individually, issued one Parallel/Exa web search call with three query variations covering official identity, student/community route, and fellowship/campus/chapter route.

Search results did not establish an official opportunity tied to the exact census entity in this batch. Generic program roundups and unrelated namesakes were excluded. This is a bounded name-by-name screen, not a claim that no opportunity exists globally. No provider failures or rate-limit warnings were returned.

Cutoff is separately assessed as 2026-11-09: none of the retrieved evidence established a qualifying exact-entity route open on that date, so status is unverified. Nov 10 search observations were used for discovery only. India eligibility is unknown/not stated and was not inferred. Existing duplicate checks compared exact names against prior small audit slices and `ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv`.

Identity anchors use the census-listed GitHub handle URL and validate only the namespace/profile, not legal identity or program ownership. Search coverage did not include exhaustive official-domain/manual/archive or multilingual review. No forms, contacts, applications, joins, X MCP, or central ledger changes were made.
