# CV, OCR, speech/audio and multimodal opportunity screen — cutoff 9 Nov 2026

## Scope and method

Targeted the requested non-LLM lanes: computer vision and pattern recognition (IEEE/CVF, IAPR, OpenCV), OCR/document AI (ICDAR and OCR project routes), speech/audio/music (ISCA, IEEE SPS, Common Voice), multimodal perception, and Indic-language data/community projects. The purpose was to find an individual student ambassador, chapter/campus lead, representative, developer champion, volunteer, or community-organizer route. I used official organizer/program pages for status and eligibility; conference directories and repositories are contextual discovery only. No X MCP was used; no form was submitted or user information entered; no organizer was contacted.

The cutoff is 9 Nov 2026. Pages crawled or checked on 10 Nov are only evidence of the current page state and do not prove that an undated route was open on the cutoff. All such timing uncertainty is explicit in the CSV.

## Screen result

- **One newly verified formal community-organizing invitation:** OpenCV’s official support page says a new volunteer program is starting and invites volunteers to organize OpenCV meetups and school groups via an application form. No enrollment, age, major, geography, workload-hour, deadline, term, or compensation criteria are published. The invitation is active-appearing on the page as retrieved, but the page has no dated announcement; Nov 9 status is uncertain. India eligibility is not explicitly stated.
- **One closed/previous-cycle student support route:** CVPR 2026 Broadening Participation offered student travel/registration support with an application link and rolling decisions. CVPR 2026 is past by the cutoff; the extracted page has a missing deadline field, and no 2027 equivalent was verified. This is support for conference participation, not an ambassador or student representative role.

## Existing routes checked and deduplicated

The 9 Nov `ai-vision-speech-community-delta` already captures Mozilla Common Voice contributions, AI4Bharat IndicNLP resource submissions, PaddleOCR contributions, OpenCV forum/repository contribution, IEEE CS TCPAMI, Manga OCR, DCASE 2027 task-organizer proposals, IEEE ASLI challenge proposals, and PHOCR. The 9 Nov AI priority ledger already captures ISCA-SAC year-round volunteer participation and the open 2027 IEEE SPS Video and Image Processing Cup proposal call. These were not duplicated as new candidates. OpenCV’s official volunteer program is distinct from previously recorded code/forum participation.

CVPR/CVF official pages show CVPR 2027 dates and conference content, while the reviewed Broadening Participation page is explicitly for 2026. I did not find an official CVPR 2027 student-volunteer or broadening-participation intake in the targeted search. Absence in these searches is not proof that none will be announced later.

The IAPR/ICDAR search results surfaced historical student travel/volunteer examples (e.g. ICDAR 2017), not a current 2026/2027 role; these are not included as active opportunities. Student chapters, general societies and OpenCV communities were not converted into roles without a current organizer invitation.

## Limitations

This is a compact opportunity audit, not an exhaustive inventory of CVPR/CVF/IAPR local chapters, every conference volunteer call, or every language corpus. Official announcement archives and localized sources were not exhausted. Search engine results for IAPR and ICDAR produced stale historical material, which was excluded. Current OpenCV page state cannot date the invitation to the 9 Nov cutoff, and official silence about India does not establish eligibility. No application-form contents were inspected.
