# AI Latin America Spanish/Portuguese priority pass — 2026-11-09

## Scope and search method

- Fresh web searches: 12 multilingual queries across three batches. Queries covered Spanish phrases for AI ambassadors, student/campus ambassadors, chapters and communities in Latin America; Portuguese terms for AI ambassador/student programs in Brazil; English queries for Latin America chapter leads and AI ambassadors.
- Search sources were discovery only. Candidate details were checked on organizer-controlled pages. Reviewed 8 unique program pages across Pioneras en IA (1), CLET (1), Comunidad IA LATAM (2), CreateLatam (3), and the Google Gemini Brazil alumni portal (1). The portal itself explicitly says it is not an official Google channel, so it was not entered as an organizer-verified Google program.
- Four candidates are recorded in the CSV: two live or standing AI community ambassador/chapter routes with explicit constraints, one AI-focused NGO volunteer leadership route with eligibility not stated, and one AI education nonprofit’s broader volunteer roles labeled adjacent.
- Census/profile used for fit: user is 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur, India, graduating 2029. No nationality or gender assumption beyond the profile facts supplied to the task.

## Search coverage and exclusions

- Searches found Pioneras en IA (open, but limited to women 18+ residing in Latin America), CLET regional chapter proposals (open, with a local network proposal requirement and no stated international eligibility rule), Comunidad IA LATAM country ambassadors (open seats but born-and-resident-in-country eligibility), and CreateLatam volunteer/leadership roles (active call, but applicant geography/gender rules are not stated on the role page).
- Google Gemini Brazil Student Ambassadors 2026 appeared in a Portuguese organizer portal, but the page says the portal is restricted to current participants and “not an official Google channel”; the landing page provided no application criteria/status. Excluded as an unverified Google-organized opportunity. FAM’s AI Ambassador program was found only as an older 2025 call and was excluded as stale/closed without a current intake.
- Other exclusions: Google Gemini Mexico search results were third-party and no organizer-controlled active intake was located; Claude Campus is already covered elsewhere and was not duplicated; NVIDIA University Ambassador in the Dominican Republic surfaced as a faculty/teacher program, not an undergraduate student role; CABA Docentes Embajadores en IA is restricted to teachers/future teachers in Buenos Aires and its current enrollment is closed.
- Existing output files were checked for exact candidate-name overlap. Claude Campus is a known duplicate and deliberately omitted. No material status correction to existing AI census rows was made in this pass.

## Limits

- This is a focused discovery pass, not an exhaustive census. Search engine indexing may miss local university pages, social-only notices, forms that require login, or programs marketed only through WhatsApp/Instagram.
- Dynamic organizer pages can have inconsistent totals: Comunidad IA LATAM’s current pages disagree on active representatives and open-seat counts. The CSV records the explicit role eligibility/commitments and flags the count inconsistency.
- “No restriction stated” is not treated as worldwide eligibility. CLET and CreateLatam remain uncertain for a MUJ student; the user’s specific eligibility was not tested by application or organizer contact.
- No forms were submitted, no contact was made, and no central AI ledger was edited.
