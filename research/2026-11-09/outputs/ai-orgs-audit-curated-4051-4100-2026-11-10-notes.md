# AI organization census audit — source rows 4051–4100

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row ID, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Screened exact names and source-ledger links for student/campus ambassadors, advocates, champions, fellows, chapters, representatives and community roles. Checked likely official pages. This is a bounded screening pass, not exhaustive platform, language or country research for every name.
- Counted a route only when evidence tied it to the exact census entity. Kept fellowships, student internships, ordinary project contributions, events and general community access separate from ambassador roles.
- No X MCP, applications, forms, messages, outreach or personal-data entry occurred.
