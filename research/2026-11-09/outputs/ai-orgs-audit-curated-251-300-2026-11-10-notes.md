## Method and source limits

- Each of the 50 organization names was submitted as an individual official-route search objective, emphasizing organizer-controlled sources and student/campus ambassadors, developer communities, open-source routes, education programs, competitions and volunteer pathways. A first large parallel search batch encountered Exa request-rate limits on part of the batch, so the remaining positive candidates were rechecked in smaller batches. This limits the strength of negative findings.
- Positive claims in the CSV link directly to organizer pages or an official company LinkedIn announcement. General official domain links only support identity/context, not absence of programs.
- Where official pages were undated or showed no dated intake, the CSV says that Nov 9 status is not confirmed. Publication date or a live page alone is not treated as proof that applications were open on cutoff.
- India is called eligible only where explicitly supported. “Not stated / unconfirmed” is used otherwise. A localized page or India-targeted marketing does not establish eligibility for an associated program.
- No inference is made from silence. There is no application, organizer contact, form submission, or use of X MCP in this work.
- Existing output CSVs were available, but this pass did not make changes to central ledgers. The row-level duplicate field is conservative and records no exact route duplicate confirmation unless the relationship is clear from the adjacent route finding. It is not a complete exhaustive cross-file uniqueness guarantee.
