# AI maker and product resume pass — 2026-11-09

## Scope and search coverage

This was a targeted global discovery pass across AI model makers, coding/developer tools, creative and research applications, AI infrastructure, and general AI student-community recruitment. It used **8 Parallel web-search requests containing 23 keyword queries** across distinct search objectives. Search-result excerpts were screened against program names and organizer URLs in the existing AI ledgers and source outputs before selecting records.

**Eight organizer-owned source pages were reviewed**: Memori's student ambassador page and linked HubSpot form, CLIF AI's 2026–27 student ambassador page, MemMachine's student ambassador page, Superform AI's careers role, Creatify's ambassador page, and Humble AI's join-us page and student-societies page. Current page content was extracted with live fetching. The HubSpot form was viewed read-only: only its first screen was inspected; no fields were completed and no personal data or submissions were made.

The searches surfaced many existing records, which were excluded from the new-lead set: 2077AI, InfraOne, KriyagniAI, CLIF search noise that was actually the CLIF lead, Superform AI, TheNoah.ai, Edad AI, TryHackMe, Trinka, Claude Campus, SwytchCode, Muneo, Creatify, and MemMachine. Program/community pages were treated as leads, not proof of recruitment, until checked against organizer-owned pages. This pass did not contact anyone or submit any application.

It is relevant to an ECE student if interested in agent memory/community building, but geography remains unconfirmed.
- **CLIF AI — new distinct 2026–27 lead.** The organizer page says Apply Today and describes a nationwide student ambassador network; the Apply CTA destination was not exposed in the page extraction, so intake is open-appearing and the application route remains partially unresolved. The page does not name the country or give citizenship, degree, year, or age rules. INR rewards strongly suggest an India-based national program, but that reading remains an inference; India acceptance was not treated as proven. A second-year student likely matches the student/campus framing if the inferred India geography is correct. Work is substantial: a 12-month term, minimum two social posts monthly, a 50–100 registration target per semester, 1–2 campus events per semester, and monthly reports. Listed returns include ₹20 per valid registration, tiered bonuses, ₹1,000 event support, and top-performer internship/cash awards up to ₹10,000, plus tools, mentoring, and recognition. Consider whether the referral target is acceptable before prioritizing.
- **Humble AI — new conditional lead.** The organizer describes an AI product for neurodiversity/ADHD and actively invites campus ambassadors. The route is a named application email and a book-a-call path. It is an unpaid, flexible volunteer role with no published hours, term, age floor, or country list. Duties focus on running short workshops and connecting university societies, especially neurodiversity, mental-health, or wellbeing groups. Benefits include product access, founder recognition, community participation, swag, and practical AI/startup experience. It may fit if the user has genuine interest and can connect a relevant student society; the fit cannot be assumed from ECE enrollment alone.
