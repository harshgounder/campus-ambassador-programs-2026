# AI organization census audit — source rows 3001–3050

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows, preserving `name`, `tier`, and `category` verbatim and in order.
- Screened exact entity names for student/campus ambassador, community leader, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative and developer community routes. Official organization/project sources were prioritized.
- The source ledger's search URLs included broad campus directories and unrelated companies/programs. These were excluded unless a direct ownership link to the census entity was established.
- Considered non-LLM fields represented in the slice: computer vision (JDAI-CV, foolwood), speech (TensorSpeech), ML compiler/runtime (IREE), Go/data science/ML (GopherData), world models/robotics (danijar), 3D vision/NeRF (MrNeRF), embedded OS (tutti-os), and general AI health/privacy projects. These topic communities do not by themselves establish ambassador programs.
- No forms were submitted, accounts created, personal data entered, or organizations contacted. No X MCP was used.

## Findings and status distinctions

- One exact-entity lead was located for Anakin-Inc: [Anakin Campus Ambassador Program](https://anakin.io/ambassadors). The page observed on 2026-11-10 states applications are open, applications are reviewed weekly, and the role involves campus workshops, Discord/WhatsApp community leadership, weekly content, and open-source contributions. Listed benefits are API credits, early product access, direct team access, and merchandise. The page gives no fixed deadline or cash compensation in the observed text. This is an **open-appearing post-cutoff observation**, not proof it was open on Nov 9. The page is undated, so cutoff status remains unverified; applicant eligibility and detailed workload remain gaps.
- No exact-entity route was established as cutoff-open, rolling, closed, future, or restricted for the other 49 rows. This means no qualifying status was verified; it is not proof that no role exists.
- Adjacent participation routes are not counted as student leader roles: IREE, TensorSpeech, GopherData, Mesh-LLM, ClaraVerse, PipesHub, and dromara have project/community/contribution surfaces; Google Antigravity references Google Developer Groups events. A generic project community or contributor path is not an ambassador intake.
- Several names resolve only to individual GitHub handles, not a demonstrable formal organization. For those rows, the exact handle is preserved, the GitHub locator is supplied as an identity pointer, and program ownership remains unresolved. Knetic specifically has a same-name UK retail/fulfilment locator; its connection to an AI org is unverified.

## Search bounds and date handling

- Bounded exact-name web searches were run for the most organization-like and subfield-relevant entries (including JDAI-CV, IREE, TensorSpeech, OpenHealthForAll, Mesh-LLM, GopherData, ClaraVerse, Anakin, Premier Octet, and selected individual handles) with combinations of student, campus, ambassador, community, and developer terms. Result lists sometimes surfaced generic directory pages or similarly named unrelated entities; these were excluded.
- Official source locators from the input ledger were used as additional identity pointers, not as evidence of an ambassador route where they were unrelated or third-party. Several profiles and pages are undated. Nov 10 current-page observations are kept distinct from cutoff evidence; no undated observation is projected backward to Nov 9.
- Coverage is bounded: not every handle's entire web/social history or all Chinese/local-language channels was searched. China-connected JDAI-CV and Hangzhou-based Janspiry were also searched with Chinese terms (校园大使, 学生, 社区, 招募). A dated third-party repost surfaced a 2027 JD.com general campus ambassador intake (Jun 26–Jul 24, 2026), but that is JD corporate recruitment and not attributed to JDAI-CV/京东 AI 研究院, so it was excluded. Searches for Janspiry surfaced no verifiable exact-entity student program. The Chinese-language sweep was limited to those names and search-engine-indexed pages; it is not comprehensive. The agent-reach CLI was unavailable in this environment, so searches used the connected read-only web search service. No X MCP was used.

## Validation

The CSV has 13 columns and 50 records. IDs 3001–3050 are consecutive and ordered; each name, tier, and category matches its source row exactly. No row treats absence of a search result as proof of program absence. Evidence URLs and unresolved eligibility, deadline, workload, compensation, and cutoff questions are captured row by row.
