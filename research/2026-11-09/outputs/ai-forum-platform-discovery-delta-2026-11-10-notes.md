# AI forum and community discovery pass — 2026-11-10

## Scope and method

- Focus: independent AI/community leadership routes that may be missed by company ambassador lists: GitHub/GitLab/Gitee, Hacker News, Reddit, Discourse and official community forums, CSDN, Juejin, Bilibili, Zhihu, Hugging Face discussions, Chinese-language community sources, and specialized AI groups.
- Ran **47 keyword query variations** across **10 platform/source families**: GitHub, Hacker News, Reddit, Discourse/n8n, Hugging Face, CSDN, Juejin, Gitee, Bilibili, Zhihu/WeChat, plus cross-domain searches for AI safety, vision, robotics, audio, AI for science, edge AI, RL, and climate/geospatial AI. Search was discovery only; search snippets/social reposts were not taken as official proof.
- Followed distinct candidates to official organizer pages and compared names to `outputs/ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv` and related AI education, platform-community, China-ecosystem, non-LLM, and vision/robotics/edge outputs. Did not edit the central ledger.
- X MCP and browser were not used. No one was contacted, no group joined, and no account/form was created or submitted.
