## Coverage and method

- Used the canonical source /home/liebert511/campus-ambassador-hunt/data/ai-orgs-census.csv. Copied the 50 consecutive records at data-row IDs 4101–4150, including exact census name, tier and category, into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Screened each exact census name with an ambassador search, then used official GitHub account/organization profiles as identity locators and reviewed relevant first-party pages returned for possible exact-entity leads.
- Screened for student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative, creator and community-lead routes. A result was counted only when ownership tied to the exact census entity.
- Excluded unrelated same-name college ambassador programs, generic directories, general memberships, events, ordinary open-source contributions, education resources, research opportunities, jobs, internships and recruiting.
- No application forms were submitted, no account was created, no contact or outreach was made, and no personal data was entered. No X MCP or browser automation was used.
