# Small-tier AI organization census audit: ranks 51–100

Scope: filtered small-tier ranks 51–100 from `outputs/ai-orgs-small-tier-search-ledger-2026-11-10.csv` (50 records). The audit preserves both source `census_row` and filtered `small_rank`. Results are saved in `outputs/ai-orgs-audit-small-0051-0100-2026-11-10.csv`.

## Method and cutoff

- Checked exact handle/name batches for official entity/profile identity, student/campus routes, developer community programs, ambassadors/champions/advocates/fellows/chapters/creator pathways, and possible name collisions. Used five grouped web searches spanning all 50 handles plus four focused follow-ups for SKT, IFM, InstaDeep, OpenVINO, KU-NLP and KLUE. The batches were completed using Exa web search; no X MCP was used.
- Hugging Face profile URLs are recorded as the canonical exact-handle source where no stronger official identity source was independently verified. A matching profile proves the census handle exists, but it does not by itself prove whether the owner is an organization, company, lab, or individual. Same-name hits were not treated as identity matches without a handle/domain link.
- Cross-checked exact names against the other CSV outputs under `outputs/` for existing-ledger duplication. No exact-name hits were found outside the source census for these 50 entries. A mention would not itself count as a program duplicate; no exact verified program duplicate surfaced in the cross-ledger scan.
- `cutoff_status` is explicitly anchored to 2026-11-09. Pages and identity observations made on Nov 10 are identified as later discovery observations and are not backdated into the cutoff. No India eligibility is inferred from silence, country, language, or organization location.
- Search results were discovery evidence, not proof of absence. Rows without a verified route are bounded-pass non-hits; official pages and program evidence should be revisited when a deeper exact-entity review is warranted.

## Material leads

- **SKT / SK Telecom:** official materials document the DEVOCEAN YOUNG student tech-influencer community recruitment in 2022, the AI Fellowship program and alumni network (reported in 2023), and an AI Fellowship program page. This supports an organization-level student pathway lead, but the material reviewed does not establish an application window open at the Nov 9 cutoff or India eligibility. Sources: [DEVOCEAN YOUNG recruitment announcement](https://news.sktelecom.com/179204), [AI Fellowship page](https://thub.sk.com/community/aiFellowshipAbout.do), [2023 Fellowship announcement](https://news.sktelecom.com/199818).
- **IFM:** official IFM collaboration/careers pages surfaced, along with K2 Horizon Ascent Challenge terms limiting participation to MBZUAI-affiliated participants. This is not a general student ambassador intake and no external student recruitment window was verified. Sources: [IFM Collaborate](https://ifm.ai/collaborate/), [K2 challenge rules](https://k2-ascent.ifm.ai/rules).
- **OpenVINO:** official project sources show participation in Google Summer of Code 2026 and a maintained contributor guide. This is a mentored open-source program rather than an ambassador role; the 2026 application period had ended before the Nov 9 cutoff. Cross-border eligibility was not established by the reviewed project sources. Sources: [GSoC 2026 organization page](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2026/organizations/openvino-toolkit), [OpenVINO contributor wiki](https://github.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/wiki/Google-Summer-Of-Code).
- **Kenpath:** exact Hugging Face profile links to Kenpath Labs; its official site describes Indian-language open-source AI work. No student or ambassador intake was found in the reviewed materials. Sources: [Hugging Face profile](https://huggingface.co/kenpath), [Kenpath open-source page](https://www.kenpath.io/en/open-source).
- **Bad Theory Labs / ByteDance Research / InstaDeep:** exact Hugging Face handles linked to official entity surfaces. No student leadership intake was substantiated in this bounded search. Sources: [Bad Theory Labs](https://badtheorylabs.com/), [ByteDance Open Source](https://opensource.bytedance.com/), [InstaDeep](https://www.instadeep.com/).
- **Name collision control:** the Nunchaku search surfaced different Nunchaku/Nunchux projects; no link to the census handle `nunchaku-ai` was verified, so those external pages were excluded from its identity or route assessment.

## Validation and limitations

The output contains 50 rows, the requested 14 columns, and unique small ranks 51–100. `census_row`, `small_rank`, `name`, `tier`, and `category` were validated row-for-row against the source ledger. Every evidence/status field is populated. The profile/identity audit remains shallow for most low-salience handles; individual profiles may represent people or model-upload accounts rather than organizations. One focused InstaDeep search returned a web-search authentication failure, so that entry remains a bounded non-hit rather than a verified absence. No people were contacted and no forms were opened or submitted.
