# China / East Asia AI community discovery delta — 2026-11-09

## Scope and result

Additional Chinese-language and niche East Asia searches focused on official campus/student organization pathways outside the previously reviewed ModelScope, NICE, Lumina, PaddlePaddle, Huawei Kunpeng, NVIDIA DLI, and X Square Robot Bright Campus Ambassador records. Topics included computer vision, speech/audio, robotics and embodied/physical AI, Taiwan AI education volunteering, and student-run technical communities in Japan and Korea.

Two distinct additions are recorded in `china-east-asia-ai-community-delta-2026-11-09.csv`:

1. KUPAC (Kyoto University Physical AI Community) explicitly welcomes anyone interested in Physical AI and says it is recruiting operations members for study-group planning and publicity. This is the strongest new student-organizer lead. Its official Japanese page does not say whether non-Japan-based members may take operations roles or whether English is supported.
2. X Square Robot's official Bright Plan 2.0 page describes active developer participation, campus talks, open simulation/project practice and a November 2026 hackathon. It is a useful embodied-AI community route but is not a campus ambassador recruitment. It is listed separately from the previously recorded unresolved “具亮校园大使” role. The hackathon is onsite, and India eligibility/travel feasibility are not stated.

No match was found. The separate X Square Robot “具亮校园大使” listing already exists in central and first-wave ledgers, so it was not duplicated or reclassified as open.

## Closed, restricted and non-qualifying findings

- ChinaVis 2026's official volunteer page said applications closed 2026-06-28 and service was 2026-07-19 to 07-22; it allowed domestic and overseas registered university students, subject to CSIG student membership. Stale/closed as of cutoff.
- Harbin Engineering University's official PRCV 2026 volunteer notice restricted applicants to full-time students at that university and had an 2026-08-09 deadline. Institution-restricted and closed.
- Kyungpook National University's official AI COSS supporters call was restricted to students in participating departments, with March 2026 application close. Closed and institution/program restricted.
- Taiwan AIAA describes a volunteer-instructor program and welcomes teacher/high-school participants, but its fetched page timed out; its indexed page excerpt did not establish current intake or a university-student organizer role. Not promoted.
- CAAI's official notices page showed active 2026 events and notices but no surfaced campus ambassador/volunteer call in the results reviewed. This is a no-lead result for this search pass, not proof no such role exists.
- ChinaLLM 2026 and other event promotion ambassador results were dated to July 2026 and had ended by the cutoff; excluded as stale event promotions.

## Limitations and gaps

Agent Reach was required for platform research but its health check failed because `agent-reach` is not installed (`command not found`). Searches therefore used the available Exa search and fetch connector. No direct Bilibili, Zhihu, Gitee, WeChat-public-account, or Feishu UI session review was conducted in this delta; indexed search visibility for these sources is incomplete. The 14 search objectives are not 14 distinct programs, and search output is not a census. Third-party job or event posts were used only as discovery and were not used to assert current eligibility. No person was contacted, no account or community was joined, and no form was opened/submitted.
