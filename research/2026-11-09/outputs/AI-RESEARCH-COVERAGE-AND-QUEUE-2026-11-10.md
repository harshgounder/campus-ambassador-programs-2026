## Working AI field map

Treat each branch as a search lane. Do not collapse AI into LLMs or wrapper companies.

1. **Foundations and learning paradigms:** mathematical/statistical foundations, optimization, supervised/unsupervised/self-supervised learning, representation and metric learning, transfer/domain adaptation, continual/federated/distributed learning, causal and probabilistic ML, reinforcement learning, imitation/preference learning, planning and decision systems, neuro-symbolic/hybrid AI.
2. **Model families and architectures:** language and foundation models; transformers and state-space models; diffusion/flow matching, GANs and other generative methods; graph neural networks; energy-based models; world models, JEPA and predictive learning; multi-agent systems; small/efficient models.
3. **Modalities and perception:** NLP and multilingual/low-resource language technology; speech recognition, synthesis, diarization, audio/music; computer vision, image/video generation, video understanding, 3D/spatial perception, document/OCR/layout, multimodal and sensor fusion.
4. **Robotics and physical AI:** manipulation, locomotion, autonomous driving, drones, embodied/interactive agents, SLAM/control, simulation/digital twins, industrial/medical/service robotics, edge robotics.
5. **Scientific and vertical AI:** biology/medicine/health, genomics, drug discovery, chemistry/materials, climate/weather, energy, agriculture, geospatial/remote sensing, transport, education, finance/quant, public sector and accessibility.
6. **Data and knowledge:** datasets, annotation/labeling, data-centric AI, synthetic data, curation, knowledge graphs, retrieval/search, vector databases, data quality and provenance.
7. **AI systems and infrastructure:** chips/accelerators, GPU programming, HPC, model training, distributed training, compilers/kernels, inference and serving, quantization/compression, edge/on-device, model gateways, MLOps, evaluation/observability, AI cloud, compute access, networking and cost/energy efficiency.
8. **Evaluation, safety, security and governance:** benchmarks, measurement and interpretability, robustness/reliability, privacy, fairness/accessibility, AI safety/alignment, governance/policy/standards, red teaming, model/data supply-chain security and AI-enabled cyber defense/offense.
9. **Learning and community ecosystems:** open-source foundations and project communities; campus clubs, ambassadors and chapters; hackathons/competitions; research programs; creator/teaching roles; standards groups; niche forums and regional/local-language communities, especially Chinese and Asia-Pacific.

Search for the work even when the organizer avoids the word `ambassador`: student/campus lead, community champion, developer advocate, ecosystem partner, chapter lead, student fellow, evangelist, contributor/maintainer, educator, creator, youth representative, meetup organizer, user-group leader, campus promoter, research/community fellow. Keep competitions, jobs, internships, grants, and one-off content contests distinct from an ongoing ambassador role.
