## Search method and limits

Ran one exact-name Exa web search for each of 50 labels, then a focused follow-up query across 10 potentially promising exact entities. The broad search returned exact GitHub profiles/repositories for many rows and some unrelated or generic-name noise. Follow-up official pages were fetched for KiviDB community/careers, Caspian Devpost, Motphys contact, and Flow-Like support; returned content was reviewed. Search results and undated live pages do not establish the historical state on Nov 9. The cutoff field therefore separates dated evidence from Nov 10 discovery.

This is a bounded screen, not an exhaustive official-domain crawl, archive review or multilingual/local community review for each handle. Search can miss differently named parent organizations, aliases, unindexed or private channels, local-language routes, and forms with no searchable text. A no-hit is not proof no opportunity exists. Most rows are LOW confidence because exact identity/program ownership and role-specific terms were not established.
