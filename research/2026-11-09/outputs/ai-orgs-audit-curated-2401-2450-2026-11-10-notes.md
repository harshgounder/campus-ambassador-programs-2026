## Coverage and method

- Screened 50 consecutive exact census names/handles for student/campus ambassador, chapter, champion, advocate, volunteer, representative, and community routes across AI/ML and adjacent fields. Preserved source row, exact name, tier, category, and order.
- Used ledger URLs as discovery locators; unrelated brands, search-result programs, jobs, internships, general communities, and creator/commercial partnerships were not counted as student programs.
- Evidence reviewed on 2026-11-10 is explicitly distinguished from cutoff proof. A bounded non-hit does not prove no route exists. Eligibility unknowns are not treated as exclusions.
- No forms, personal data, accounts, outreach, X MCP, or edits to the campus-ambassador-hunt repository were used.
