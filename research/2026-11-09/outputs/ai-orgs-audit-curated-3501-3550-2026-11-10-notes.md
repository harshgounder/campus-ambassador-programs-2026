# AI organization census audit: rows 3501–3550

Research date: 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09.

## Source alignment

Read `outputs/census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv` directly with the header excluded. The output contains exactly 50 records. A programmatic comparison passed for each record’s row number, exact name, tier, and category (3501–3550, inclusive).

## Screening scope and method

Each exact census handle received one Exa web screening query for its official student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, or organized community-leadership program. Results were screened for identity match and owner-controlled pages. Results commonly returned GitHub Campus Experts regardless of queried handle; these generic matches were excluded unless they actually belonged to the exact identity. The input ledger’s third-party campus-program links were treated as discovery leads only, not evidence of a route. Follow-up owner-page searches/fetches were done for Recall, Supabase, OpenMind, eProsima, and MarkTechPost. GitHub and official project pages were used to check identity where surfaced.

This is bounded screening, not exhaustive research: it does not establish absence of a program. I did not search X, submit applications, enter forms, message anyone, or verify status through contact.

## Verified exact-identity findings

- **openrecall / Recall Academic Ambassador Program:** Recall’s official program page describes a real academic ambassador route, including eligibility for enrolled undergraduates through PhD or academic employees, activities, flexible 5–10 hours/month workload, a 3-month minimum, free Premium/early access, and referral commissions. Its official FAQ says international academics may apply if legally able to receive payments in their country. India is not named, so local payment eligibility remains unconfirmed. The program page is undated; it was visible on Nov. 10, 2026 and says applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, but the exact state on the requested Nov. 9 cutoff is not independently established. [Program page](https://docs.recall.it/recall-academic-ambassador-program), [FAQ](https://docs.recall.it/recall-academic-ambassador-program/faq).
- **supabase-community / SupaSquad:** Supabase’s official page describes an advocate program with Contributor, Content Creator, Trusted Host, and Event Speaker tracks. It involves community support, content, meetups, speaking, and benefits including recognition, early access, team access, partner deals, swag, and a potential path to paid contribution/employment. It is not student-specific. Supabase’s official February 2026 developer update said the program was open and linked an application, which supports a pre-cutoff open announcement; no close date was found. The live page was visible Nov. 10. No age, student, country, workload, or stipend terms were provided on the official program page. [Official program](https://supabase.com/open-source/contributing/supasquad), [Feb. 2026 update](https://supabase.com/changelog/42531-developer-update-february-2026).
- **MARKTECHPOST-AI-MEDIA-INC:** The company’s official Student Contributors page invites college students and recent graduates to submit articles. This is editorial contribution, not an ambassador role. Its page does not state compensation, deadline, geography, or cutoff availability. [Student Contributors](https://www.marktechpost.com/student-contributors/), [About](https://www.marktechpost.com/about-us/).
- **OpenMind:** Official materials surfaced the OpenMind Developer League and OM1 bounty/contribution activity, not an ambassador appointment. A student/researcher access page says its application form is “coming soon”; access is not ambassador leadership. [Developer League](https://openmindagi.substack.com/p/introducing-the-openmind-developer), [OM1 bounty program](https://github.com/OpenMind/OM1/wiki/Bounty-Program), [student/researcher access](https://docs.openmind.com/full-autonomy-guidelines/premium_features).
