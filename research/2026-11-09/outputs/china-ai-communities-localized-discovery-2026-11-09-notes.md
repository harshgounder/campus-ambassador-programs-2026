# Chinese AI communities and developer groups — additional discovery lane (2026-11-09)

## Result

Saved seven records in `china-ai-communities-localized-discovery-2026-11-09.csv`. Two are practical current routes to investigate: ModelScope Co-Builders and NICE volunteers. A named X Square Robot role has an official recruitment listing, but its page would not render enough detail to establish that the application remains open or whether India-based students qualify. Lumina is an open community/co-building route rather than a formal ambassador job. The other records are retained as institution-restricted, teacher-only, or stale historical evidence.

No overseas/India eligibility is inferred from silence. The strongest explicit cross-region signal is NICE's separate overseas volunteer workstream and Lumina's statement that it connects students/researchers from overseas institutions; neither publishes an India-specific guarantee. ModelScope says activity is unrestricted by city or campus, but does not explicitly define international eligibility.

The objectives were distinct search intents, not 30 unique programs or proof of broad source coverage.
- Directly fetched seven official source pages: ModelScope Co-Builders, X Square Robot's Feishu campus-recruitment role, Lumina community, NICE, PaddlePaddle Campus Navigator, Huawei Cloud HCSD, and NVIDIA DLI University Ambassador. Nanjing Agricultural University's AI Co-Creation Space source was reviewed from its official indexed page excerpt.
- Huawei HCSD/Kunpeng was deduplicated against `ai-east-southeast-maker-community-delta-2026-11-09.csv`; Huawei's Kunpeng route is already recorded there with partner-institution eligibility unknown. Meyo, Baidu ERNIE/PaddlePaddle-adjacent student routes, Qwen, MiniCPM, and other major branded programs surfaced in discovery, but were not repeated where already represented in prior outputs. PaddlePaddle Navigator is retained only as a separate stale historical community program, with current status explicitly unresolved.
- NVIDIA DLI was kept only as teacher-only/restricted evidence; it is not a student opportunity. Search findings for past university cohorts and one-time recruitment posts were not promoted as currently open.
- No X MCP was used. No form was submitted, account created, community joined, or person contacted. Email addresses and WeChat IDs appearing on official pages were not used.

## Findings and limitations

- ModelScope's official page is unusually clear that recruitment is underway, describes a three-minute application and contact within ten working days, and invites event, tutorial and topic co-building across locations and campuses. It does not publish explicit country eligibility or an exposed form URL in the fetched text.
- NICE explicitly seeks volunteers, prefers students, distinguishes domestic and overseas communications work, and gives email application instructions. It does not publish citizenship/residency rules, hours or a deadline. It is unpaid volunteer work by description, with no cash commitment stated.
- X Square Robot's official Feishu listing exists under the exact title “具亮校园大使,” but the page extraction returned the title only. The third-party job listing is not treated as authoritative for role terms, status or eligibility. Do not count it as currently actionable without reading the official application page directly.
- Lumina's current official join page describes 25 WeChat groups, a Feishu community and international-university membership, and explicitly invites content, events, competitions and open-source contributions. It has no formal selection/intake route or ambassador role.
- Nanjing Agricultural University states its September 22 AI Co-Creation Space call is for its own teachers and students. That evidence is institution-restricted and not applicable to a MUJ-enrolled student on published terms; the page does not establish whether the first-cohort call is still open.
- NVIDIA's current Chinese page is a University Ambassador program for full-time university teachers who become DLI certified instructors. The student route is therefore restricted, even though an application mechanism exists.
- PaddlePaddle's organizer page says recruitment is ongoing but is dated November 2022; no fresh call or current application route was verified. Its past “global university students” wording cannot establish a 2026 opening.
- Search indexing for WeChat/Feishu and local university communities is incomplete. A search result, historical program page, social-group QR code, or third-party job repost does not prove present intake. This is a focused additional lane, not a complete Chinese-language census.
