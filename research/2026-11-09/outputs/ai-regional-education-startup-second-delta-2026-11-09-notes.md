## Retained records and main findings

- **Lawexa Campus Ambassador — net new.** Official page says “Now recruiting across Africa & beyond,” lists India and UK among countries/campuses, and accepts current students or recent graduates with rolling weekly review. Form includes country, institution, level/year (including Year 2), department optional; no law-major requirement is stated. The role seeks interest in law, technology and leadership. User’s MUJ/ECE profile appears to fit the published student/year criteria, with legal-tech interest and practical campus availability still personal factors. Performance incentives are mentioned without amounts; no base stipend is stated.
- **Haystack Ambassador — verified source-lead correction.** Organizer’s official page explicitly says geography is not a barrier and shows active ambassadors in India. Applications are reviewed quarterly, no fixed hours are required, but candidates must have at least six months of active Haystack community work and evidence of public contributions/building/teaching. If the user lacks that history, it is not an immediate fit.
- **Dropstone Ambassador — net new.** Current official page invites applications through a contact route and asks for one event each quarter for 12 months. Applicants need an active city developer community with at least 100 engineers; no location/country restriction or Jaipur-specific density evidence is published. It is not a salaried role; quarterly event funding and a paid summit speaking honorarium are conditional, not personal salary.
- **Talentta Campus Ambassador — manual verification/correction.** It appeared only in the automated registry screen previously. Official page says applications open now, requires age 18–30, an active account, student status and at least nine months. Geography conflicts within the page (Nigeria-only in the eligibility section versus Africa-wide in FAQ); either way India is excluded. Compensation also conflicts (monthly stipend plus bonuses versus unpaid volunteer with incentives), so no pay claim should be relied on.
- **OrcaRouter Ambassador — verified source-lead correction.** Official page makes the role an add-on to the creator program, requires a public creator channel and at least 1,000 followers, plus one AI post per week and a required bio line. It offers $50 per month of model credits, not cash, while the creator route separately has revenue share. No country restriction is stated. The user’s follower count is unknown.
- **Voya Founding Contributor/Ambassador — net new, adjacent.** Official page advertises 150 founding spots and a live application; it has Contributor, Ambassador and Influencer tiers. The Contributor tier requires no audience size but is a product-feedback/content route; the formal Ambassador tier asks for 50,000+ reach. No country, age, or student rules are published. Revenue share is on completed bookings, not a stipend.
- **Women AI Builders — net new, conditional.** The page says chapter-leader applications are open city by city, but the network is for women in AI and technology. Chapter leads volunteer a few hours a week and host at least one event per quarter; the official Ambassador title is earned through activity/points, while sign-up only creates a Candidate. User eligibility depends on gender, which is not in the supplied profile.
