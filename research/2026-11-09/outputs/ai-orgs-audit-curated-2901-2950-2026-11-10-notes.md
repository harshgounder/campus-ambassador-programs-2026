Cutoff evidence is distinguished from Nov 10 discovery observations. The only apparent student fellowship in this slice has an approximate, not independently verified, pre-cutoff posting date and remains open-appearing rather than confirmed open at cutoff. Picsart community ambassadors are reported as handpicked but no public application was established. No closed route was found for a named-entity ambassador program; the closed PAIR research job is explicitly an adjacent non-ambassador role. I used Agent Reach through Exa for grouped exact-name searches, then added targeted searches for Fudan Chinese-language sources, Picsart AI/community roles, John Snow Labs, and adjacent regional entities. Exa returned noisy cross-entity matches; no tool/API error was reported. Coverage was bounded: not every entity received a separate language-specific query or a full crawl, and short usernames have weak identity resolution. Chinese sources for Fudan were inspected, but no WeChat, Douyin or Bilibili community accounts were searched. No X MCP was used. No-hit means no matching role confirmed in this bounded pass, not proof none exists. Nov 10 page observations are not treated as proof of Nov 9 application availability without dated supporting evidence.

No forms were submitted, personal data entered, accounts created, or organizations contacted. No source files or census ledger rows were edited.
