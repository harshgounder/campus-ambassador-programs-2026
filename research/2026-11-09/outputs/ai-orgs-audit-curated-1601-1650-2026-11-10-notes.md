# AI organization census audit — source rows 1601–1650

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile used only for fit checks: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Method and limits

Ran exact-handle Exa web searches for the 50 source labels with student, campus, ambassador, community, champion, advocate, fellowship, chapter and representative terms, followed by official-page retrieval for plausible exact-entity routes. Search results were screened for exact-entity identity; unrelated results (including Anthropic/Claude matches returned for `modelcontextprotocol`, Consensus for `sinaptik-ai`, and other unrelated student ambassador pages) were discarded. Prior census search, ambassador-title, distinct-lead and AI priority/verification files were checked for exact duplicate routes. No X MCP was used.

This is one bounded pass, not an exhaustive determination that the global AI-first opportunity universe is complete. Search can miss unindexed, differently named, non-English, private or changed routes. Search errors/rate limiting were retried in smaller batches where necessary; they were not counted as negative evidence. No accounts, applications, forms or outreach were made, and original census/repository files were not edited.
