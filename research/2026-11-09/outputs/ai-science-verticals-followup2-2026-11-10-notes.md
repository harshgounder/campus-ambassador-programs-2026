## Bounded coverage

This was a focused official-source search, not an exhaustive census of every society or local-language community. Search covered computational neuroscience/community volunteering; HPC/AI scientific conference volunteering; student science/earth and climate ambassador routes; and cross-domain AI-for-science community/research leads across bioinformatics, climate, Earth observation, astronomy, physics, chemistry/materials, agriculture/ecology. Candidate eligibility and status were checked on organizer pages only. General search results and third-party opportunity aggregators were used for discovery, not as evidence. No application, outreach, account login, or X MCP was used.
