# AI organization census audit — source rows 2351–2400

Prepared 2026-11-10. Evidence cutoff requested: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Screened 50 consecutive entities by exact listed name/handle for student/campus ambassador, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative, and community routes across AI/ML and adjacent technical fields.
- Kept census row number, spelling, tier, category, and order exactly as recorded in `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`. Census candidate URLs were treated as discovery locators only; unrelated terms, products, internships, projects, and similarly named entities were not credited as programs.
- Checked official organizer material when a potentially relevant result appeared. A bounded screen non-hit is not proof no route exists. Unknown geography, age, year, major, and cutoff details remain unknown; no India exclusion was inferred absent explicit evidence.
- No X MCP, forms, accounts, outreach, personal details, applications, or source-repository edits were used.
