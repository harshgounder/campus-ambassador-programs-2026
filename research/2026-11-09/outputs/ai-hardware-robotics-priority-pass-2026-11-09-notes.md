# AI hardware, robotics and physical-computing priority pass — 2026-11-09

## Scope and method

- Targeted AI hardware, robotics/embodied AI/Physical AI, edge or on-device AI, embedded systems, maker/physical computing, and adjacent open-source hardware organizations for individual ambassador, champion, fellow, evangelist or campus roles. Also retained a clearly labeled organization-level partnership lead where relevant.
- Used Exa semantic web search for discovery with query families: (1) official robotics ambassador/community developer programs; (2) embedded hardware and maker ambassadors; (3) edge/on-device AI developer ambassadors; (4) robotics evangelist/champion/fellow programs; (5) robotics company student/campus ambassadors; (6) exact-candidate checks for BitRobot, Menlo, D-Robotics and Seeed; (7) Seeed Pioneer/Ranger application, geography, workload and compensation terms.
- Search results and third-party job boards were discovery only. GitHub code/repository search and Hacker News search were also used for discovery; results were mostly noise or technical product/community discussion and did not produce another current ambassador, champion, fellowship, or evangelist program that survived official-page verification. Each retained program was checked against the organizer’s own page. Official evidence URLs and row-level caveats are in the CSV.
- Checked existing project outputs for Arduino/Qualcomm, NVIDIA DLI, Arm, Edge Impulse, Cerebras, Runpod, MiniCPM, SwytchCode and related robotics/hardware records. Did not duplicate those records. Arm remains represented in existing output as closed to new applications/waitlist; no status correction was warranted from this pass.
- No X MCP used; no applications/forms submitted, contacts sent, or shared central ledger modified. The optional Agent Reach version check could not run because the `agent-reach` command is not installed in this environment.

## Retained leads and rationale

- Menlo Ambassadors: official application listing says global/remote, invites students, unpaid and low commitment; Ashby job metadata simultaneously lists Singapore/HCMC and hybrid, so location is explicitly unresolved rather than silently assuming India is eligible.
- BitRobot Network Ambassador: official dated page invites applications and explicitly includes students and SEA/global contributors; at least two posts/month and optional community/events are specified. Cash/token/referral rewards are contingent, not a salary.
- Seeed Pioneer Mentor: official August 2026 page names student community leaders and has a published apply-and-review pathway for local AIoT/open-hardware technical/community work. No formal deadline, age, country list, or fixed workload is stated.
- D-Robotics Global Evangelist: official call describes international RDK community participation, contribution goals and quarterly reward redemption. Because the call page exposes no date or current cohort window, status remains UNDATED CALL rather than open.
- Seeed Physical AI Global Workshop Partnership: retained as an organization-level lead only. It targets university labs/developer communities and expects an experienced local co-host with a 30+ participant venue; an individual student alone is not established as eligible.

## Exclusions, deduplication and limits

- Existing ledger programs (Arduino/Qualcomm, NVIDIA DLI, Arm, Edge Impulse, Cerebras, Runpod, MiniCPM, SwytchCode and associated prior rows) were not recreated.
- ROAR Berkeley Ambassadors was not repeated: earlier project output already records its October 11, 2026 deadline and K–12/ROAR Academy prerequisites; that adult B.Tech applicant does not meet its stated student-level requirement. The existing record already preserves closed status.
- Robot Framework Ambassadors were not included in this narrow pass because they concern software test automation/open-source software rather than a hardware, embodied-AI, edge-AI or physical-computing ecosystem, and the existing census already contains that program.
- Seeed legacy Ranger pages from 2023 and earlier were not treated as separate new openings; current Seeed pathway was represented via the 2026 Pioneer and Ranger 3.0 pages. The 2026 Physical AI workshop partnership is a separate organization-level route.
- General programs/community membership, product access, competitions, grants or internships without an organizer-published ambassador/champion/fellow/evangelist pathway were not counted. A discovered but unverified rumor or a link on GitHub/HN/other community source alone was insufficient.
- No eligibility was inferred from silence: absence of age or country rules is recorded as “not stated,” not as a confirmed eligibility rule. Geographic scope is distinguished from eligibility confirmation. No evidence was found in these official pages establishing India-specific access for Menlo, BitRobot or D-Robotics.
- This is a priority pass, not a full census of every hardware maker or robotics ecosystem. Search ranking can miss small/local organizations and non-English programs; not all pages expose accessible intake forms. No contacts were made to resolve those gaps.
