Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied `name`, `tier`, and `category` verbatim and in order. Companion CSV follows the neighboring 13-column format.
- Used grouped exact-name public web queries for campus/student ambassador, community leader, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative, creator, developer-community and topic-specific terms, then focused exact searches on quantum, CV/robotics, RL, embeddings, and identified products/labs. A long OR-batched query returned an empty result set; it was not treated as evidence of no program. Followed apparent leads to official/project sources where possible. Checked local-language variants for QData (Chinese), POSTECH-CVLab (Korean), and Tianjin University DRL Lab (Chinese). Search index surfaced yilewang as an individual neuroscience PhD/student developer and Bergside as a Romanian design software company, underscoring the need not to infer a formal organization/ambassador relationship from their census handles.
- The local Agent Reach CLI was unavailable (`command -v agent-reach` returned no path), so searches used the available public web-search tool. No X MCP or login-gated social tooling was used. No forms, accounts, personal data, or organizational contact were involved.
- Excluded generic campus-ambassador directories and unrelated third-party schemes (e.g. Claude, Qualcomm, Zed, Trinka, HackerRank, GUVI, student councils), jobs, generic hiring, research placements, public contribution channels, code contributions, sponsorship and ordinary community membership as evidence of an exact-entity ambassador role.
- Cross-checked all 50 names against the local prior census organization-search wave and title-candidate discovery CSVs; matches there are discovery records, not proof of a qualifying program. No exact-name duplicate qualifying student-leader opportunity was established in curated opportunity outputs.
