## Limitations and safety

This is a targeted discovery pass, not an exhaustive global census. Exa results are similarity-ranked and include duplicates/noise; the search-slot count is not a unique-source count. Language coverage was broad in the query design, but official-page validation was predominantly English-language; fully local-language country directories may reveal further programs. No forms were opened, completed or submitted; no email/DM/phone/contact was sent; no user data were entered; no social post was made; and no X MCP was used. Any live application can be capped or paused between checks. Country branding, a global member network, or a general “worldwide” claim is not proof of every applicant's legal eligibility. The CSV preserves those uncertainties rather than inferring citizenship or visa status.
