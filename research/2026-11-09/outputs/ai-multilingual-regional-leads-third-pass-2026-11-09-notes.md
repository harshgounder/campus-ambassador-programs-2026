Two were new distinct records; 1111 Taiwan was already present in `ai-regional-languages-followup-delta-2026-11-09.csv` and is excluded from this delta. Its FAQ limits participation to students enrolled in Taiwan, and does not fit the MUJ student.

Result snippets and discovery posts were not treated as organizer proof.

## Limits

This was a targeted search tranche, not a complete regional census. It covered seven language/query groups at search level; organizer-page validation in this output was limited to three URLs. Search engines can miss non-indexed, social-only, new, or locally named programs, and language/country wording may require native-language review. Both new live routes still require the user to decide whether to apply; this research did not enter personal details or contact organizers. No completeness claim is supported.
