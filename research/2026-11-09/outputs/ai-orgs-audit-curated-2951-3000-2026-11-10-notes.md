# AI organization census audit — source rows 2951–3000

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive ledger rows and copied `name`, `tier`, and `category` verbatim and in order.
- Used exact-name queries for organization-specific student/campus ambassador, community leader, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative, creator, and developer community roles. Official pages were prioritized; local-language Chinese results were considered for OpenCSG and Dora-rs. Non-LLM areas checked include computer vision, robotics/embodied AI, federated learning, accelerated computing, and learning science.
- Excluded generic ambassador directories and search results for unrelated providers (including SmartBridge, generic campus directories, and separate Anthropic/2077AI/Cursor/Zed/LangChain programs) absent direct attribution to the census entity.
- Research status is a bounded screen, not proof of no programs. No submissions, accounts, personal data entry, or outreach occurred. No X MCP was used.

## Findings

- No exact-entity student ambassador or campus leader intake was verified as cutoff-open, rolling, closed, future, or restricted for these 50 entities. There are no open-appearing or confirmed closed leads in this slice.
- Adjacent participation only: Langroid, Open Spaced Repetition, UXL Foundation, Dora-rs, Deepchecks and Skyhook/Radar have official community or contribution routes; these are not formal student ambassador intakes. IntRoLab is an academic research lab with student research placements, not an ambassador track. OpenCSG search surfaced its Chinese 2025 Open Source Promotion Plan projects, not an ambassador track and not current.
- Non-LLM coverage: official identity/topic sources confirm IntRoLab robotics and embedded AI, Dora-rs AI robotics middleware, FedML distributed/federated AI, SNAP Stanford research including computer vision/graph learning, UXL accelerated computing, Deepchecks ML validation, Charmve computer vision, and Open Spaced Repetition learning algorithms. These topical communities did not yield a verified ambassador route in this pass.
- Official URLs and limitations are captured row by row. Where exact identity is only a handle or could not be verified, a repository/profile locator is not represented as proof of a formal entity.

## Cutoff and source access

A first parallel Exa web-search burst exceeded the provider's documented per-user limit and returned HTTP 429 rate-limit errors. Retried queries sequentially; some official results were obtained, but not every organization received a successful distinct follow-up query. This materially bounds coverage. Exa results available during the Nov 10 audit are observations after the requested Nov 9 cutoff; absent dated evidence no Nov 9 status is inferred. Sources with no publication date are not treated as dated cutoff proof. Other statuses remain unverified, not guessed.

Sources include official project pages linked in the CSV: OpenCSG campaign pages; UXL Foundation home/about; IntRoLab/Université de Sherbrooke; Dora-rs and FedML repositories/sites; Open Spaced Repetition; Langroid; Deepchecks; Stanford SNAP; Charmve; atopile; and Skyhook/Radar community pages. Search-result excerpts were used only to identify pages; where source content was dated after cutoff or undated, it is explicitly not used to backdate availability.

## Validation

The CSV has 13 columns and 50 rows. IDs 2951–3000 are ordered without gaps; each `name`, `tier`, and `category` is checked against the census source row. No row asserts that lack of a search hit proves program absence.
