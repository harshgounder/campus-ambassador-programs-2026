# AI organization census audit, rows 301–350

Audit date: **2026-11-10**. Requested evidence cutoff: **2026-11-09**. The 50 CSV records map one-to-one to data rows 301–350 (header excluded): row 301 is Factory; row 350 is Gumloop.

## Verified findings and leads

- **Zed (row 309):** Official 2026 Campus Ambassador page gives student requirements (undergraduate or master's enrollment; three or more events per year) and benefits (event budget, mentorship, community, team access, swag). It has a live apply link but no dated intake, hours, compensation, or explicit country list. [Program](https://zed.dev/education/zed-campus-ambassador). Zed's general education page explicitly says its separate student product plan is worldwide, but that does not establish ambassador eligibility.
- **Kiro (rows 307–308):** Official ambassador application form asks for country/region and evidence of developer-community engagement; listed benefits include early access, a free subscription and access to the team. It is developer/community focused, not stated as student-only. It is undated, so a currently open cohort is unconfirmed. [Application](https://kiro.dev/ambassadors/apply/).
- **Warp (row 310):** Current official docs say the ambassador program is being built and instruct interested developers to join community Slack for opening news. An older official post described a university Campus Lead program and benefits, but does not establish a current intake. Recorded as future/lead-only. [Current status](https://docs.warp.dev/support-and-community/), [older campus lead page](https://www.warp.dev/blog/get-free-warp-swag).
- **LangChain (rows 324–325):** Official community page lists global Ambassadors and contribution-based Champions/Experts, with event grants, previews, LangSmith access, roadmap access, review priority and swag. A separate 2023 Student Hacker in Residence cohort offered $7,500 for eight weeks at a minimum of 10 hours weekly to full-time students; it is historical/closed and not represented as currently open. Current ambassador application window is unconfirmed. [Community](https://www.langchain.com/community), [historical student program](https://www.langchain.com/blog/student-hacker-in-residence-fall-23).
- **MongoDB Atlas (row 321):** Official student/academia pathways provide learning, certification and Atlas credits; India-specific MongoDB for Academia activity is documented. These are education benefits rather than ambassador/volunteer roles. The Creators Program is an adjacent community-leadership route; its page still refers to applying when applications open in 2025, so current intake is unclear. [Academia](https://www.mongodb.com/academia), [Creators](https://www.mongodb.com/community/creators).
- **CrewAI (row 326):** Official developer community and open-source contribution routes are public and rolling; no student-specific ambassador role verified. [Open source](https://crewai.com/open-source), [community](https://community.crewai.com/).
- **OpenHands Agent (row 331):** Official community invites contributors to open source, events and calls; an official contributor directory tracks merged PRs. This is a developer-contributor path, not a student ambassador appointment. [Community](https://www.openhands.dev/community), [contributor directory](https://champions.hub.openhands.dev/).
- **n8n (row 348):** Official support material describes an Ambassador program for people who host meetups/workshops, share expertise and lead local communities, and links to the program application. No student-only condition, workload, benefit or compensation is published on the cited FAQ; current cohort window not dated. [Ambassadors](https://n8n.io/ambassadors), [official FAQ](https://support.n8n.io/article/im-looking-to-become-an-n-8-n-ambassador).

Other rows are labeled **NO VERIFIED ROUTE FOUND IN THIS PASS**. This bounded outcome means the pass did not establish a route; it is not evidence that no route exists.

## Method and limits

- Verified the source alignment first: `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv` has 5,745 data records; source data row 301 is Factory and row 350 is Gumloop. The audit uses source rows 301–350 without counting the header.
- Conducted official-domain targeted web searches for program evidence across software development agents, database/embedding products, agent frameworks, memory products and automation tools. Positive program details link directly to official organizer-controlled pages. Identity/context links are not treated as proof that no program exists.
- Existing output CSVs were scanned for exact-name rows in prior `ai-orgs-audit-curated-*` batches. The duplicate field records prior exact-name research where found, and distinguishes this from confirmation of an actionable duplicate route. Central ledgers were not edited.
- Program status is separated into active/rolling, future/lead-only, historic/closed and undated/unconfirmed. A live page does not prove applications were open on 2026-11-09. Benefits, workload, student criteria and India eligibility are included only where the cited official page states them.
- India eligibility is not inferred from global branding, a country selector, localized marketing or international community members. Unspecified terms remain explicitly unconfirmed.
- This is a bounded research pass and not a complete negative search of every language, regional channel or unpublished program. No organizer was contacted, no forms were submitted, and no applications made.
