Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row number, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Screened exact names against student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative and creator terms. The supplied result URLs were treated as discovery leads; plausible matches received official-page checks. This is a bounded screen, not exhaustive searches across every language/platform for each name.
- Required exact-entity ownership before counting a lead. Excluded generic lists, namesake programs, standard open-source contribution, product/student offers without a representative role, event/community participation and recruiting pages as ambassador routes.
- No X MCP, applications, forms, account creation, personal data entry or outreach occurred.
