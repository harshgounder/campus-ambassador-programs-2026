# GitHub developer-ambassador catalog audit — 9 November 2026

## Scope and approach

Reviewed the current README for [pransh15/developer-ambassador-programs](https://github.com/pransh15/developer-ambassador-programs). Its README labels the collection as 96 active developer programs and says it was last audited in August 2026; it also warns readers to confirm live application status. I treated it only as a lead list, extracted the AI-focused, AI/ML, student/campus, and closely related program sections, deduplicated against the current 203-row AI priority ledger and 131-row first-wave ledger, then checked potential gaps with organizer-owned sources.

The repository README changed after the source was first indexed: the live content includes 96 programs, and the website title refers to 101 curated programs. Neither count is a verified count of open intakes. Snapshot date and status signals can differ; organizer pages govern.

## Distinct verified additions

- **Google Women Techmakers Ambassador Program 2026**: Added as a distinct, manually verified lead. The Women Techmakers organizer announced its 2026 applications opened May 21; its official post said the close date was June 22. This intake is closed as of Nov 9. The official call is for women in tech; global eligibility is described by Technovation’s community page. The user’s gender and prior community work are not established, so no eligibility conclusion is made.
- **Google Developer Experts (GDE)**: Added as one expert-recognition/community path, not as separate AI, Cloud, Web, Android, or Flutter student programs. Google’s official FAQ says applicants must be 18+, have strong Google-technology expertise and significant community contributions, and be nominated by a current GDE or Googler; it says successful applications ideally demonstrate at least two years of relevant contribution. This is a conditional, low-near-term-fit path from the supplied profile, not a campus ambassador intake.

## Coverage limits

This is an audit of one community-maintained catalog, not the entire GitHub universe and not a complete review of all 96 catalog entries. No forms were filled or submitted, no accounts were created, and no organizers were contacted. It produced two distinct, source-checked additions, one of which is already closed and one of which is a high-bar expert-recognition route. The global discovery task remains incomplete.
