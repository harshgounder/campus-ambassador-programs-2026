## Source alignment

## Screening scope

Each exact census identity received an Exa search for first-party student ambassador, developer champion, advocate, and AI/ML community leadership routes. Apparent matches were checked using organizer-owned pages and identity profiles. Results are separated into unit-specific programs, parent-company programs, referral schemes, and general community access. Generic directory matches were excluded. This is a bounded screen, not exhaustive coverage of all local chapters, regional-language pages, social channels, or unindexed forms. No forms were entered or submitted, and there was no outreach or X MCP activity.
