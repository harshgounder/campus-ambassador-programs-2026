## Method and limits

Reviewed the 50 source labels in order. Ran a bounded exact-name web-search pass for each label using Exa, asking for exact identity and official student, campus, ambassador, champion, developer-community, advocacy, and volunteer-organizer routes. Follow-up searches checked the strongest apparent lead candidates and selected official sources. Exact organization identity remains qualified where a census label is a GitHub user/account, ambiguous name, or project handle. Search excerpts and third-party pages were used for discovery only; they do not establish program terms or complete coverage. No X MCP was used.

The search can miss differently named, poorly indexed, non-English, or invitation-only programs. “No exact-entity route confirmed” is a bounded no-hit, not evidence that no program exists. Undated live pages observed on 2026-11-10 are current observations, not proof of availability at the 2026-11-09 cutoff. Search results dated before cutoff do not by themselves confirm the role remained open at cutoff.
