## Purpose and limits

This is a coverage audit of opportunity discovery for a second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur (India), age 18+, graduating 2029. Target: worldwide ambassador, student, developer, and community opportunities open on 2026-11-09. It does not add or alter the central ledger. “Strong” means a dedicated lane or substantial evidence in reviewed rows; it does not mean an exhaustive global search. A taxonomy covering AI research does not itself prove that an organization runs an open student program.

## Source strength and concrete gaps

**Strong evidence:** dated, official source checks and a sizeable AI/ML verification delta; explicit technical taxonomy from ACM/MSC/ANZSRC/arXiv/OpenAlex; specific AI safety, robotics/hardware, student-network, and regional passes. The non-LLM modality note records its exact bounded scope and honestly distinguishes one unique, closed, ineligible role from absence of discovery. This is good process evidence, not a census.

**Weak evidence:** taxonomy breadth is product-heavy. The main 223-row delta and organization-centric universe can create an appearance of coverage without demonstrating that each research field received a purpose-built search. Some source registries are only “FOUND” or “LANDED”; the ai-map project itself says it remains layer 1 and has not completed its planned conference, curriculum, and gap waves. Regional searches cover a few languages and localized channels, not all major AI communities.

**Uncovered/underspecified:** no systematic organizer inventory by CV/OCR, audio, RL/control, planning/KR, neuro-symbolic, fuzzy/evolutionary, computational cognitive science, quantum/AI, or scientific AI. No robust query lane maps labs and societies in these subfields to open student roles. For all fields, distinguish truly open on cutoff date from undated “Apply” forms, closed cohorts, waitlists, and generic communities; separately track India eligibility, enrollment/degree restrictions, age, graduation year, and role type.
