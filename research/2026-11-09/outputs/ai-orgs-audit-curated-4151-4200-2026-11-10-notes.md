Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive entries from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row ID, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order to the companion 13-column CSV.
- Screened exact entity names against ambassador, student/campus, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative and community-program terms. Census result URLs were discovery leads; plausible matches received official-page checks. This is a bounded screen rather than exhaustive country/language/platform research.
- Counted a route only where evidence established that the exact census entity owns it. Distinguished public community membership and open-source contribution from formal student leader roles.
- No X MCP, forms, applications, personal data entry, messages or outreach occurred.
