# AI organization census audit — source rows 1751–1800

Prepared 2026-11-10. Evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate fit context: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Method and limits

Matched every record to the source census by row position and preserved its exact name, tier and category. Searched exact entity names with student, campus, ambassador, community, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative and creator terms; checked the relevant official/GitHub identity route and followed plausible results to official pages. Reviewed prior source-census search ledger and AI audit/verification outputs for duplicate leads. No X MCP, forms, accounts, outreach, or source-repository edits were used.

This is one bounded audit pass and does not establish global completeness. Search may miss non-English, renamed, private, unindexed or differently named opportunities. A missing route is not proof none exists. For 46 rows, no exact formal student-leadership route was confirmed; generic third-party campus ambassador listings and unrelated identities were excluded. The CSV records exact source profile URLs as identity keys; public-profile existence was not independently confirmed for every one of those 46 rows.

## Plausible exact-entity follow-ups

- **StackBlitz (row 1751):** Official material surfaced community activity and a student-organized hackathon partnership, but no formal student ambassador, chapter, representative or student leader intake. A community manager job is employment and does not qualify. Prior census search ledger had unverified job-search leads; those are not a student-route duplicate. Sources: [StackBlitz community blog](https://blog.stackblitz.com/tags/community/), [student hackathon partnership](https://blog.stackblitz.com/posts/building-together-in-illinois/).
- **ArduPilot (row 1761):** Official documentation confirms GSoC participation for students and a code-of-conduct definition for appointed project representatives, but no formal student ambassador or representative application/intake was found. GSoC is a contribution/mentorship route, not a student-leadership program. Prior census search ledger held GSoC/partner leads. Sources: [ArduPilot GSoC](https://software.ardupilot.org/dev/docs/gsoc.html), [code of conduct](https://software.ardupilot.org/dev/docs/developer-code-of-conduct.html).
- **Kubeflow (row 1778):** Official pages document community participation, an Outreach Program call and a 2026 LFX mentorship cycle, not an ambassador intake. CNCF ambassador pages naming people with Kubeflow experience are CNCF roles, not Kubeflow-run programs. Prior search ledger has community/GSoC leads; no qualifying duplicate route. Sources: [Kubeflow community](https://www.kubeflow.org/docs/about/community/), [LFX 2026 term 3](https://www.kubeflow.org/events/upcoming-events/lfx-2026-term-3/).
- **DataTalksClub (row 1792):** Official GitHub profile describes community contributions such as articles, talks/workshops, course materials and Slack support; no formal student ambassador or chapter/representative intake was confirmed. Prior census search ledger contained general entity/community leads only. Source: [DataTalksClub GitHub profile](https://github.com/DataTalksClub).

For these adjacent community/contributor routes, no formal program-specific age, India eligibility, study-year, major, workload, term or compensation terms were found. Fit for the stated candidate therefore remains unconfirmed.
