# Output archive index

This directory contains the dated follow-up research outputs. The objective is worldwide discovery across every industry. AI receives highest priority, and the full technology industry is in scope; examples do not narrow the search. Begin with the archive-level [current state](../CURRENT-STATE.md), then read the [scope and count reconciliation](SCOPE-AND-COUNT-RECONCILIATION-2026-11-10.md), [READ-ME-FIRST.md](READ-ME-FIRST.md), the [apply-now action sheet](APPLY-NOW-2026-11-10.md), the [coverage and queue](AI-RESEARCH-COVERAGE-AND-QUEUE-2026-11-10.md), and the [repository gap audit](../REPO-GAP-AUDIT-2026-11-10.md). The action sheet is a priority subset, not the total number of open routes. The append-only [continuity log](RESUME-STATE-AND-AUDIT.md) is historical; it is not the current resume instruction.

## Research lanes

- AI programs, labs, models, systems, education, open-source communities, safety, hardware, robotics, scientific applications, and regional ecosystems use the ai- prefixed files and related notes.
- Census discovery and audit files include census-, organization-search-, and small-tier files. These are identity and discovery workflows; a name is not a verified opportunity.
- Worldwide and cross-sector searches include global, fresh, company, software, regional, community, and ambassador-prefixed files.
- Platform checks and corrections include X observations, GitHub and Hacker News leads, browser check notes, and source-verification rechecks.
- Raw search captures and service responses are retained for provenance. Their results require review against organizer sources.

The complete list of files in this directory, including nested output folders, is in [FILE-INDEX.md](FILE-INDEX.md). Sizes and SHA-256 values for the archived source files are in [FILE-MANIFEST.csv](../FILE-MANIFEST.csv).

## Reading a row

Check the observed date, status, evidence URL, geography, student and age requirements, deadline, workload, compensation, and limitation fields together. This archive contains open, closed, future, restricted, uncertain, adjacent, and discovery-only records. Inclusion is not a recommendation to apply, and inclusion does not prove Nov 9 availability.
