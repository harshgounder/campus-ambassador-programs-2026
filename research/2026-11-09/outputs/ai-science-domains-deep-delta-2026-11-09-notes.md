# AI-for-science domains: community and opportunity delta — 9 November 2026

## Scope and result

Focused follow-up on non-LLM AI communities and opportunities in computational biology/biomedicine, chemistry/materials, climate/weather, Earth observation/remote sensing, astronomy/physics, agriculture, and neuroscience/cognitive AI. Profile used: 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur, India, graduating 2029. The accompanying lane contains ten distinct records across true ambassadors, volunteer/contribution routes, a community, research internship, and technical challenges. No application was submitted, no user data entered, and no organizer contacted.

Highest-actionability items as of the cutoff:

- **OBSCURA satellite AI challenge** — open/upcoming Nov 18–24, student-only, majority-age and worldwide participation expressly stated, environmental remote-sensing model task; $50 initial prize pool.
- **NV5 GeoAI Challenge / ASPRS** — open student remote-sensing wildfire workflow challenge due Dec 18, but the page reviewed does not specify country rules; verify the linked rules before relying on India eligibility.
- **SASYAM-AI Agrithon / IIT Bombay TIH-IoT** — ongoing seasonal crop image/data collection and model-building activity; expressly open to Indian citizens and B.E./B.Tech students in any discipline; high field workload (1–2 hours/day) and competitive awards.
- **NASA AI/ML STIG** and **NASA AI-Astrobiology** — current astronomy-focused learning/community routes; STIG says its sessions are remote and open internationally, while the mailing list has no published location barrier.
- **CCAI Volunteer Matching** — current interest route for ML/data-science volunteers to match with climate NGOs and academic labs; actual placement, country eligibility, and hours are not published.

The lane also preserves narrower or adjacent routes with status clearly marked: OpenAQ’s real ambassador cohort closed Nov 6; ISCB YBS 2026 volunteer dates have passed; Space Warps is citizen-science contribution rather than AI model-building; Rosetta Commons REU has an upcoming 2027 cycle but explicitly excludes students studying outside the United States.

## Exclusions and deduplication

- **Nexus AI4S “Geek Pioneer” ambassadors** — already recorded in `ai-asia-languages-fourth-pass-2026-11-09.csv` and the AI/ML verification delta; no duplicate row added.
- **IndiaAI UG Fellowship, Arc Institute AIxBio Fellows, UCSF ci²** — already in `fresh-ai-for-science-student-pathways-2026-11-09.csv`; not repeated.
- **AI4Climate Global Youth Climate Solutions Challenge** — closed Oct 19, 2026; it is a project competition, not a community or ambassador route, so not added to this tighter continuation.
- **Climate Change AI AmazôniaHack 4.0** — event/application ended in September 2026; not current at cutoff.
- **Rosetta Commons REU** is recorded because its 2027 application is current/upcoming, with the explicit outside-U.S. exclusion.
- **Simply Neuroscience Campus Brain Ambassador Network** — official page says the application is closed and points only to a notify-me signup; it is neuroscience advocacy but not AI/scientific-AI specific. Excluded from the candidate CSV. The volunteer program page also has stale 2024/2025 text, so it was not treated as open.
- General ESA/NASA citizen-science projects and OpenAstronomy were not included unless AI or AI-assisted analysis was explicit in the organizer description. Space Warps qualified because ESA states images are AI-preselected and human classifications support lens-finding.
- Chemistry/materials and cognitive-neuroscience surfaced fewer current global student opportunities in this bounded pass. No evidence of open, India-confirmed ambassador roles in those two specialties was found; this is a gap, not proof none exist.

## Limits

This is a focused source sweep, not an exhaustive census. Some official pages provide activity or application routes without stating India eligibility; those remain explicitly unknown. Challenge participation is not equated with ambassador leadership, a fellowship, or guaranteed compensation. No central ledger was edited.
