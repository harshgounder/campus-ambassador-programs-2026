# AI organization census audit — source rows 2251–2300

Prepared 2026-11-10. Evidence cutoff requested: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile used for fit: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive rows from `outputs/census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`, retaining the exact census name, tier, and category and preserving row order.
- Searched each exact entity name/handle with student/campus ambassador, community, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative, creator, and developer-ecosystem terms. Targeted exact-entity searches were used to distinguish similarly named projects and unrelated program pages.
- Primary-source pages were fetched for the plausible attributable community/advocacy routes below. Search hits for generic programs (including X Student Ambassadors, SmartBridge, Campus Unite, Claude Campus, and 2077AI) were not attributed to unrelated handles.
- Checked for exact-entity duplicates in the central AI priority ledger and preceding curated audit slices.

## Official routes or adjacent community leads

- **Project Calico — Calico Big Cats:** Project Calico's official homepage links to the ambassador program and says people can join to share their experience with other users. This is a real general user/community ambassador route. The official program page requires participants to be Project Calico users and describes helping users via talks, meetups, forums, posts, or open-source contributions. It lists discretionary swag/badge with no cash value. It does not establish a student-specific role, whether new participants were being accepted at the Nov 9 cutoff, India eligibility, or hours. The page is dated 2023, so the live description does not verify current intake capacity. Sources: https://projectcalico.org/ and https://www.tigera.io/project-calico/calico-big-cats-ambassador-program/.
- **Alteryx — Advocacy / Alteryx Visionary:** the official page describes current customers, organizational collaborators, and champions as its intended members, with ways to educate, collaborate, and share experience. It is not student-targeted; current Nov 9 acceptance, India eligibility, and student fit are unresolved. Source: https://www.alteryx.com/customer-center/alteryx-advocacy.
- **OpenTelemetry:** official community docs offer contribution, newcomer Slack, and SIG participation. This is an open developer community rather than a student-leader intake. Source: https://opentelemetry.io/community.
- **Feast:** official docs offer Slack/mailing lists and recurring user/contributor community calls; this is general participation, not a student-leader cohort. Source: https://docs.feast.dev/v0.18-branch/community.

For the remaining 46 rows, no exact-entity qualifying formal student-leader route was confirmed in this bounded search. Their census handles often represent individuals/projects rather than organizations. No-hit rows do not prove that routes do not exist. The Uedu/Taiwan `Campus Ambassador` page appeared in searches for unrelated `XiaoDuoYa`; it was deliberately excluded absent evidence that the census handle and Uedu are the same entity.

## Candidate fit and limits

No route in this slice has verified requirements sufficient to establish fit for the stated candidate. The Project Calico and Alteryx entries are general community/advocacy leads, not confirmed student opportunities. Country, age, enrollment/year, major, graduation, duties, time commitment, term, compensation, intake and selection must remain unknown where the organizer did not state them.
