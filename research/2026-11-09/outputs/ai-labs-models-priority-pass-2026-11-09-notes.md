## Exclusions and caveats

- Hugging Face Student Ambassador source found was its official 2022 launch post; no current open intake was verified in this pass.
- Google Student Ambassador listing was US-only, required US work authorization and campus presence, and the search result said its application window ended Oct 5, 2026; the official career page returned “Job not found” when opened. Excluded from priority candidates for the India-based profile.
- Perplexity Campus Partners terms exist in official search results, but no current application page/intake status or precise geography was verified. Not listed as an active candidate. Perplexity’s current Fellows page is a separate paid deployment role; it recruits from the Campus Ambassador community, and its India eligibility/local in-person feasibility remain unconfirmed.
- Huawei Student Developers ambassador page surfaced but failed to open (404) and is a broad developer ambassador program rather than AI-first; excluded.
- Cohere search surfaced an older Cohere For AI Scholars item, not a verified active ambassador/fellow intake. No current organizer-source program found in this pass.
- This was a targeted priority pass, not a census of every AI company globally. Search-result indexing can lag; application pages and availability may change after the as-of date. No forms were submitted and no organization was contacted.
