# AI/ML exact-name audit — source rows 1301–1350

Date prepared: 2026-11-10. Status cutoff requested: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for relevance: 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE, Manipal University Jaipur, India, graduating 2029.

## Method and limits

Ran one bounded exact-name Exa search for each of the 50 source labels, targeting the quoted source name plus student ambassador program context. The first parallel batch hit Exa's per-user rate limit on some requests; those exact same-name queries were retried in small batches until each row had one successful search payload. Results were discovery evidence, not exhaustive web coverage. Exact-name searches can miss differently named or poorly indexed programs, non-English pages, and routes that do not use ambassador language. Common names/handles can yield noisy or mismatched results. No-hit means no route was confirmed in this bounded pass, not proof of absence.

Followed official pages for the Google Gemini Student Ambassador, HeyGen Ambassador, and MetaGPT community-evangelist leads. The Google and HeyGen entries were cross-checked against existing AI/ML verification ledger records. No X MCP was used. Search-result dates and undated live pages alone do not establish a status at the Nov 9 cutoff.

## Findings

Two formal ambassador routes surfaced and both are already in the prior AI/ML verification ledger:

- **Google Gemini Student Ambassador 2026 (India), row 1301:** the official portal currently displays “Registrations Closed” and describes an India-specific program. It publishes Indian citizenship, age 18–25 as of April 1, 2026, active student status at an Indian university, any major/semester, Instagram/LinkedIn activity, and active Gemini use among its conditions. The page text says applicants must not graduate before “September 31, 2026,” which is not a valid calendar date; it is preserved as stated rather than corrected. The current page is undated, so its present closed banner alone does not prove the precise state on Nov 9. The existing verification ledger already records this India Semester 2 cohort as closed at the cutoff (row 160); this is a duplicate, not a new opportunity. Candidate fit remains conditional on citizenship, age, and profile/product-use criteria.

- **HeyGen Ambassador Program, row 1326:** the official page describes a rolling application for standout entrepreneurs, marketers, educators, and knowledge-based creators, with AI-video workshops, community building, content, and feedback. It lists recognition, credits, private community, exposure, speaking/team access, and grants for proposed events. The official page does not publish country, age, student, school, or audience requirements. The existing verification ledger already records the program and its terms (row 70); this is a duplicate, not a new opportunity. Candidate fit and India participation remain unconfirmed.

- **MetaGPT Chief Evangelist, row 1337:** the official FAQ describes a monthly rotating community position to maintain community resources, respond to questions, encourage contributors, and optionally organize small events. It does not describe a student ambassador intake, selection route, availability, expected workload, or benefits. This is an adjacent community duty, not a verified student ambassador vacancy.

Other search results were not recast as ambassador roles. These included individual maintainers who hold third-party ambassador affiliations; ordinary contributor/maintainer paths, community discussions, education resources, generic company partnerships, hiring, technical-writer/content programs, and unrelated ambassador search noise. For example, the tt-a1i account surfaced a Kimi Global Ambassador self-description, but that is an individual affiliation and not an ambassador program for the exact source entity. Generic search-result mentions of campus programs were excluded unless an exact-entity official source confirmed the role.
