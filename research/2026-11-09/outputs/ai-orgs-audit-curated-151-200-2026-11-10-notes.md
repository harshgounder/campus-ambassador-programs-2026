## Eligibility and cutoff handling

Country eligibility is recorded as unknown unless an official source expressly states geography. An India-focused third-party listing is not treated as proof of India eligibility, and a global program label is not assumed to include every country. Where an official opportunity is for a specific Samsung national market, that market restriction is carried through and no India eligibility is inferred. Adobe is a special case: an official Adobe Global Student Community post says a Higher Ed Student Ambassadors pilot has begun in India, while another official student-facing page says the public ambassador program is currently available in the U.S. Those statements may describe different regional tracks or dates. The record therefore identifies India as a pilot, not proof of an open India application. Student, age, major, workload, benefits and deadline details are included only where the cited official page supplied them; omissions are listed as unresolved gaps. “No route verified” means no official route was found in the bounded search, not proof that no route exists.

Dates are evaluated against the 2026-11-09 cutoff. A page crawled or viewed on 2026-11-10 can support the existence of a page, but not retrospectively prove a deadline or availability on the cutoff unless the page itself supplies relevant dated information. Current/rolling status is stated only where official copy uses that language. Adobe page copy says applications are open, but no closing date was shown; cutoff-day availability and open India intake are not established.

## Limits

This was a bounded official-source review, not exhaustive verification across every local-language page, campus partner, private community, or invitation-only route. Several organizations have country-localized official sites; English search and accessible pages may omit those channels. Exact live application-form behavior, age thresholds, country lists, and workload may require opening the organizer's application flow, which was outside this read-only review. Adobe program naming and regional scope appear to vary between official surfaces; the row preserves those distinctions and flags open questions rather than implying universal eligibility.
