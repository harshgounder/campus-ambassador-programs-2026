# AI organization census audit: rows 4251–4300

Research date: 2026-11-10. Requested status cutoff: 2026-11-09.

## Source alignment

The census ledger `outputs/census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv` was read directly with its header excluded. The output contains exactly 50 records, and a programmatic comparison passed for row ID, exact name, tier, and category for every source row from 4251 through 4300 inclusive.

## Screening scope

Each exact handle received a web screening search for organizer-owned AI/ML student ambassador, developer advocate, community builder, and champion routes. Apparent matches were followed up with official-page or identity checks. Generic directory results and unrelated programs were excluded. This is a bounded screen, not exhaustive coverage of all regional, social, local chapter, and unindexed sources. No X MCP, applications, forms, or outreach were used.

## Verified and historical findings

- **Stripe (row 4251):** Stripe’s official Community Builders guide invites people to launch and lead a community for a city or topic, including an “AI Monetization” community. Builders volunteer to host at least four events annually and moderate an online forum. The page says applicants should know Stripe products, have credibility/existing audience, and be able to convene people; Stripe offers funding, access to product teams, and event support. This is a first-party community organizer opportunity, not student-specific and not named “ambassador.” The page was visible Nov. 10 and says “Apply to launch,” but has no visible date or close date; exact intake availability on Nov. 9 is unconfirmed. It does not state age, country, student eligibility, or compensation. [Official guide](https://stripe.com/guides/become-a-stripe-community-builder).
- **KudoAI (row 4257):** Outreachy’s page labels KudoAI a “past community participant.” This is historical participation in an internship/mentorship program, not a current ambassador route. No current student intake was verified. [Outreachy record](https://www.outreachy.org/communities/cfp/kudoai/).
- **Monoscope (row 4252):** Official project pages describe observability tools and events/community activity. No named ambassador or student leadership appointment surfaced; general events and community access are not counted as a formal route.
- **ROCm (row 4279):** A PyTorch Foundation ambassador application surfaced in search. It is not an AMD ROCm program, and no organizer link was established, so it is excluded.
- **youngfish42 (row 4278):** TinyFish ambassador pages surfaced through similar-name matching. The census identity is an individual developer account; no TinyFish connection was verified, so excluded.

## Rejected lookalikes and non-hits

Results also surfaced Microsoft student programs, Anthropic/Claude and OpenAI campus roles, university ambassador opportunities, IncuXai, AsyncAPI, and other generic technology programs for unrelated census handles. These were not attributed without evidence that the exact entity organizes them. The `Stripe Community Builder` opportunity is the only exact-identity first-party community leadership route verified in this batch; it is not tailored specifically to students or AI.

## Cutoff and eligibility cautions

Research occurred one day after the cutoff. Stripe’s currently visible page did not show a publication date or close date, so current visibility is not treated as proof of Nov. 9 availability. KudoAI’s Outreachy page is explicitly historical. India eligibility is not confirmed for Stripe, and no personal eligibility was inferred. Duplicate status remains unknown because this batch was not compared against all earlier ledger rows and artifacts.
