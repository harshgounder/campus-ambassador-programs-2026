## Findings

- **Kiro (`kirodotdev`)**: official Kiro Ambassador program for active developers/community builders, not student-only. The official page says applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, estimates 3–4 hours/month of ambassador-specific work, sets a one-year term, and lists subscription, early access, feedback, calls, and event support. An official May 21 post said applications were still being accepted. The live rolling page was observed Nov 10, after the requested cutoff; exact Nov 9 intake status is not archived here. The official page says globally open where Kiro is available; India availability should be checked against Kiro's supported-regions list.
- **IBM**: IBM Z Student Ambassador program, run with Your Big Year, is a real student/campus leadership route. An official program account welcomed new ambassadors on Oct 29, before cutoff. Partner pages observed Nov 10 describe rolling entry, minimum age 18, undergraduate/on-campus enrollment, IBM Z Xplore Concepts badge, English proficiency, and an eligible-country list that includes India; recommended commitment is 5 hours/week. No stipend was stated. The cohort activity is cutoff evidence; the Nov 10 rolling-application statement does not independently establish intake status on Nov 9.
- **Docker**: a historical Campus Ambassador program was documented in 2017; no current student-specific intake was found. Docker's official Captains community program is a separate current-seeming route for experienced technical community builders and is open internationally; it is not student-only and has substantial expertise/contribution criteria. Its page was observed Nov 10, so current page state alone does not prove Nov 9 intake availability.
- **TencentCloudADP**: no ADP-specific student ambassador route confirmed. Chinese Tencent Cloud developer channels surfaced broader Tencent Cloud campus-promotion ambassador recruitment material (including historical 2021–2023 posts and referral rewards). This parent-platform route must not be attributed to the ADP product without confirmation. ADP campus/product pages and campus events are not themselves ambassador programs.
- **Rust-ML**: adjacent volunteer open-source working-group and contributor activity exists, without a formal ambassador title or student intake.
- **x-cmd**: official community/support participation exists, without a formal student/campus ambassador intake.
- **Cyberbotics**: robotics simulation and educational/research use are adjacent routes, not a formal student ambassador program.

For the remaining names, the bounded exact-name search did not confirm a named-entity student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, student chapter, or volunteer-organizer program. Search pages that merely returned a generic or third-party campus program were not treated as affiliation evidence. Every row retains its official identity anchor and program evidence URLs where applicable; "no hit" does not mean no program exists.

## Search coverage and limits

All 50 census names were sent through exact-name Exa searches for official student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, student-developer, and community-leadership routes. Additional targeted searches checked Kiro, IBM Z, Docker, Rust-ML, Tencent Cloud/ADP, Seldon, and other likely leads. Official GitHub namespaces and official project/company pages were preferred; unrelated same-name results and aggregator pages were not accepted as proof. Chinese-language Tencent Cloud developer surfaces were searched where practical. No X MCP, account creation, application, form submission, outreach, or personal-data entry was used.

This is bounded discovery, not a completeness claim or an exhaustive organization-site crawl. Search results for short handles can be noisy. Direct source pages observed Nov 10 are recorded separately from dated evidence available by the Nov 9 cutoff. Benefits and application availability can change; verify the organizer's current terms before treating a route as actionable.
