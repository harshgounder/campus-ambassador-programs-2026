# AI organization census audit: rows 4001–4050

Research date: 2026-11-10. Requested status cutoff: 2026-11-09.

## Source alignment

The source `outputs/census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv` was read directly with the header excluded. The output contains exactly 50 records. Programmatic comparison passed for the source row number, exact name, tier, and category for every row from 4001 through 4050 inclusive.

## Screening scope

Each exact census handle received a web screening search for organizer-owned student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, or named student community leadership opportunities. Plausible matches were followed up using official pages and exact identity sources. Generic aggregators, unrelated university roles, and same-name results were excluded. This was bounded screening, not an exhaustive audit of every social account, subdomain, local chapter, regional-language source, or unindexed form. No forms, applications, messages, or X MCP were used.

## Verified exact-identity route

- **tinyfish-io / TinyFish Student Program and Ambassador Program:** TinyFish’s official GitHub organization links to tinyfish.ai. The official student program is open to any currently enrolled student worldwide. Students can sign up, claim and submit bounties, and earn gift cards for approved builds; three approved builds automatically grant Student Builder status. Campus Leads are invited from the campus leaderboard, run campus events, and can receive an event budget. The separate ambassador program is open to students and non-students; a referral partner reaches Advocate at 50 qualified signups per quarter, with an event requirement to maintain the title, while Regional Ambassadors are invited and lead locally. The official pages list India academic email suffixes `.ac.in` and `.edu.in` for automatic $35 wallet credit and show campus leads in India. This supports student participation from India; the page says any currently enrolled student can join worldwide, but academic-email domains may affect automatic credit eligibility. [Student Program](https://www.tinyfish.ai/students), [Ambassador Program](https://www.tinyfish.ai/ambassadors), [program launch post](https://www.tinyfish.ai/blog/tinyfish-students-and-ambassadors-programs), [ambassador tiers](https://www.tinyfish.ai/blog/tinyfish-ambassador-program).
