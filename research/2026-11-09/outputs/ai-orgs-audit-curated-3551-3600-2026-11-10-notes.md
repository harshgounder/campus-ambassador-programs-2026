# AI organization census audit — source rows 3551–3600

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`. Copied row number, exact `name`, `tier` and `category` verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Treated census search result URLs as discovery leads only. Per-name/topic screening sought an exact-entity student/campus ambassador, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, community representative or creator intake. A broad search per name is only a screening pass, not exhaustive evidence.
- Reviewed official pages for the strongest/most plausible project and program leads, including Moltis, Spring AI Alibaba, OpenLIT, Machine Learning Tokyo, Intel oneAPI/Intel Labs and PythonOT. A named program must belong to the exact census entity to count as a positive route.
- Excluded generic ambassador directories, unrelated companies' offers, ordinary OSS contribution, open community participation, events and jobs/recruitment as student ambassador intakes.
- No applications, forms, accounts, messages, or personal-data entry were made. No X MCP was used.

## Entity-specific findings and disambiguation

- **Moltis (`moltis-org`, row 3551):** The [official project site](https://www.moltis.org/) and [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/moltis-org/moltis/discussions) identify a software project with ordinary community and contribution channels. No named student-leader intake was found. The census hit for Knotic did not establish an affiliation.
- **Intel Labs (row 3556):** The census hit for Intel's [oneAPI Student Ambassador page](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/developer/tools/oneapi/training/academic-program/student-ambassador.html) is an Intel ecosystem lead. It does not establish a distinct Intel Labs-owned program, so it is not recorded as an exact-entity match. Current page visibility and cutoff availability are separate questions; no dated evidence here establishes Nov 9, 2026 intake status.
- **Spring AI Alibaba (row 3560):** The [official community contribution page](https://java2ai.com/en/community/contributing) and team information describe project contribution/team engagement. No named student/campus ambassador or representative intake confirmed.
- **Machine Learning Tokyo (row 3582):** The [official MLT site](https://mlt.ai/) welcomes people to attend events, join Discord and contribute on GitHub, globally and without an application for ordinary community participation. This is not a named student ambassador/champion program. The third-party campus/student-program hits in the census search results do not belong to MLT.
- **OpenLIT (row 3568):** The [official About page](https://openlit.io/about-us) invites contribution, sharing work and sponsorship. These are project/community pathways, not a named student ambassador intake. Its [contact page](https://www.openlit.io/contact) says it is headquartered in New Delhi; that location alone does not prove India eligibility for an intake that was not confirmed.
- **PythonOT (row 3600):** The census hit for Black Python Devs' Student Ambassador Program belongs to Black Python Devs, and the PyTorch ambassador link belongs to PyTorch. Neither establishes a PythonOT-owned program. The project's [official documentation](https://pythonot.github.io/) identifies the Python Optimal Transport project.
- **Remaining 44 names:** Bounded exact-name and ambassador-topic screening did not confirm an official student/campus/developer/community student-leader intake owned by the exact census entity. Many entries are GitHub-style handles or project names, so the source tier `org` is not itself proof of a legal organization. Generic external programs and unrelated namesakes were excluded.

No dated official evidence in this slice establishes a qualifying route as open, rolling, closed, future or geographically restricted on Nov 9. Undated/current pages are not backdated. ## Coverage limits

This is a bounded screening pass, not an exhaustive country-by-country or platform-specific audit of all 50 entities. Unindexed, private, regional and local-language pages may exist. Handle ownership and legal identity remain unresolved where the official source did not establish them. Exact Nov 9 status, application terms, selection, term length, workload, compensation and benefits remain open gaps unless directly stated in the CSV. Negative findings mean no qualifying route was confirmed in the reviewed evidence, not that none exists.
