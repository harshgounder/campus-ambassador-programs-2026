# AI non-LLM modalities and disciplines: official-source sweep — 9 November 2026

## Scope and method

A targeted discovery pass covered computer vision/pattern recognition/OCR, speech/audio/music AI, reinforcement learning/control, robotics/autonomy/embodied AI, planning/knowledge representation, fuzzy/evolutionary/soft computing, and AI hardware/edge. Six Parallel AI web-search calls were run, with 18 query phrases total. Results were discovery leads only. Candidate terms were checked against the existing central AI ledger and the specific AI hardware/robotics and AI developer-tool passes; four official URLs were fetched for exact program status and criteria.

## Distinct organizer-source result

One new, distinct, official program record was suitable to preserve: the IEEE Robotics & Automation Society Technical Committee on Soft Robotics Student Representatives call. This is a student technical leadership role adjacent to an ambassador program. The 2026 call required PhD or MS-PhD enrollment, IEEE and RAS membership, and soft-robotics interest; applications were due 15 February 2026 AOE. Its term is 1 March 2026–28 February 2027. As of the research cutoff, it is closed, and the undergraduate profile does not meet the stated degree-level requirement. The sources give no India-specific eligibility statement, so India eligibility is unknown rather than assumed.

The role offers conference travel support and one free registration to a RAS-sponsored conference, not a stated stipend. No weekly workload estimate is published.

## Exclusions and deduplication

- Edge Impulse general and campus ambassador routes were already in the central AI ledger; the 2026 campus cohort was selected and no new cohort call was confirmed.
- Qualcomm Student Ambassadors and Qualcomm/Arduino/Edge Impulse developer ambassador listings were already present in the discovery or existing program ledgers; not added again.
- BitRobot, DeepStation, Learnrithm, Swytchcode, IncuXai, Agora, and other AI-community results were already represented in existing ledgers/follow-up files; not repeated.
- The Construct Robotics Ambassador page says the program is currently paused.
- Search results for OpenCV, OCR, speech/audio and music-specific ambassador roles did not yield a new, directly evidenced organizer program beyond duplicates and general community routes in this bounded pass.
- Arm Ambassadors appeared in prior source/discovery files, but the official page now points to a waitlist and says applications open twice yearly. It requires experienced developers/community leaders; no student-specific or India-specific criterion was found. Not repeated as a new program record.
- IEEE RAS Soft Robotics Student Representatives was included despite being closed because it is a distinct modality-specific organizer route and gives a clear degree-level exclusion for the user's profile.

## Coverage limits and action boundary

This was a finite search sample, not an exhaustive survey of every society, project, or company in these technical fields. Search count is not a program count. The result file contains one distinct program record and does not imply that no other programs exist. No forms were opened or submitted and no organizers were contacted. No X MCP was used. Agent Reach was unavailable in the prior project environment; Parallel AI search and official organizer pages were used for this lane.
