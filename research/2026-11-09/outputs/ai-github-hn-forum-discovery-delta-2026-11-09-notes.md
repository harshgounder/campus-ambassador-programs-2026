## Result

One distinct, current-looking adjacent route was recorded in `ai-github-hn-forum-discovery-delta-2026-11-09.csv`: 2hives’ AI Ambassador Programme for human operators of AI agents. It was discovered through a recent n8n Community post and an active GitHub kit repository. The kit describes the operator terms and rules. It is not a conventional AI developer, student chapter, research, robotics, or hardware ambassador role: the product is guided meditation audio with AI-written scripts and synthetic voice, and the work is disclosed referral promotion. Status is therefore “current/open-appearing,” not guaranteed admission.

The profile match is conditional. The operator must be 18 or older, a human, and eligible for payout in their country. The terms include people living in countries the payout platform serves, subject to sanctions and local law, but the reviewed terms do not explicitly confirm India. The scheme is commission-only, with no salary or guaranteed payment, and requires an AI agent plus tools/equipment at the operator’s expense.

The only fresh 2026 community forum recruitment discovery was the n8n post for 2hives. Web indexing can omit, misdate, or de-rank posts, while Reddit/community pages can be ephemeral or unverified.
- **Other community channels:** This pass did not log into or inspect private Discord servers, closed groups, or non-indexed community portals. Existing AI source audit files already cover known GitHub catalogs, AI Map, prior HN searches and selected community forums; this delta adds a focused fresh repository/HN/forum check, not a complete sweep.
