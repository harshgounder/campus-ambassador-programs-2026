# AI organization census audit — source rows 1551–1600

Prepared 2026-11-10; requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile used only for fit checks: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Method and limits

The 50 exact census labels were compared with their individual organization-search records in `census-organization-search-wave2-2026-11-09.csv`. Those results are discovery leads only: many return generic campus-ambassador aggregators, unrelated programs, or no exact-entity evidence. I made a separate Exa/agent-reach pass using exact-name searches framed around official organization identity and student/campus/ambassador/champion/advocate/fellow/chapter/representative routes. The service rate-limited part of the batched follow-up and some output was truncated, so the CSV does not represent 50 fully completed official-page investigations. Follow-up searches prioritized exact official domains for deepset/Haystack, Prefect, HumanSignal/Label Studio, Vercel/v0, Apollo Auto, promptfoo, Activepieces, and Letta. I read official pages for Haystack, Prefect, Label Studio, Vercel, Apollo Auto and Letta where relevant.

Exact entity names were distinguished from unrelated search results. Search-result URLs in prior census logs were not treated as affiliation evidence. The Github profile links for many rows locate the census account only; they do not establish a separate legal organization. Official role pages were used only where corroborated. No X MCP, accounts, forms, applications, or outreach were used. This bounded pass does not establish that a route does not exist, nor that the global AI opportunity universe is complete.

## Findings and near-matches

- **deepset-ai / Haystack Ambassador Program (row 1567):** Official page: https://haystack.deepset.ai/ambassadors. It invites existing contributors active for at least six months, with regular public work; quarterly application review; activities include sharing, talks, workshops, meetups and helping builders. It is not student-specific. The page lists ambassadors based in India, but does not explicitly state country eligibility. The user's prior Haystack activity is unknown. The live page is undated and observed Nov 10, so its text does not prove intake was open by the Nov 9 cutoff. Retain as an undated/rolling-intake lead, not confirmed open or fully eligible.
- **PrefectHQ (row 1557):** Versioned official Prefect documentation names Prefect Ambassador through Club 42: https://docs-2.prefect.io/2.20.11/community/. No current application route, intake date or eligibility terms were confirmed. This is an older/versioned lead with no verified Nov 9 status.
- **HumanSignal (row 1553):** Official Label Studio page invited community content proposals from people with AI/ML experience and product use: https://labelstud.io/blog/contribute-content-to-the-label-studio-community/. The page is dated Jan 26, 2023. Its Creators Fund beta was limited to Feb 1–Jun 30, 2023; this is a stale content-contributor opportunity, not a current student ambassador role.
- **Vercel (row 1563):** Official Vercel community guidance describes the v0 Ambassador program: https://vercel.com/kb/guide/how-to-contribute-to-the-vercel-community. The existing AI priority verification ledger already records the v0 Ambassador intake as closed as of Nov 9: `ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv`, row for “v0 Ambassador Program.” This is an exact duplicate and no new ledger item is warranted.
- **Letta (row 1589):** Official forum announced Letta Labs beta testing in September 2025, with feedback, early access, and a private channel: https://forum.letta.com/t/join-letta-labs-our-beta-testing-program/66. Product beta participation is not a student ambassador route. Prior source row 342 was also checked and had no verified student route.
- **Near-matches excluded:** Apollo Auto's official university education, research and competitions are not ambassador appointments. Promptfoo's official contribution/docs results did not confirm an ambassador route. Activepieces' July 2025 community thread was an interest poll about a possible expert partner program and explicitly left launch details prospective; not a live student ambassador intake. Search results about Agora, Adobe, Microsoft, Claude, Swytchcode, SmartBridge, campus aggregators, or other unrelated organizations were not attributed to census organizations merely because they appeared in the same query results. Cilium/Dapr references to CNCF ambassadors were not verified as organization-specific intake for the exact census entities.

For the remaining rows, no exact-entity official student route was confirmed in the bounded pass. Row-level identity locators and uncertainty are retained in the CSV; they are not negative proof. For a no-route row, age/student/year/major criteria, India eligibility, workload, term, benefits/pay, route and cutoff status remain unknown.
