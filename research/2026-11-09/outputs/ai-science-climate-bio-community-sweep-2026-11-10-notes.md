## Coverage and limitations

This is a bounded sweep, not a complete census of global societies, labs, scientific platforms, or local chapters. Search coverage does not prove absence. The CSV contains one additive, explicitly qualified near-match; it should not be read as evidence that OOI was actively recruiting at the cutoff or that an India-based undergraduate would be selected. A dated call or direct organizer confirmation would be needed to resolve those points. No central ledger was modified.
