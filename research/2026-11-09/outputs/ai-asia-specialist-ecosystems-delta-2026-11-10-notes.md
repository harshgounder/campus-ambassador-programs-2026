## Result

One distinct route was retained in `ai-asia-specialist-ecosystems-delta-2026-11-10.csv`: **Tongyi Lingma Campus Ambassador (通义灵码校园大使)**, linked from the official Tongyi Lingma campus page. The current page displays an “Apply to Join” control and describes a campus-ambassador system. Its linked Alibaba Cloud Developer Community article, dated January 10, 2025, describes student responsibilities and an Alibaba survey application link. The article says to promote AI-assisted programming within schools, support school/department partnerships and course co-development, publish product practice/tutorials, answer beginner questions, and organize campus talks, workshops, online classes and discussion groups. Benefits are product access, learning opportunities, expert exchange, possible ACA/ACP exam access, and official promotion of campus activities/tutorials. No stipend, weekly hours, term, deadline, country, or acceptance status is published.

This is an **unverified, conditional lead**, not a confirmed-open or India-eligible opportunity at the November 9 cutoff. The live campus page was observed Nov 10; the article is an older, user-contributed post on the organizer’s developer community. The publicly linked form returned no readable fields, and was not completed. The page/article do not explicitly exclude overseas students, but they frame work around a student’s own school and contributing to talent development in China. MUJ participation and remote/cross-border onboarding are unknown.

## Search coverage and limitations

Used indexed web searches and official organizer pages. Query families included Simplified Chinese searches for 2026 AI/open-source student ambassadors across coding assistants, models, frameworks, vision/speech/robotics/edge AI, AI for Science, accelerators, safety and data; regional searches for AI campus leads in Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand and Vietnam; and named organization checks for ModelScope, PaddlePaddle, OpenMMLab, MindSpore, Baidu AI Studio, Shanghai AI Lab and Tongyi Lingma.

Organizer pages directly reviewed: Tongyi Lingma campus homepage; the homepage’s linked Alibaba Cloud Developer Community recruitment article; its linked survey route (no readable public form content); Nexus AI4S official ambassador page; OpenBMB official ambassador page; OpenAI Student Collective; and regional pages for BaseMe and NAII. Search indexing does not comprehensively cover WeChat, QR-only or account-gated Chinese channels, Bilibili, Gitee, Zhihu, campus group chats, or local-language social feeds. No forms were submitted, no one was contacted, no community joined, and X MCP was not used. The pass is targeted and not exhaustive.
