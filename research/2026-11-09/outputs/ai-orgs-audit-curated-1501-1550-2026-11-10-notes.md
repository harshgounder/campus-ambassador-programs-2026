Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile used only for fit checks: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Method and limits

Ran one exact-name Exa web search for each of the 50 labels in 10 batches of no more than five. Every query returned a result payload; no failed search was counted as a no-hit. Search results were discovery evidence. Followed official pages or program-linked sources for E2B, Tencent, Infisical, Kestra and Qwen, and searched prior curated AI audit and priority/verification files for exact-route duplicates. No X MCP was used.

Coverage is bounded and may miss differently named, poorly indexed, non-English or unlisted routes; usernames and common names can be ambiguous. “No route confirmed” does not prove absence. Pages observed Nov 10 were not automatically backdated; undated live pages are marked with cutoff uncertainty. Internships, research, fellowships, competitions, coursework, jobs, credits and ordinary open-source contribution are not counted as ambassadors unless separately coupled to a qualifying leadership route. No accounts, forms, applications or outreach were made.
