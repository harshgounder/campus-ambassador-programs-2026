# AI global product and literacy ambassador recheck — 9 November 2026

This lane looked for current or open-appearing AI product, AI coding, and AI-literacy student/community ambassador routes not already represented in the project’s verified AI ledger. Search discovery was followed by organizer-owned page checks. Three distinct records were retained; closed or duplicate candidates were excluded.

## Distinct records retained

- **Anna Ambassador Program (Anna AI Apps):** the organizer’s community forum post dated 22 October 2026 says the program is officially open long-term, with rewards counting from Nov 1, and describes two application steps: try Anna Deck and give product feedback, then submit a 30-day promotion plan. The official forum post does not state geography. The University of St. Thomas career board lists the program as remote, recruiting Oct 26–Nov 25, and says applicants must be undergraduates or graduates at U.S. colleges. Treat MUJ/India eligibility as unresolved and potentially excluded; a remote label is not worldwide eligibility. The official forum post says rewards depend on usage and purchases, with full rates after approval. No applicant data or application was submitted.
- **Bleenk Ambassador Program:** official page currently invites applications, describes a global ambassador community and a campus-leader track, and lists 20% recurring referral commission for a referred customer’s first 12 months. The official launch announcement is dated June 21, 2026 and explicitly includes students. The linked application destination (`/en/ambassadors/apply`) rendered a generic “Application received” state on a direct GET rather than a visible form, so the application being currently accepted is not confirmed. Record as open-appearing only. No form was submitted.
- **AI Sprouts Ambassador Program:** organizer page embeds a current Google Form whose visible first page asks name, school and current grade; its page invites students to support responsible-AI learning and workshops. The organizer’s chapter page lists locations in the US and India, but it does not claim global scope or define accepted countries. Its wording and grade field may be school-oriented, so a university student’s eligibility is unclear. No personal information was entered or submitted. This is distinct from AI Sprouts Africa.

## Dedupe/exclusion checks

- Checked candidate program names against the central AI ledger, first-wave ledger and all `outputs/*.csv`; none of the three records above appeared as a curated program.
- Kimi Ambassador, Microsoft Copilot Student Ambassador, KriyagniAI, ImagineArt, Arduino & Qualcomm, Mamentis, NANDA Shapers, Menlo Ambassadors and Super Qodoer were already in project outputs and were not duplicated.
- JA Worldwide AI Ambassador Program’s current page states a March 20, 2026 application deadline, so it is closed as of this audit.
- TAMIDS is a university-based US program, not a worldwide route; AI Sprouts Africa is a separate Africa-focused organization.

## Method and limits

The `agent-reach` executable is not installed (`command not found`). I used the available Exa web search and Parallel search/fetch tools, then fetched organizer-owned program pages directly. Search results are discovery evidence, not proof of program status. The application pages were only read; no account, form, or organizer contact was used. This lane is a small delta, not an exhaustive AI ambassador census.
