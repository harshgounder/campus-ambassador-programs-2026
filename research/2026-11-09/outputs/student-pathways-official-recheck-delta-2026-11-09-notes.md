## Limitations

The review is a targeted recheck, not a full audit of every source in the CSV directory. An application link or job posting being visible does not ensure MUJ is eligible. No form was submitted and no organizer was contacted. GitHub’s official docs state the annual month but do not announce the next cycle date. Intel’s contradictory DevMesh and old Intel.com signals leave intake unclear. SAS’s official regional page provides inadequate current application/eligibility details. MATLAB roles are campus-specific and the current open list is a snapshot. AWS does not establish that a Student Builder Group already exists at MUJ; only its new-group route is clearly usable from the published criteria if all applicant facts hold.
