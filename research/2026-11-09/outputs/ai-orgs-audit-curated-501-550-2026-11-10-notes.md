# AI organization audit: census rows 501–550

Cutoff requested: 2026-11-09. Audit created 2026-11-10. The census CSV header was excluded; source data rows 501 through 550 were verified before research, beginning with Absentia and ending with Canva Magic (50 rows). The source names, tier, category and row numbers are copied exactly. No central ledger was changed.

## Method and limits

Read-only web searches targeted each organization for official student ambassador, campus leadership, contributor and community routes, then official-page excerpts were fetched for the most plausible route hits. Official pages were preferred; unrelated school ambassador pages, third-party reposts and company-name collisions were rejected. Search was scoped and not an exhaustive crawl of every product, social account or localized site. “No verified route found” means only that this pass did not verify one; it is not proof of absence.

Official-page checks with actionable or notable findings included You.com’s global professional ambassador page; AI4Bharat’s official research identity/open-source focus; DhiWise’s notice that its ambassador program is being revamped; v0’s published ambassador benefits and application; Base44’s application form and age/term requirements; Asana’s general user ambassador community; Microsoft Copilot’s student program details and affiliated-university condition; Canva’s US Campus Canvassadors cohort and 2026 timeline; GMI Cloud’s Clouder rewards and regional cash caveat; Zoom’s education ambassador announcement; and official Notion/Airtable community context. These are not equivalent routes: student status, AI relevance, intake status and fit vary and are stated per row.

## User-fit rules

Fit considered the user’s stated profile: age 18+, second-year BTech Electrical and Computer Engineering at Manipal University Jaipur, India. India acceptance, student eligibility, affiliated-school status and cash payments were treated as unknown unless official evidence stated them. Noncash benefits and conditional compensation are distinguished in row notes. No applications, forms or messages were submitted.

## Duplicate check

The audit compared the rows against the earlier curated audit outputs 001–450 for exact actionable student-route duplicates. No exact duplicate was confirmed for this batch. This is not a claim that the organizations or general communities are absent from all source ledgers.
