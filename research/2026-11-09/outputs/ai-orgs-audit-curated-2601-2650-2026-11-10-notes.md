## Coverage and method

- Screened 50 consecutive census records in order. Exact names/handles and their source URLs were checked as discovery leads; route language included campus/student ambassador, community, volunteer, advocate, champion, representative, fellowship, and developer community.
- The census source labels every record `org`, but several identifiers are personal handles, repositories, or project names. The output retains those identifiers and avoids treating unrelated search results as organizational ownership.
- Distinguished formal student leadership from general open-source contributions, learning resources, publishing/contributor opportunities, research roles, employment, partner programs, and community discussion.
- No exact-entity intake was confirmed for the remaining rows through this bounded review. That is a no-hit result, not evidence that no program exists. Material accessed on 2026-11-10 is not used as proof of status on the 2026-11-09 cutoff.
- No forms, personal data, accounts, outreach, X MCP, or original repository edits were used.
