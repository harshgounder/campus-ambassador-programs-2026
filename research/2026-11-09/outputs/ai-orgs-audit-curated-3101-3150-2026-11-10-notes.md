The Agent Reach command-line program was unavailable (`agent-reach: command not found`); `mcporter` was present. 23 exact-name Exa calls completed with search results. 27 were rejected with HTTP 429 rate-limit errors; the per-row response/error artifacts were held temporarily in `/tmp/ai-census-3101-3150-exa/` and are not part of this delivery.
- Used a separate search-service fallback in four bounded batches (up to four queries per call) to investigate the highest-likelihood name collisions and route candidates: Ravenscroft/Glitternetwork/AlephAITech/Rebelytics; Polyaxon/Laravel/Filament/NExT-GPT; Morphik/LLM Workflow Engine/Odigos/edtechre; and OpenSumi/AIDotNet/NExT-GPT/Memobase. These fallback results surfaced the specific distinctions recorded above. They do not replace per-channel searches for all 50 entities.
- Reviewed exact official pages where surfaced, including Chinese-language OpenSumi/AIDotNet/community material. No X MCP was used. This pass did not exhaust X, LinkedIn, local forums, organization-controlled social accounts, every local-language query, all subdomains, closed groups, or unindexed application forms. Search throttling left some exact queries incomplete.
- Results/pages were reviewed on Nov 10, one day after the requested cutoff. Where no dated evidence establishes Nov 9 status, the CSV says so; pages observed on Nov 10 are not silently backdated. Statuses are kept distinct: active-appearing official proposal page (Polyaxon, cutoff uncertain); ambiguous or closed/older external role listing (Ravenscroft & Schmierer); open/rolling official external Replit route (unaffiliated to census entity); historical closed/dated contribution cohort (OpenSumi); adjacent ordinary contribution or academic access (several projects); and no confirmed exact-name route (bounded non-hit).
- India/MUJ eligibility, applicant fit, age, enrollment year/major, workload, compensation, and deadlines are recorded only where the cited page establishes them. Candidate fit is conditional on the user-supplied profile reflected in neighboring audit outputs (18+, second-year B.Tech ECE, Manipal University Jaipur); no additional personal data was used. No forms were opened for submission, no personal data entered, no accounts created, and no organizations contacted.
