# AI organization census audit — source rows 3301–3350

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied `name`, `tier`, and `category` verbatim and in order into the 13-column companion format.
- Used exact-name source-ledger URLs as discovery leads, then focused public-web searches and official-page checks for credible matches. Checked English and Chinese materials where indicated, particularly Jittor. Agent Reach CLI was unavailable, so research used the available Exa search/fetch connector. No X MCP or login-gated social tooling was used.
- Excluded generic campus-ambassador directories and unrelated namesake schemes (including X/Microsoft, Claude, GUVI, HackerRank, SmartBridge, DEVCON, Qualcomm, Solana, UQ Ventures, AWS, OpenAI/Codex, NMG and YNOV), jobs, ordinary membership, open-source contribution, research participation, GSoC or referral marketing as evidence of a student ambassador appointment. Oxylabs is separately labeled as an adjacent creator/affiliate route.
- Identity could not be fully resolved for several short handles or repository-style labels. A locator is not proof that a handle owns an organization-run program.

## Organization-specific findings

- **Jittor (row 3336; deep-learning framework):** the official Tsinghua-hosted Chinese notice dated 2023-06-16 recruited university and graduate students as campus ambassadors. It sought campus outreach experience, interest in AI/deep learning and familiarity with Jittor. Duties included campus promotion and events; listed benefits included an appointment certificate, remuneration, merchandise, and possible study/internship/visiting opportunities for top ambassadors. Application was via Tencent Docs with Chinese QQ/WeChat channels. This proves only a historical intake; no dated official source established a 2026 intake open on Nov 9, or India-campus eligibility. Source: [Jittor campus ambassador notice](https://cg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/jittor/news/2023-06-16-08-50-00-00-jtds/).
- **PyData (row 3320; data science community):** official PyData/NumFOCUS pages provide a pathway to start a local meetup chapter by contacting PyData and following organizer guidelines. This is an international volunteer community/chapter organizer path, not ambassador-branded or student-specific. NumFOCUS pays the Meetup platform cost; the organizer page does not state compensation, student terms, selection criteria or a dated cutoff snapshot. Sources: [Start a PyData Meetup](https://pydata.org/start-a-meetup/), [NumFOCUS PyData program](https://numfocus.org/programs/pydata). The census seed included a Prayagraj chapter URL, which is not itself proof of India-specific intake.
- **Oxylabs (row 3327):** official affiliate page invites digital specialists, including creators, bloggers, YouTube publishers, GitHub developers and Discord users with established channels, to apply for referral commissions. This is creator/affiliate marketing, not a campus ambassador or community-leader appointment; no cutoff-dated snapshot or student-specific terms were found. Source: [Oxylabs affiliate program](https://oxylabs.io/affiliates).
- **MAIF (row 3324):** official Campus Dev article describes a 22-month in-person application-development retraining program with Simplon.co, launched in Niort on 2024-01-29 for jobseekers or career changers. It is not an ambassador route and does not fit current MUJ enrollment. Source: [MAIF Campus Dev](https://entreprise.maif.fr/campus-dev).
- **Leela Chess Zero (row 3325):** official site offers a developer community and 2026 Google Summer of Code technical projects; neither is a student ambassador appointment. Sources: [community](https://lczero.org/about/community), [2026 projects](https://lczero.org/contribute/gsoc/2026).
- **Eclipse Zenoh (row 3303):** official community materials offer Discord help, issue tracking, roadmap discussions and contributions, not a named student ambassador intake. Source: [Zenoh community](https://zenoh.io/community).
- **Asymptote Labs (row 3317):** its official careers page lists a founding Developer Relations Lead role; employment is separate from a student ambassador/fellowship program. Source: [Asymptote careers](https://www.asymptotelabs.ai/careers).
- **Bitbrain (row 3302):** official page identifies a neuroscience/AI/EEG neurotechnology company; no student-leader route was identified. Source: [About Bitbrain](https://www.bitbrain.com/about-bitbrain).
- **Forward Future (row 3347):** official pages identify its AI media and education properties, but no ambassador, campus-leader, fellowship or creator intake surfaced in bounded searches. Source: [About Forward Future](https://forwardfuture.com/about).

## Cutoff, geography, candidate fit, benefits

Research was conducted Nov 10, after the requested Nov 9 cutoff. Only dates published by a source are used as dated cutoff evidence. Jittor’s only verified ambassador notice dates to 2023; PyData and Oxylabs show current undated pathways, so exact Nov 9 availability is not established. No country exclusion is inferred from missing eligibility text. Jittor states student eligibility but not whether non-China campuses are eligible. PyData describes an international chapter network but makes no India-specific or student-specific promise. No candidate-specific selection criteria or quantified workload/term were established for an open qualifying 2026 cohort.

## Coverage and access limitations

The search sweep was bounded, not an exhaustive review across all 50 entities’ private channels, regional sites, local languages and social platforms. Source-ledger seeds produced numerous unrelated namesake and third-party results, which were excluded. Agent Reach CLI was unavailable. No X MCP was used. No forms were submitted, accounts created, personal data entered or organizations contacted. Absence from indexed public search is not proof that no unindexed or invitation-only route exists.

## Validation

CSV has exactly 13 columns and 50 data rows. IDs are 3301–3350 in ascending order with no gaps; each `name`, `tier`, and `category` exactly matches the corresponding source-ledger row. Only the requested CSV and notes file were written.
