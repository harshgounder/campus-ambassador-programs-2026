# Chinese-language specialized AI community discovery — fresh additive pass

**Research cutoff:** 2026-11-09. Discovery and source review took place on 2026-11-10. Pages without dated evidence are labeled unverified at the cutoff rather than assumed open.

## Result

Saved two distinct organizer/community-lead pathways in `ai-china-specialized-ecosystems-fresh-pass-2026-11-10.csv`:

- **MindSpore Study Group (MSG) Organizer:** the official MindSpore site has an explicit organizer invitation, role duties, a resume/application-letter route, and selection stages. Parallel's official-page extraction was acquired 2026-10-01, before cutoff, but the page has no dated call or end date. Therefore this is an observed live route, not proof of continued acceptance on November 9. It is distinct from the MindSpore Evangelist listing already in prior work.
- **Triton China Community Contributor / Community Ambassador:** an official FlagOpen/Triton China Community WeChat article explicitly describes a community-ambassador track and a QR-based contributor intake. The accessible source extraction did not include a publication date or QR destination, so cutoff status remains unverified.

Neither row asserts India eligibility. Both could fit ECE interests in AI systems and hardware, but the MSG page does not say whether overseas local groups are accepted, while Triton's language and community-location expectations are unstated.

## Discovery and primary-source review

Used **Exa web search** and **Parallel web search/fetch**. Discovery included Simplified Chinese queries for:

- specialized AI community organizers and campus leads across vision, speech, robotics, edge AI, AI for Science, and accelerator ecosystems;
- CSDN and Gitee AI ambassador/open-source evangelist calls;
- AI safety, model evaluation, datasets, and benchmarks;
- AI climate, weather, agriculture, and remote-sensing volunteer/community roles;
- named technical routes for MindSpore MSG, Triton China Community, OpenGrow, and OpenI.

Two official pages were directly fetched for the qualifying rows: [MindSpore MSG](https://www.mindspore.cn/msg) through Parallel and Exa; and the [Triton China Community WeChat article](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/vtSMaGgp_Uxo_QvmmV7sjg) through Parallel and Exa. The official Gitee [OpenGrow project page](https://gitee.com/ccf-ai-infra/opengrow) was checked and describes an AI-infrastructure volunteer/open-source project, but does not itself specify an individual ambassador, campus representative, or named community-lead intake; it was not included as a qualifying route. Official OpenI's 2022 “启梦之旅” campus-lead call was historical and therefore excluded.

Existing MiniCPM/OpenBMB, ModelScope, Qwen, Baidu/PaddlePaddle, Kimi, Huawei Kunpeng, MindSpore Evangelist, Meitu, and other China/local-channel entries were not repeated.

Historical OpenI/Paddle/MindSpore posts were not called current openings. No forms were submitted, account created, community joined, QR scanned, or organizer contacted. X MCP was not used.

## Query coverage and limits

Coverage included targeted indexed-web searches on CSDN/Gitee and specialized CV, speech, robotics, accelerator, AI-for-science, environmental/agriculture, and safety/evaluation terms. The pass also searched official technical-community pages and Gitee-hosted AI infrastructure. It produced two distinct leads, not evidence that the channel inventory or Chinese-language ecosystem is exhaustive.

Search indexing is incomplete for WeChat official accounts and closed or login-gated CSDN, Gitee, Zhihu, Baidu Tieba, Feishu, and campus groups. The Triton WeChat page's publication date and QR destination were not extractable. The MindSpore MSG page is undated. No source confirmed India eligibility, and a live-looking page is not evidence that intake was open on the cutoff date.
