# AI organization census audit — source rows 3651–3700

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row number, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Used exact-name/topic screening and the supplied census links as discovery leads, then checked likely official-entity/program pages. The bounded pass looked for student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative or creator pathways. A broad search per name is screening, not an exhaustive audit.
- Required exact-entity ownership before counting a program. Excluded unrelated namesakes, generic directories, ordinary project contribution, event attendance, general community membership, commercial affiliate/partner programs and recruitment.
- No X MCP, applications, forms, messages, accounts, or personal-data entry were used.

## Material findings and mismatch checks

- **MemMachine student ambassador result vs `KimMeen` (row 3680):** The [official MemMachine Student Ambassador page](https://memmachine.ai/community-student-ambassador-program/) describes an actual campus representative program, including 1–2 events per semester, project/content activity, mentorship, a six-month active phase and a Discord-based application path. However, the census entity `KimMeen` resolves to [Ming Jin's GitHub profile](https://github.com/KimMeen), which identifies him as a Griffith University assistant professor. No evidence ties that account to MemMachine, so the MemMachine program is an unrelated search result and is not attributed to this row. The page's current visibility does not establish exact availability on 2026-11-09.
- **MetaTool AI (row 3656):** [GitHub's metatool-ai organization](https://github.com/metatool-ai) identifies MetaMCP and related MCP software projects. The census search result for MemMachine is a different organization. No metatool-ai student ambassador route was confirmed; project contribution remains an adjacent pathway.
- **`ARISE-Initiative` (row 3687):** Census results include [Arize AI's Phoenix Ambassador page](https://arize.com/phoenix-ambassador-join/), a genuine program open to students, professionals and independent builders with event support, but it belongs to Arize AI/Phoenix, not `ARISE-Initiative`. Another hit, Africa2100 ARISE campus chapters, is also a different entity. Neither is attributed to this row.
- **`michaelgutmann` (row 3697):** Census result for the official [OpenAI Codex Ambassadors](https://developers.openai.com/community/codex-ambassadors) page is a topical match, not evidence that the exact GitHub-style account owns that program. The official page currently states applications are closed and that students are not being accepted into that program; this is current page status only and is not projected back to Nov 9. No affiliation with the census account was established.
- **Bright Data (row 3683):** Its [official partner FAQ](https://brightdata.com/partners/faq) describes global commercial partners and affiliate referrals. Those routes are not campus/student ambassador programs.
- **Other 45 rows:** Bounded exact-name screening did not confirm an official student/campus/community student-leader intake owned by the exact census entity. Many are GitHub-style handles or project names; the source tier `org` does not establish a separate legal organization or program owner. Census hits for HackerRank, Claude, CampusUnite, PyTorch, Arize, Black Python Devs, or other entities were treated as unrelated unless ownership was established.

## Coverage limits

Exact identity, program ownership, Nov 9 availability, application requirements, selection, duration, workload, compensation and benefits remain unresolved except where stated above. Negative findings mean no qualifying route was confirmed in reviewed evidence, not that none exists.
