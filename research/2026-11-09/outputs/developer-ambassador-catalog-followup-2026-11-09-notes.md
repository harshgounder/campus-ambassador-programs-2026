# Developer ambassador catalogue follow-up — 2026-11-09

## Scope and result

Used the direct GitHub developer-ambassador catalogue at https://github.com/geshan/developer-ambassador-programs as a discovery index only. It is a third-party catalog, not application or eligibility proof. Checked all 16 requested names against every existing output file before writing. The focused first-wave/AI-ledger comparison found no matches for 15 names; Twilio Champions was already represented in multiple verified outputs, including the AI developer-ecosystem ledger. GitKraken Ambassador was already supported by a detailed organizer-page verification in `github-ai-catalog-tools-audit-2026-11-09.csv`; its current page remained consistent, so no duplicate row was added.

Created the CSV with 15 records: organizer-verified additions/status enrichments for prior raw or unverified leads, one new Tableau entry, and a narrow material correction to Twilio’s existing record (explicit 18+ and English requirements). GitLab Heroes and Unleash are deliberately marked as unresolved/current intake not confirmed; no open application is claimed. Recognition and user-community programs are separated from student-only campus roles.

## Current eligibility signals

- BrowserStack is the clearest explicit broad-geography case: its organizer says users globally can apply, subject to two recent community contributions.
- Figma explicitly says applications for starting new groups are open, with groups around the world; it expects at least one event each quarter.
- Docker explicitly says its program is open internationally and requires applicants to be 18+, with substantial expertise, contributions, and visibility.
- Twilio’s existing open 2027 record is materially enriched: the official page specifies 18+ and comfort communicating in English. India appeared in the prior official application-country-menu inspection; no citizenship requirement is inferred.
- Tableau’s 2026 cycle is closed; the organizer invites notification signup for 2027.
- Appwrite’s latest explicit staff reply found says it is not onboarding new Heroes while revamping; no later opening notice was located.
- GitHub Stars is nomination-only and bars self-nomination; GitLab’s current monthly rolling recognition is Notable Contributor, not a confirmed GitLab Heroes intake.
- Auth0’s current 2026 Auth0 Heroes page is invitation-based for the Japanese developer community. It does not establish an open global Auth0 Ambassador path or India eligibility.

## Source coverage

- Exa searches: 18 calls, with `numResults` 10, 10, 8, 6, and 4 per workstream batches, for 144 result slots total (including duplicates), counted per the Exa Search skill guidance.
- Read the organizer-owned program/status pages for the requested candidates. Where a current page was absent, inaccessible, historical, or had a same-name collision, used current adjacent organizer pages to establish the limit and marked the program unresolved rather than open.
- Compared requested names against all files under `outputs/`, then separately checked the named first-wave and AI-ledger files. Existing matches in large discovery/census files were treated as raw leads, not evidence of verified eligibility or intake.

## Limits

This is a bounded follow-up using the 16 names from the catalog, not a census of all developer programs. “Open-appearing” means the organizer currently invites applications or presents a route but gives no explicit dated cycle; “not confirmed” does not mean formally closed. Do not infer citizenship or worldwide legal eligibility from a global roster or silence. No forms were filled or submitted, and no organizers were contacted.
