## Scope and method

Focused search for student ambassadors, campus representatives, youth/community champions, and individual AI-security or cyber-defense assurance programs. Discovery used Agent Reach's Exa web search; final facts below were checked against organizer-controlled pages (including official organization LinkedIn posts where no current program page was available). The accompanying CSV uses the required 10-field schema from the existing priority-verification export. It contains five verified program records, including active, rolling, closed/current-cohort, and non-student fit states so that application status and eligibility boundaries remain explicit.
