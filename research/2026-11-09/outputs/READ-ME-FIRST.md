## Applicant and status rules

Assess the profile from published requirements: age 18+, second-year B.Tech Electrical and Computer Engineering at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation in 2029. The search is worldwide. Do not infer India eligibility from a global description or from a selected cohort.

Keep confirmed open, open-appearing, rolling, undated route, future, closed, restricted, unknown, and adjacent cases separate. A live form, search result, directory listing, or page response by itself does not establish an open program. A source checked on 10 November does not automatically prove its state on 9 November.

## Research evidence and limits

Discovery rows, search snippets, census names, and raw service responses are leads. They do not count as completed program investigations. Verify organization identity, organizer authority, intake status, country rules, age and student conditions, deadlines, workload, compensation, and application route from the best available source. Preserve contradictions and uncertainty in the row and its notes.
