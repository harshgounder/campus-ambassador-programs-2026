Parallel supplied an additional four-query search batch for Juejin campus ambassador recruitment, localized AI student ambassadors, Taiwan/HK/Singapore campus roles, and embodied-AI/open-source student communities; its result count is not added to the Exa slot count.

Platforms and organizer sources represented in results/rechecks included official Kakao, NAVER, OpenI, Jittor/Tsinghua, Huawei Kunpeng, Meyo/觅游, Juejin, Linkareer (discovery only), Google Student Ambassador Thailand/Vietnam, Uedu/Yodo, and Taiwan regional community sources.
