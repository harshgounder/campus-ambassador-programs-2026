## New distinct lead

- **Connectome 2026–2027 Student Ambassador** — organizer says any student worldwide is eligible, but the student must first complete one of three community actions (outreach/shares, peer encouragement, or a self-led connectomics activity) and then apply with evidence. The cycle is stated as August 2026–December 2027, with competition submission due Dec. 1, 2027; no ambassador deadline or required time is stated. This is a real ambassador role attached to a science competition, not a relabeling of competition participation. It is computational neuroscience/neuroimaging adjacent, not explicitly an AI program. Sources: [ambassador route](https://clematisresearch.github.io/connectomeguide/get-involved/), [official dates](https://clematisresearch.github.io/connectomeguide/important-dates/), [competition overview](https://clematisresearch.github.io/connectome/).
