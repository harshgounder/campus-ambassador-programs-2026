# AI organization census audit — source rows 2301–2350

Prepared 2026-11-10. Evidence cutoff requested: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: age 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Screened 50 consecutive census entities by exact name/handle with targeted student/campus ambassador and community champion/advocate/fellow/chapter/representative/creator searches; used official organization pages where a potentially relevant result appeared.
- Preserved census name, tier, and category exactly and retained source-row order. Exact GitHub/profile URLs are locators, not proof that every handle is a formal organization or an official program owner.
- Reviewed the listed candidate/source URLs as discovery leads only. Unrelated exact-term hits were not attributed to the census entity. A bounded search non-hit is not proof no route exists.
- Compared likely leads with the central AI/first-wave ledgers and prior curated slices. The Firebase/GDE relation and the MemMachine/Open Multi-Agent name collision are explicitly separated; neither is a distinct Firebase or OMA campus cohort.
- No X MCP, account access, forms, outreach, personal information, applications, or source-repository edits were used.
