# China and regional AI community lane

Research cut-off requested: 2026-11-09. Searches and direct page reads were performed on 2026-11-10, so an undated page observed live on Nov 10 is **not** treated as proof that applications were open on Nov 9. That evidence gap is carried into the status field for each row.

## Files and interpretation

- ai-china-regional-leads-2026-11-10.csv contains candidate ambassador, community-builder, evangelist, creator-incentive, and campus-leadership opportunities.
- Status is separated into active/rolling, historical, undated, unverified, and lead-only evidence. “Page live” does not itself mean the intake was open by the cutoff.
- The intended candidate profile used for fit notes is an 18+ second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected to graduate in 2029. Where the official source omits a condition, the file says unknown rather than inferring it.
- A few adjacent programs are not formal ambassador roles. They are clearly labeled as co-builder, creator incentive, or historical campus recruitment so they are not mistaken for named ambassador cohorts.

## Search methods

Used Agent Reach's Exa web search and fetch, plus Parallel web search. Search languages were English and Simplified Chinese. Queries were grouped around:

- AI 开发者大使 计划, 人工智能 开源 布道师 招募, 社区大使 AI 中国 招募
- site:modelscope.cn 大使 招募, 魔搭社区 布道师 招募, Qwen Ambassador eligibility global India
- PaddlePaddle 开发者大使 招募, 百度 飞桨 校园开发者 大使, 文心大模型 社区大使 招募
- MindSpore 开发者布道师 招募, 华为 开发者大使 AI 招募, 昇腾 开发者 布道师 计划
- 腾讯 混元 开发者 大使 计划, DeepSeek 社区大使 开发者, Kimi 月之暗面 开发者大使, and variants covering ByteDance/Doubao, Zhipu/GLM, MiniMax
- robotics/physical-AI and edge-AI ambassador calls, including Arduino/Qualcomm/Edge Impulse
- AI community ambassador opportunities in India and Southeast Asia

First-party program pages, application pages, official community posts, and official company/community announcements were preferred. Third-party aggregator material is retained only as an explicitly unverified lead. Search snippets were used to discover sources; the most material pages were fetched for direct content. No social X MCP was used, no forms were filled, and no one was contacted.

## Coverage and limits

This is a focused discovery lane, not an exhaustive census. Search coverage included Baidu/ERNIE/PaddlePaddle, Alibaba/Qwen/ModelScope, Huawei/Kunpeng/MindSpore, Moonshot/Kimi, MiniMax, ByteDance/Volcengine/TRAE, Tencent, and physical/edge AI. Queries also targeted DeepSeek, Zhipu/GLM, Xiaomi, and Tencent Hunyuan, but this pass did not produce a dated official open ambassador/campus call for those targets. That is a discovery gap, not evidence that no community program exists.

Chinese-first pages often omit citizenship and cross-border admission rules. “Global,” “not limited by city or campus,” or an international meetup list is not automatically treated as confirmed India eligibility. English-language pages can also be regional landing pages, so country rules need checking on the application form or official terms.
