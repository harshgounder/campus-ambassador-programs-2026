## Scope and findings

Focused official-source sweep for AI student advocacy, campus champions, fellowships, and related pathways across AI for biology/biotech/drug discovery, medicine/imaging, chemistry/materials, climate/weather/Earth/astronomy, agriculture, physics, and scientific machine learning/open science. User profile used for fit: age 18+, second-year B.Tech Electrical & Computer Engineering at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029. No forms were completed, contacts made, or personal information entered.

Three distinct science pathways were added to the accompanying CSV. None is an active ambassador opening:

1. **IndiaAI Fellowship (UG track)** — fresh AY 2026–27 applications were announced on Oct 16. India nationals only; for a four-year UG program, eligibility begins after sixth semester/third-year results, so this user is not currently at the published application stage. It may be a later-cycle route. The official page has inconsistent grade thresholds: eligibility says CGPA 7.0/70%, while the UG disbursement FAQ requires 80% for the second tranche. No deadline is visible in the reviewed sources.
2. **Arc Institute AIxBio Fellows** — next applications are listed for January 2027, but teams must be located in North America. It is a future, geographically restricted AI-biology research fellowship; India-based students are outside the stated location requirement.
3. **UCSF Center for Intelligent Imaging (ci²)** — the official page says the program is paused for 2026 and to check back in late 2026/early 2027. Prior cohorts involved in-person, full-time medical-imaging/ML research and F-1/J-1 visa eligibility. No next intake is announced.

These are research pathways, not ambassador positions. Status labels distinguish open-cycle, future, and paused routes; no worldwide eligibility is inferred.

- 12 Parallel AI web-search calls, 36 targeted queries across science subfields and named science-AI organizations.

## Limits

“Open cycle” means an official source announced fresh applications; it does not mean the user meets every criterion or that the portal was tested. “Future” means the organizer named a future application month but had not opened it as of the audit date. “Paused” means a source explicitly says no 2026 program and only suggests checking later. Workload, location, compensation, and geography remain unknown when the organizer does not state them.
