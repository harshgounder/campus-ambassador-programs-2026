## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive entries from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row number, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order to the companion 13-column CSV.
- Used exact-name/topic screening and the supplied URLs as discovery leads, then checked likely official entity pages. Screened for student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative and creator pathways. This is a bounded screening pass, not exhaustive platform-by-platform research.
- Required exact-entity ownership before treating a search hit as a qualifying route. Excluded generic directories, unrelated programs, standard contribution, community participation, research/event participation, jobs and university-wide opportunities not owned by the census entity.
- No X MCP, form submission, applications, personal data entry, accounts or outreach were used.
