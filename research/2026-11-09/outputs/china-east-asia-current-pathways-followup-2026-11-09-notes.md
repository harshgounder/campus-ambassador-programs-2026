## Source limits and geography conflicts

- Searches used indexed web search and official page fetches. This is not a native-platform census: no logged-in V2EX, Bilibili, Zhihu, Gitee, WeChat public-account or Feishu interface review was done in this follow-up. Indexed search access to Chinese social/group content is incomplete; a missing result is not evidence of no program.
- The TEEP international scheme signals overseas student participation, but this specific project page does not name India or enumerate eligible countries. It also requires in-person residence and participant-paid travel/visa costs; do not label it confirmed India-eligible without an organizer answer.
- KARURA publishes an international workflow and cross-Pacific cohorts, but does not explicitly list India. This is positive international evidence, not a categorical India guarantee.
- SJTU SAIL explicitly welcomes international visiting students/interns. It does not say whether remote work from India is accepted, and travel/visa support is not stated.
- Search counts represent eight query objectives, not eight opportunities or complete coverage. The three CSV records are current/open or open-appearing on the stated pages as of Nov. 9, not confirmed admissions or offers.
