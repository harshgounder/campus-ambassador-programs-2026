Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile supplied for fit assessment: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive entries from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied row ID, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Screened exact census identifiers against student/campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative and community-leader terms. Census URLs were discovery leads, not evidence of ownership. Likely matches were checked against first-party sources. This is bounded screening, not exhaustive research.
- Distinguished organizer-owned student leadership from general community participation, project contributions, research recruitment, commercial programs, unrelated opportunities and programs owned by similarly named entities.
- No X MCP, forms, applications, personal data entry, messages or outreach occurred.
