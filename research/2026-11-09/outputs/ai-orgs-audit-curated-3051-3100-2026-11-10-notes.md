## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows and copied `name`, `tier`, and `category` verbatim and in order. The companion CSV matches the neighboring 13-column format.
- Searched exact names/handles with student ambassador, campus ambassador, community leader, champion, advocate, fellow, chapter, representative, creator, developer community, plus relevant topic terms. The search sweep included grouped exact-name queries spanning all names in the slice; additional focused official searches were made for Datadog, Atmosphere, lightly-ai, and Borglab. Discovery was followed by organizer/project-source review for meaningful leads.
- The Agent Reach executable required by the local search skill was not installed (`agent-reach: command not found`), so Exa/Agent Reach CLI and social backends could not be used. Used the available web-search tool instead. No X MCP was used. Chinese and local-language results were not systematically covered; this is an explicit gap for Chinese projects/handles and regional communities.
- Excluded generic campus-ambassador directories, unrelated provider programs (for example Claude, Zed, Qualcomm, and third-party university schemes), broad job/recruiting pages, and bare contributor/sponsor/community pages as student ambassador evidence.
- Checked the 2026-11-09 core verification outputs (`ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv` and `ambassador-research-first-wave-2026-11-09.csv`) and adjacent relevant output slices for obvious qualifying duplicates; exact-entity duplicate found: none.

## Cutoff, access, and coverage limitations

Searches and source observations were made on Nov 10, after the requested Nov 9 cutoff. Only dated material published before cutoff is treated as cutoff evidence; later crawls and undated pages are not backdated. The Datadog 2026 cohort announcement dated May 26 is pre-cutoff evidence for program existence only, not open status. Official program pages crawled/observed Nov 10 are post-cutoff observations. No applications were opened or submitted, no accounts or personal data were entered, and no organizations were contacted.

Coverage is bounded: grouped public web queries are not exhaustive per-name searches across private communities, university-only channels, non-English sources, social platforms, or all relevant local communities. The absent Agent Reach route further limits social/channel breadth. Four Chinese-language search formulations were also run for the China-associated handles; they surfaced unrelated Jittor and university programs, which were not attributed to these census entities. Do not treat a no-hit result as verified absence. All row-level remaining questions, including India eligibility, age/enrollment/year rules, deadlines, compensation, and time commitment, are captured in `unresolved_gaps` unless explicitly proven for the Datadog adjacent program.
