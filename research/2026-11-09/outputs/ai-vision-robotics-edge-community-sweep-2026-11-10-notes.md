# Vision, robotics, edge AI and technical-community opportunity sweep — 10 November 2026

## Scope and cutoff

This additive sweep looked for explicit ambassador, student-representative, technical-advocate, community-lead, or organizer routes in computer vision and imaging (including medical imaging), speech/audio, robotics/embodied AI, autonomous systems, edge AI, accelerators/chips, and AI hardware. Fit reference: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur, India, expected 2029. Status cutoff: 2026-11-09. Research ran Nov 10; undated/live current pages were not backdated into confirmed Nov 9 openings. The CSV retains two formally explicit but closed routes and one clearly marked near-match; none is claimed to be open at cutoff.

## Discovery and primary-source method

Used Exa and Parallel web search. Search snippets were treated as leads, then relevant official pages were fetched with both services. Exa searches covered:

1. Official computer-vision, imaging, OCR, medical-imaging and pattern-recognition communities with student ambassador, representative, community-lead, developer-advocate or chapter-organizer roles.
2. Speech-recognition, audio-AI, voice-AI and music-technology student ambassador/campus representative/community organizer roles.
3. Robotics society student ambassadors, student representatives, technical committee student reps and community-organizer calls.
4. Edge-AI, embedded-ML, accelerator, chip and AI-hardware developer/campus ambassador programs.

Parallel searches used six clusters (three formulations each): (a) CVF/BMVA/pattern recognition and medical-imaging AI student roles; (b) IEEE Signal Processing, IEEE RAS, IEEE Circuits and Systems; (c) robotics, embodied AI and autonomous systems; (d) speech/audio AI; (e) RISC-V, NVIDIA Jetson/DLI and Intel student ambassador routes; (f) MICCAI, medical imaging and computer-vision student boards. An Exa follow-up targeted official IEEE SPS, robotics society, OpenCV and edge-AI/hardware ambassador sources. Search results surfaced Microsoft Student Ambassadors, Copilot, CNCF, Hugging Face, Qualcomm, Intel, Edge Impulse, Jetson, MICCAI, IEEE IMS, IEEE SPS and IEEE RAS among other leads. Official pages fetched for exact checks included MICCAI Student Board, RISE-MICCAI, IEEE IMS, IEEE SPS, and Qualcomm (Qualcomm only for deduplication).

## Findings and status

- **MICCAI Student Board (MSB) 2026:** a distinct medical-imaging student leadership and organizer route. The official call set a Feb 14, 2026 AOE application deadline, results Feb 21, and first board meeting Feb 23, so it was closed well before cutoff. The application accepts Bachelor's as a degree level but preferences vary by officer role: senior students for scientific events; early Master's/PhD for finance; UAE/Abu Dhabi knowledge for local liaison. PR is the most plausible role without those stated preferences. No age or explicit India rule is published. The original call page named Abu Dhabi, later superseded by MICCAI's official relocation announcement to Strasbourg. The year cycle elapsed either way.
- **RISE-MICCAI Ambassador 2026:** a one-off event ambassador role where an attendee in person presents one poster for an author unable to travel. It is not an ongoing campus/community representative program. The MICCAI conference call says Sep 11 deadline; the RISE page, updated Sep 10, says Sep 13. Both event days (Sep 27 and Oct 1) preceded Nov 9. No age, nationality or travel/registration support is stated. This is retained as a closed explicit ambassador near the requested field.
- **IEEE Signal Processing Society (SPS) Ambassador 2026:** a distinct representative program related to speech/audio, signal processing and image/video processing. The official evergreen 2026 program page lists Feb 20 nomination deadline and Mar 1 start; it requires student members to be nominated by a higher-grade/full member and at least one year of SPS student membership. A separate official April 2026 call states May 15 deadline, end-of-May announcement, immediate start, and restricts that call to US and South America. Both dated windows ended before Nov 9. No country restriction is published in the February page, so India eligibility for that earlier cycle is unknown; the April cycle explicitly did not include India. Workload/term and pay are not stated.
- **IEEE IMS Student Ambassador:** a near-match only. The official page describes formal student-ambassador duties and an annual June–July cycle, but gives no year-specific 2026 date, no explicit India eligibility, and no AI-specific remit. It aligns with ECE/sensing/measurement, less directly with AI. Its cutoff status remains unknown.

## Exclusions

- Society membership, chapters, conference attendance, research-network participation, tutorials, generic volunteering and ordinary open-source contribution were excluded unless explicit representative/ambassador/organizer duties were published.
- Competitions and hackathons (including edge-AI challenges), internships/research placements, contributor-only programs, credits, affiliate/referral schemes and creator-only opportunities were excluded.
- MICCAI's Doctoral Consortium, educational challenges and generic society groups are activities rather than separate representative routes; the MSB officer route is the distinct included item.
- Intel Student Ambassador, Edge Impulse, Qualcomm/Arduino, NVIDIA Jetson/DLI and Arm search leads were deduped against the supplied program outputs or excluded where pages described certification/education resources or generic developer-community membership rather than a new record.
- IEEE RAS Regional Student Representatives appeared in search as already appointed regional representatives with a contact route, but this pass found no dated open nomination/applicant call; the Sectional Student Representative call was a 2024 section-chair nomination cycle and is not treated as a 2026 applicant opening. IEEE SPS and MICCAI routes are retained because they had dated explicit 2026 ambassador/board calls despite being closed.

## Coverage limits

This is a bounded search sample, not an exhaustive census of societies, local chapters, private forums, regional developer groups, research networks, company developer relations teams or university communities. Search coverage does not establish absence of other opportunities. Search indexes may surface stale, dynamic or undated pages. Official sources establish only the details in the CSV; they do not establish the candidate's ability to travel or unlisted requirements. No forms were submitted, accounts created or organizers contacted, and no X MCP was used. Only these two additive outputs were created; no central ledger or application content was changed.
