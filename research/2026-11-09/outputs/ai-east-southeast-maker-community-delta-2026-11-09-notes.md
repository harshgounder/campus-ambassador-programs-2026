## Limits

This was a targeted discovery tranche, not a complete East/Southeast Asia census. Search coverage was limited to four language groups and eight queries; only two new organizer pages were directly validated. Search engines may omit local forum posts, university portals, and community channels, and translated marketing language may not reveal legal or practical country restrictions. For Kunpeng, partner-university eligibility cannot be resolved from the public page alone. No claim of worldwide completeness is supported.
