# AI campus program follow-up — 10 November 2026

## Claw Campus Ambassador Program 2026–27

The official [Claw Campus page](https://claw.phd/programs/campus) describes three university tracks. The user's undergraduate route would have been the Claw Builder Club: lead an AI club, organize workshops and hackathons, share resources, and receive a stated USD 3,600 cash stipend if selected. The same page says students anywhere may apply, applicants must be 18+ and have work authorization in their country of study, and some AI/Claw proficiency is expected. Its FAQ gives a Sep 1–12 application window and says the Fall 2026 program is closed; therefore it is recorded as closed before the Nov 9 cutoff, not as an actionable lead. The next cohort timing is unpublished.

## JobWizard Campus Ambassador Program

The official Chinese-language [JobWizard ambassador page](https://jobwizard.ai/zh-cn/ambassadors) describes two paths: Creator and Connector. It says any major and any student year may apply, no previous experience is required, applications are reviewed continuously, replies arrive within five business days, the intake is open all year, and the expected commitment is about 3–5 hours weekly. Creator videos that pass review earn at least USD 30 each, with higher pay for higher-effort formats and possible viral bonuses. Connector benefits include free Pro while active, community access and founder calls; the page does not state a guaranteed cash payment for this path. The page names no country boundary, so India eligibility remains unknown.

The application was inspected directly in the user's Brave browser. The visible form asks for name, email, university, year, major, social account, chosen path, motivation, promotion plan and prior content/community experience. It says submissions are sent to JobWizard through FormSubmit. No field was filled and nothing was submitted. Because the published policy says year-round but no Nov 9 snapshot is timestamped, the ledger labels cutoff availability as inferred from the ongoing policy and live page checked Nov 10.

## Method and limits

These two rows were checked against organizer-owned pages, and exact-title searches found no pre-existing row in the central AI priority ledger before they were added. Claw is retained as a closed program for status hygiene; JobWizard is a rolling, geography-unknown applicant route. The JobWizard page is product-specific AI career tooling. It is one applied-AI route and does not represent the full AI field. No X MCP, application submission, account creation or organizer contact was used.
