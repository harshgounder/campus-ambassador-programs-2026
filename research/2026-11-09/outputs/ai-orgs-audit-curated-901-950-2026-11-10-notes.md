## Verified routes and distinctions

- **Cohere Labs Open Science Community:** official community-programs page says active members can propose and lead community programs, earning the “Community Lead” title with Cohere Labs staff support when sufficient interest exists. This is a genuine community-leadership mechanism, not a student ambassador intake. Page gives no age, education, India eligibility, deadline, standard workload, or stipend. [Program page](https://labscommunity.cohere.com/community-programs), [community home](https://labscommunity.cohere.com/).
- **StepFun Builder Program:** official campaign page invites builders to create agents, workflows, tools, integrations, or demos; post a demo on X tagging StepFun and using `#BuildwithStepFun`; submit demo link/API UID. Qualifying submissions may be rewarded or featured. This is a builder campaign, not a campus ambassador role. The page did not state a close date, age/education/country requirement, workload, or reward amount. [Official page](https://platform.stepfun.ai/builder-program).
- **IBM Champions:** IBM’s official program is a global advocacy recognition program for IBM customers and partners, based on expertise and knowledge sharing (speaking, user groups, articles/videos and related activity). It is IBM-wide, not IBM Research’s student ambassador program; no student-specific requirements or stipend were confirmed. [Official overview](https://www.ibm.com/community/champions-program/).
- **Kujira Academy:** a Team Kujira official post dated 2023-10-17 described a four-week Student Ambassador Program with seminars/workshops, individual and team business proposals, research/articles/threads, and weekly feedback calls. It is blockchain-related rather than broad AI/ML, and this historical article does not establish an open route as of 2026-11-09. No current application, geography, student age, or cash amount was verified. [Dated post](https://medium.com/team-kujira/kujira-academy-the-litepaper-8bb3502d99d1).
- **Alibaba Cloud AI Forward:** official page lists developer AI competitions, courses, datasets and certifications; one contest listing names students in India and several nearby countries. These are challenge/learning opportunities, not DAMO Academy ambassador appointments; individual challenge dates and terms vary. [Official page](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/developer/ai-forward?_p_lc=1).
- **MongoDB:** official Students & Graduates page covers internships/recent-graduate jobs, not an ambassador application. A third-party campus-award article mentions an Ambassador Program, but I did not find an official current intake route, so it is not counted as verified. [Official early-careers page](https://www.mongodb.com/company/careers/students-and-graduates).
- **ByteDance / TRAE:** official ByteDance pages surfaced student career recruitment, i.e. jobs. Search also returned a closed TRAE campus-marketing/ambassador job posting on a third-party jobs board; this is not proof of a student ambassador intake and is treated as closed employment evidence only. [Official early-careers portal](https://jobs.bytedance.com/en/students/trends?index=0), [campus portal](https://jobs.bytedance.com/campus/topseed).
- **DeepChem:** official community forum surfaced project contribution and Google Summer of Code material (including a 2026 community topic). GSoC/project participation is a mentored contribution pathway, not an ambassador role. Current deadline/pay details were not verified in this pass. [Community forum](https://forum.deepchem.io/c/community).
- **SpeechBrain, Modular, FastFlowLM, and H2O-3:** official project sources invite ordinary code, documentation, issue, forum/Discord, office-hour, or demo participation. These were classified as contribution/community access, not appointed student leadership. Pages: [SpeechBrain](https://github.com/speechbrain/speechbrain), [Modular MAX guide](https://github.com/modular/modular/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), [FastFlowLM community guide](https://github.com/FastFlowLM/FastFlowLM/blob/main/docs/community.md), [H2O-3 contribution guide](https://github.com/h2oai/h2o-3/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Meta:** official developer programs page surfaced creator/developer programs around Horizon/VR/MR, not a student ambassador path for the `meta-models` census identity. [Official programs page](https://developers.meta.com/resources/programs/).
- **Superwhisper:** official documentation labels its community projects independent and not maintained by the company; this is not an ambassador route. [Official docs](https://superwhisper.com/docs/get-started/community).

## Bounded non-hits and limitations

For the remaining exact-name entities, Exa surfaced owner-controlled profiles, project repositories, or organization/research pages, but no relevant official student ambassador/community leadership application in the reviewed result set. The CSV retains those profile/source links and labels each as a bounded search non-hit, not proof that no program exists. Several census entries appear to be individual model creators rather than organizations (e.g. Hugging Face/GitHub profile evidence); source tier was preserved unchanged as requested.

Search coverage: one exact-name + program-type Exa query per each of 50 entities, up to three/four results per query, plus focused official-domain searches noted above. I did not exhaust X, LinkedIn, regional/social channels, all organization subdomains, unindexed forms, or every local chapter. Search results and pages were reviewed on 2026-11-10, one day after requested cutoff; pages without explicit date/status are labeled undated/uncertain rather than assumed open. India eligibility, age/year/discipline, MUJ fit, workload, stipend and noncash benefits are marked unknown unless the cited official source states them. No forms were submitted and no one was contacted.

Duplicate status checks are limited to earlier curated batch files through row 900, not every central ledger or research artifact. The user profile used for fit is user-supplied: 18+, second-year B.Tech ECE at Manipal University Jaipur.
