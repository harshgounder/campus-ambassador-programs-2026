# Asia regional and local-language AI ambassador/community sweep

**Cutoff requested:** 2026-11-09. Research performed 2026-11-10. Live pages fetched after cutoff do not establish status on Nov 9 without dated or historical evidence. This is a high-recall discovery pass, not an exhaustive census.

## Results

The companion CSV preserves four distinct regional opportunities/paths found in this sweep, with status and eligibility caveats separated:

- **Uedu/Yodo Campus Ambassador (Taiwan-linked site):** A potential cross-border fit worth checking. Official criteria cover tertiary students without a stated country limit, requiring an institutional email and one semester of prior Uedu use. Its application link is live, but it only says applications are accepted during an open period, without dating that period. The page identifies an LHU/Center for General Education link but the relationship and international-school acceptance were not independently confirmed.
- **BaseMe AI Ambassador Community (Japan):** A three-month, AI product co-creation/student community term ran Sept. 1–Nov. 30, 2026, and the page said rolling deadline. Page wording says "nationwide universities," which likely means Japanese universities; it does not explicitly address foreign-enrolled students. Treat MUJ eligibility as unconfirmed and probably geographically limited. It is less technical than an open-source developer ambassador role.
- **CleverCreator Bangladesh Ambassador:** Organizer page listed open applications, 50 openings and BDT 3,000–5,000/month. Eligibility explicitly requires current enrollment at a Bangladesh institution, so the MUJ candidate does not qualify. No date on the page anchors cutoff status.
- **Nexus AI4S Community Campus Geek Vanguard:** Official Chinese page describes student ambassadors, open-source AI4S work and bootcamps, with no country restriction stated. However it is undated, so cutoff status is unverified; claims about ambassador counts and labs were not independently corroborated. Keep as a low-confidence lead requiring organization validation.

## Sources and coverage

Used Exa/search-service discovery with English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and South Asian English queries; then opened official organizer pages where accessible. Checked candidate pages for Uedu, BaseMe, CleverCreator, Nexus AI4S, OpenBMB, Huawei Student Developers, BDAIC, SLAAI, AI Council of India, and a Shenzhen robotics employer listing. Also searched local campus, hardware/robotics, science-AI, developer and community-lead terms.

No X MCP was used. No accounts were created, applications submitted, contacts made, or personal data collected.

## Gaps

WeChat Official Account history, private campus groups, QR-code-only application forms, some Juejin/Feishu posts, Baidu Tieba/Gitee/Nowcoder dynamic or account-gated content, and school-internal recruitment notices are incompletely indexed. China and Japan local-language posts may have missed short-lived intakes; South/Central Asian searches outside English are especially incomplete. Findings do not establish that no other opportunities exist.
