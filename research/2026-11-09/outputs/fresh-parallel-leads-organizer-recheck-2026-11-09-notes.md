# Fresh Parallel leads: organizer recheck (2026-11-09)

Reviewed organizer-owned pages for the seven leads supplied: Super Qodoer (Qodo), Open Science Labs Ambassadors, Mamentis Student Ambassador, Hackviser Campus Ambassador, Vibe Security/VibSecurity Campus Ambassador, TryHackMe Campus Ambassador, and Bugv Student Ambassador. Search discovery alone was not treated as an application-status source. No one was contacted, and no application or waitlist form was submitted.

## Disposition

- **VibSecurity / Vibe Security — add as the one missing actionable opportunity.** Organizer page currently says “Now Accepting Applications” and provides an email route. It requires current college/university student status in its pitch, but does not publish country eligibility, work hours, or term. India acceptance is therefore not confirmed. Rewards include swag, early access, certificate, and a paid internship opportunity for top performers.
- **Mamentis — correction.** The official URL is live and gives a specific email application route, 2–3 hours/week, academic-year term, undergraduate/graduate status, and AI/CS/data science/related-field criterion. This resolves the stale page-not-found concern in `global-brave-verification.csv`; it was already substantially covered in `ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv`.
- **Hackviser — correction.** Official page currently exposes “Apply Now,” describes selection review within 2–3 weeks, and specifies 3–5 hours/week, an academic-year term, current university enrollment, cybersecurity interest, and a signed faculty reference. It calls itself a global community, with no India exclusion found. Existing `international-multilingual-search.csv` marked intake unknown and is now stale. Application destination URL was not captured, so treat open status as open-appearing rather than a guaranteed live form.
- **Qodo — correction to audit detail.** Program is duplicated in AI priority and primary-page audit outputs. Current official page says global and “apply or nominate now.” It also offers up to USD 250 per activity/month to support community activities. This is activity funding and should not be described as guaranteed personal compensation; the earlier audit saying no cash compensation was stated was incomplete.
- **Open Science Labs — conditional, no live intake.** Duplicated in raw discoveries and the primary-page audit. Official program is global but requires age 18+, an established OSL relationship, normally at least three months of meaningful contribution, and a confirming OSL contributor. The application issue template is still “coming soon.” Twelve-month volunteer role, two meaningful activities each rolling six months, quarterly-ish check-in.
- **TryHackMe — verified existing row; no delta.** Already covered in `recent-global-leads-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv`. India falls under “all other countries,” which the organizer says will open soon; the official page offers a waitlist. Existing-THM-user, current student of any subject/year, campus activity, and capacity to host offline events are required; 8–10 hours/quarter and stated platform/certification/merch benefits.
- **Bugv — correction, but exclude from candidate shortlist.** Existing `international-multilingual-search.csv` correctly identifies Nepal-only scope but lacked the current program page/contact route. Organizer's program page supplies an application/contact CTA (hr@bugv.com), but does not verify current cohort timing. The organizer's 2026 announcement limits eligibility to students at recognized colleges/universities across Nepal, so MUJ/India does not match.
