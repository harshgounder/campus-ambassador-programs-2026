# AI organization census audit — source rows 3451–3500

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive source rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied census row number, exact name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Used grouped exact-name web searches for all 50 names with student/campus ambassador, developer advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, community representative and creator terms; followed likely exact-entity matches with targeted official-site searches. The subject coverage included graph ML, data science, robotics, vision, speech/audio, RL, AI infrastructure and AI products, not only LLMs.
- Checked local-language/Chinese material where indicated by company or handle identity: AutoArk/无界方舟, and qingsongedu/青松. Searched English and Chinese terms for campus/student ambassadors and representatives. No X MCP or browser was used.
- Treated official pages and exact-entity evidence as required for a positive program finding. Excluded generic campus ambassador directories, unrelated companies’ programs, jobs/recruitment, research positions, contests, open-source contribution/maintenance and ordinary community membership when they were not named student-leader intakes for the exact census entity. A result for another IEEE society was not attributed to the `ieee8023` handle.
- No forms were submitted, accounts created, contacts made, or personal information entered.

## Cutoff, geography and candidate fit

Searches were conducted Nov 10, after the requested Nov 9, 2026 cutoff. Only dated pre-cutoff material is used to confirm a program existed by the cutoff. Pinecone’s dated 2025 launch establishes existence; its undated current waitlist does not establish exact cutoff status. AutoArk’s dated Chinese company announcements show activity before cutoff but not a student route. For the other negative findings, no dated official source established an exact-entity route’s Nov 9 availability. No qualifying route in this slice specifies the candidate’s 2029 graduation, second-year status, ECE major or age; Pinecone also states no explicit country restrictions on the reviewed pages, but India eligibility is not guaranteed.

No guaranteed compensation or workload was established for the candidate. Pinecone describes optional talk travel support up to $500 under conditions; it is not a general stipend. Other benefits should not be inferred where no qualifying route was found.

## Coverage and access limitations

This was a bounded grouped web-search pass rather than 50 exhaustive country-by-country or platform-specific audits. Search indexing and the inability to verify every handle’s legal owner constrain negative claims. Chinese checks were used for AutoArk, qingsongedu, zhizhuodemao, ElricLiu, Doubiiu and CVI-SZU; a Korean-language check was used for Kyubyong. Broad Japanese and other non-English checks were not performed. Current page observations are not backdated to Nov 9 unless a dated source supports that date. No X MCP, browser, login-gated social research, forms, accounts, contacts or personal-data entry were used.

The companion CSV uses the neighboring 13-column schema and contains exactly 50 data rows. Census row IDs are 3451–3500 in ascending order without gaps. Each row’s `name`, `tier` and `category` values are copied from the corresponding source-ledger row.
