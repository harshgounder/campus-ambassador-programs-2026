## Status rules used

- **OPEN** means the organizer explicitly states an application window is open or accepts rolling applications on the as-of date.
- **FUTURE / UPCOMING** means a future application start or opening is explicitly announced, but applications are not yet open on the as-of date.
- **OPEN-APPEARING** marks a conflicting official page where a current-looking invitation cannot be reconciled with stale/closed cycle details.
- Closed, completed, expired and undated leads were not relabeled as open. No undated/lead-only entry was promoted into the CSV without a current official application signal.

DC track location/participant work-authorization and compensation specifics were not independently resolved in this pass.
- Local university safety communities/programs (e.g. Cardiff, Nottingham, Western University) were not treated as globally open student programs.

## Limits and uncertainties

- “Worldwide” is reported only where an organizer explicitly states it. Remote delivery by itself is not taken as proof of global eligibility.
- No user age beyond 18+ was inferred. No nationality, residence, semester calendar, work authorization, prior research, or specific ML coding proficiency was assumed.
- Project AWARE's official application form was subsequently opened read-only in Brave on Nov 10; no information was entered or submitted. It confirms an immediate-start, three-month unpaid fellowship with rolling review, remote/hybrid location, no prior AI-safety experience required, and no named nationality or degree restriction. It asks applicants whether they are in Delhi NCR but does not state that non-NCR applicants are excluded. The form says 3–4 hours/week, conflicting with the opportunity page's 6–8 hours/week; eligibility and expected workload should remain caveated until the organizer clarifies.
- SPAR has announced an approximate Spring 2027 opening month but not its exact deadline or project list.
- AI Safety Camp's 2027 team-member application opens Nov 13; its project postings and project-specific skills were not yet available on Nov 9. Lead applications are closed. The 10-hour weekly commitment may be heavy for a student.
- AI Policy Lab does not set a weekly hours quota; student scopes are negotiated with mentors. It is unpaid.
- GovAI UK is in person and full-time in London; visa sponsorship and travel support are organizer-stated but individual visa approval is not guaranteed.
- Algoverse's same official page combines a Spring 2027 invitation and Nov 15 deadline with past 2026 dates and an applications-closed notice. It is deliberately labeled open-appearing, not confirmed open.
- AIGN's cohort is a paid educational service (€299), not paid employment or stipend. Its page does not explicitly state country eligibility and disclaims NATO/IEEE partnership/endorsement.
- This is a targeted opportunity pass, not an exhaustive worldwide inventory. Search coverage skews toward English-language indexed pages despite Spanish and French discovery queries; regional language/source coverage is incomplete.
