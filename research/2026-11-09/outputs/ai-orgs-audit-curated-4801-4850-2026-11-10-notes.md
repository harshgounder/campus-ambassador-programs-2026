# Audit notes: census rows 4801–4850

- Coverage: 50 consecutive source rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`. Exact row number, name, tier and category retained in the 13-column output.
- Method: screened exact census names and supplied result URLs for organizer-owned student/campus/community ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, chapter, representative, and creator routes. Search snippets and third-party directories were discovery only. Most census entries are account/project labels, and an unrelated official program is not evidence that the handle/entity owns it.
- False-match checks: `robocorp` (4801) search leads included RCAP Ambassador Program. Its official page identifies RoboCup Asia-Pacific Confederation as organizer, so it is not Robocorp and was excluded. `hackerkid` (4807) search leads included HackerRank Campus Crew; HackerRank's official page identifies HackerRank as organizer, so it was excluded. Likewise, official LUKSO On Campus (lead for `proxysoul`, 4831) and OpenAI Codex Ambassadors (lead for `open-thought`, 4843) were not assigned to those census entities without identity/ownership evidence.
- Other supplied leads (including Huawei developer ambassador pages, Microsoft Student Ambassadors, and unrelated student ambassador lists) were not attributed to handles or projects absent a verified relationship. Adjacent internships and ordinary contributor/community participation were not counted.
- Date/geography: no qualifying exact-entity route had dated evidence establishing open status on 2026-11-09. India eligibility is not inferred from a global label, an Indian participant, or a result surfaced for an India-based search.
- No X MCP, forms, applications, or external contact used. Bounded screening does not prove absence or overall completeness.
