# AI organization census audit — source rows 3351–3400

Prepared 2026-11-10. Requested evidence cutoff: 2026-11-09. Candidate profile: adult second-year B.Tech ECE student at Manipal University Jaipur (India), expected graduation 2029.

## Coverage and method

- Audited 50 consecutive rows from `census-search-ledger-2026-11-09.csv`; copied census row number, name, tier and category verbatim and in order into the companion 13-column CSV.
- Used grouped exact-name searches across the 50 handle/project names with student ambassador, campus ambassador, advocate, champion, fellow, developer ambassador and community leader terms, alongside topic and official-site searches for apparent matches. Search themes included robotics, AI/ML beyond LLMs, RL, developer tools, computer vision, NLP and software security. A Chinese-language query was included for FudanNLP; language coverage was otherwise limited and should not be read as exhaustive.
- Checked official organization pages for apparent program leads. Search output gave official candidate routes for Arm, Snyk, Neuromatch Academy, plus unrelated MemMachine and TinyFish matches. Only the first three were treated as exact-entity routes in this range. No X MCP or browser was used.
- Excluded generic campus-ambassador directories and unrelated third-party schemes (including OpenAI Codex Ambassadors, MemMachine and TinyFish when they appeared for unrelated census handles), jobs, recruitment, ordinary project contribution and community membership as evidence of a role with a different exact entity. No forms were submitted, accounts created, contacts made, or personal information entered.
- No X MCP or login-gated social research was used. The research was public-web-search based; grouped results and index coverage limit negative findings.

## Organization-specific findings

- **ARM-software (row 3361):** Arm’s official Developer Program has an Arm Ambassador program for experienced developers, educators, creators and technical/community leaders, spanning AI, cloud, edge, embedded, mobile, gaming, graphics, automotive and physical AI. Requirements include age 18+, Arm technology familiarity, technical communication, and an existing record of community leadership/contributions. Applications are described as opening every six months/twice a year; the site directs applicants to a waitlist if closed. No dated source established its exact Nov 9, 2026 open/closed state. India exclusion was not found; candidate age qualifies, but the experienced-leader criteria are not proven for this candidate. Sources: [Arm Ambassador overview](https://developer.arm.com/arm-developer-program/ambassadors), [application requirements](https://developer.arm.com/arm-developer-program/ambassadors/application), [criteria and process](https://developer.arm.com/arm-developer-program/ambassadors/criteria-and-application-process).
- **snyk (row 3366):** Snyk’s official, active-looking Ambassador page asks for experienced Snyk users from Enterprise customers and describes AppSec talks, webinars, articles and other community advocacy. It is not a campus-specific program and is not specifically an AI/ML opportunity. The undated page says it is always looking for new members, which does not prove the page’s state on the exact Nov 9 cutoff. Country, student status and India eligibility are not specified; enterprise customer/user criteria are not established for the candidate. Source: [Snyk Ambassador Program](https://snyk.io/snyk-ambassadors).
- **NeuromatchAcademy (row 3391):** Official Neuromatch materials describe volunteer ambassadors who answer potential-student questions, connect university clubs and present about courses. The program says it recruits yearly, usually July–October, and shows a 2025–2026 cohort / 2026 ambassadors. Course and teaching-assistant participation are not themselves ambassador roles. The usual window had elapsed by Nov 9, but no dated notice confirmed 2026 closure or the next intake. Global participation is implied by the international roster, with no India or academic-major restriction found; candidate enrollment fit and active intake remain unverified. Sources: [Neuromatch Ambassadors](https://neuromatch.io/ambassadors/), [current and past ambassadors](https://neuromatch.io/neuromatch-ambassadors).
- **Fuxi / fuxicodex (row 3369):** The source row returned OpenAI Codex Ambassadors. This is a distinct OpenAI program, not an ambassador role for the Fuxi census handle/project; it is excluded. Source locator for census identity: [Fuxi GitHub handle](https://github.com/fuxicodex); unrelated search lead: [OpenAI Codex Ambassadors](https://developers.openai.com/community/codex-ambassadors).
- **tinyvision (row 3370):** A TinyFish ambassador result was a different name and is excluded. No exact tinyvision program was confirmed. Source: [tinyvision GitHub handle](https://github.com/tinyvision).
- **coder (row 3392):** MemMachine’s student ambassador program appeared against the generic query “coder.” MemMachine is a separate AI-agent memory project, not the exact census entity, so its route is excluded. Source locator: [coder GitHub handle](https://github.com/coder); unrelated program: [MemMachine Student Ambassador Program](https://memmachine.ai/community-student-ambassador-program).
- **Other names:** Exact-name and grouped searches largely resolved to individual developer handles, small software projects, repositories, labs or ambiguous names. Source-ledger entries for unrelated ambassador programs do not establish ownership by these names. No other exact-entity student/campus/community leader intake was confirmed. This bounded negative result is not proof that no local, private, unindexed or future program exists.

## Coverage and access limitations

This was a bounded grouped web-search pass rather than 50 exhaustive local-language/platform audits. Exact handle-to-legal-entity ownership remains uncertain for many repository-style names. Search indexing is not proof of absence; some candidate program pages are undated or current observations and cannot be backdated to Nov 9. Local-language checks were limited to Chinese terms for FudanNLP; broad Chinese, Korean, Japanese and other regional coverage was not performed. No X MCP, browser, forms, account creation, contacts or personal-data entry were used.
