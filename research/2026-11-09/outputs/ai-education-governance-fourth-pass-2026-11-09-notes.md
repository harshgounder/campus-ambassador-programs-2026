# AI education, ethics and governance opportunity pass — 2026-11-09

## Scope and approach

This pass searched for student/campus ambassador, advocate, volunteer, chapter-organizer and similar AI education, ethics, responsible-AI, policy and public-interest opportunities, including non-English and regional leads. The fit field is calibrated to an 18+ second-year ECE undergraduate at Manipal University Jaipur graduating in 2029. The output is a targeted delta, not a complete registry.

Before discovery, the existing `outputs/ai-*.csv` files were reviewed for program names and scopes, including the education/chapter, regional student, Africa/MENA, Latin America, Asia, safety/policy, organization-specific and verification lanes. Exact-name searches were then run across those AI CSVs for the two selected programs. The named exclusions in the task were not repeated. Existing related programs such as Ethical AI Society, Youth for Responsible AI (closed 2026 cohort), ZAKA, AI4Ghana, AIforAfricanGirls, Latin America chapter networks, Pathfinder, AI Youth Alliance, and the other listed safety/policy programs were left to their existing records.

Discovery used connected Exa web search (agent-reach CLI was unavailable in this runtime; no social/X MCP was used). Query themes included official student AI ethics / responsible-AI ambassador applications, AI governance student roles, non-English Latin America calls, African AI education ambassadors, and globally open AI education chapter programs. Organizer pages were fetched for the candidates. Search results and third-party opportunity listings were discovery only, not evidence for the rows.

## Included sources and findings

- LatinX in AI (LXAI), volunteer roles: `https://www.latinxinai.org/volunteer`; organization scope: `https://www.latinxinai.org/about`; membership and ally context: `https://www.latinxinai.org/member-guide`. The organizer lists ongoing leadership roles (including Regional Liaison and Academic Club Organizer), says most volunteer roles can be done globally/virtually, and says opportunities open throughout the year. It explicitly says volunteer roles are unpaid. Role openings and acceptance are not guaranteed, and the mission is specifically focused on LatinX participation in AI; an applicant from India should treat fit as conditional and approach it as a genuine ally contribution.
- Utrecht University, student AI feedback pool: `https://www.uu.nl/en/organisation/ai-policy/students`. The official page invites students to sign up to a pool and says participation is optional, case-by-case, and the list is cleaned each academic year. The wording and university context make it a Utrecht-specific route, so it is not a fit for a Manipal student. It is retained as a geographically bounded European example and explicitly labeled as such.

## Status and eligibility rules

- “Current” is used only where the organizer presently invites signup or says role opportunities open throughout the year. It does not imply an appointment, an available seat, or a successful form submission.
- An interest or feedback pool is not described as a formal ambassador appointment.
- Remote availability does not prove universal eligibility. Geographic restrictions or institution-specific scope are reported as stated or carefully inferred from the organizer's page context.
- No personal information was entered, no application was submitted, and no organization was contacted.
- No X MCP was used. No central ledger was modified.

## Limitations

This is a focused discovery pass, not an exhaustive inventory of all countries or languages. Search results remained strongly English-weighted despite regional and non-English query attempts; regional searches often surfaced entries already captured in earlier lanes. Only two new source-supported rows met the no-duplicate and meaningful-source-evidence threshold in this pass. One is a conditional global volunteer route; the other is geographically out of scope for the user. Application forms were not submitted or tested for backend acceptance. No attempt was made to resolve organizer selection availability or user-specific work authorization beyond published facts.
