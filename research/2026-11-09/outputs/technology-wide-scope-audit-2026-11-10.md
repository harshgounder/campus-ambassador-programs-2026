# Worldwide technology opportunity scope audit

**Observed:** 10 Nov 2026. **Applicant lens:** India; Manipal University Jaipur; B.Tech Electrical and Computer Engineering; year 2; age 18+; graduating 2029. **Scope:** worldwide tech-industry opportunities across AI and non-AI. This is an archive coverage and current-status audit, not a claim that the entire worldwide opportunity space has been exhausted. No forms were submitted and no organizations were contacted.

## Coverage finding

The project has broad lead generation across tech, but it is not complete enough to say “all tech opportunities worldwide” have been found or checked. The `READ-ME-FIRST.md` checkpoint describes an ongoing census, not an exhaustive list; it reports 5,745 organization/curated AI entries screened in source slices and a 9,304-row small-tier queue only partly reviewed (3,550 ranks covered; 5,754 still unreviewed), plus 36,323 individual-tier entries still to review. Its 410-CSV structure scan establishes file structure, not opportunity coverage. AI/ML receives the most concentrated coverage; general CS/software, open source, cloud, cybersecurity, startups, hardware and technology are identified as subsequent work. The whole-project checklist/queue therefore remains a real gap.

## Closed, restricted, and adjacent routes worth retaining in the ledger

- **GitHub Campus Experts** — official documentation says application opens each July for one month; requires age 18+, formal higher education, at least one year remaining, GitHub account at least six months old, and Student Developer Pack. The applicant profile may fit the basic timeline, but the 2026 window is closed as of 10 Nov; next expected annual opening is July 2027, not an active current route. [Official application documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/education/explore-the-benefits-of-teaching-and-learning-with-github-education/applying-to-be-a-github-campus-expert).
- **Firefox Student Ambassadors** — legacy program is explicitly outdated/replaced by Campus Clubs and no longer accepts new Firefox Student Ambassadors. Closed/replaced, not open. [Legacy program page](https://wiki.mozilla.org/StudentAmbassadors).
- **UiPath Student Developer Champions** — official student page has an application route, but the cited 2026 cycle deadline was 30 Sep 2026; closed by observation date. [Program page](https://community.uipath.com/uipath-student-developers-program/).
- **Google Cloud Champions** — Opportunity Max marks this killed/merged into GDE; do not count separately as an active student route without a distinct current application.
- **GSoC 2026** — cycle closed; future annual cycle is not a currently open opportunity.
- **Snyk** is open on its official page but conditional on being a Snyk customer and described as an expert at an Enterprise customer; this distinction is essential for fit.
- **Debian New Member / Debian Developer** — contributor and membership processes are live, but the formal developer path requires prior project contribution and is not an ambassador/student intake. Treat as adjacent open source participation.
- **Salesforce Trailblazer, OpenUK, Civo, CNCF and broad community memberships** — retain as adjacent or intake-unknown unless a specific active ambassador application is verified.

## Practical shortlist from this scope pass

Best apparent match from direct official-page evidence: **MathWorks MATLAB Student Ambassadors** (global campus program, paid, ECE-relevant, live Apply Now). **Agora** is a credible global student/developer community path if the applicant meets its community-building, voice/developer-tools interest and local-language conditions. **Sarvam Champions** has strong India geography and rolling applications, but needs stronger applicant-specific project/community evidence. **Snyk** and **GitKraken** display rolling/open-looking applications yet require product expertise and are not necessarily student roles. **ICSC** is an adjacent CS competition outreach route with an active-looking form. **OnePlus Campus** is conditional on a OnePlus device and current cohort availability.

This is a shortlist for further applicant review, not a ranking of guaranteed acceptance. The strongest remaining work is source discovery and current organizer-page checks across the uncovered areas in the table above, then deduplication against the central priority ledger. The archive and this pass do not support a claim that every open technology opportunity worldwide has been identified.
