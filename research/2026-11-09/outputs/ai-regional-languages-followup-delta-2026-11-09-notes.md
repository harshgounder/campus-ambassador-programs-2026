## Scope and method

Ran fresh multilingual web discovery across Chinese (traditional), Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Indonesian and Filipino query variants, then opened organizer-owned sources for potential candidates. Used agent-reach's Exa web-search route; the local `agent-reach doctor --json` command was unavailable in this environment. Search results were treated only as discovery leads. Existing regional AI outputs were checked for exact-name overlap before recording.

Its student eligibility geography and weekly workload are unpublished; this is not an open route.

## Limits

This is a targeted source pass, not an exhaustive country-by-country census. The two CSV rows are lead/correction records, not active openings. No form was completed and no organization was contacted.
