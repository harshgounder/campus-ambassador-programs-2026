# AI education and student-network research delta — 2026-11-09

## Scope and method

Focused on AI education organizations, university/student club networks, research/community education organizations, and AI learning platforms. Excluded programs already named in the two requested comparison files: `ai-ml-priority-verification-delta-2026-11-09.csv` and `ambassador-research-first-wave-2026-11-09.csv`. Also screened against obvious adjacent names in the wider output directory when practical; several hits (including DeepStation, Trinka AI, Muneo.ai, 2077AI, ZAKA, and AI4ALL Ignite) were already covered elsewhere and were not repeated here.

Read the Exa Search skill's `references/searching.md` before discovery. Ran 12 Exa searches with requested result counts 15,15,15,15, then 5,5,5,5, then 10,10,10: **115 search-result slots reviewed** (the skill's source-counting measure; not a count of unique pages). Then used organizer-owned page fetches and opened WAC program/application lines directly to confirm details. Search results were deduplicated by candidate; each kept record was checked against an organizer-owned source before being recorded.

## Findings

- **Best conditional fit: WAC Student Campus Ambassador Programme.** Official page explicitly accepts enrolled students aged 18+ in any country and subject, offers a live on-page application, expects 2–3 hours/month, and sets no visible deadline. For the described MUJ student, stated age, country and subject criteria appear to fit; India-specific operational/payment/privacy details remain unverified, so this is a conditional fit, not a guarantee.
- **Dala Studio (Gebeya):** genuine campus ambassador program and page invites applications; 1–2 events/semester and credits/support are described. Its Pan-African-network language leaves India acceptance unresolved, and no deadline/status signal is stated.
- **AI Student Club:** role source says “Now Recruiting · Spring 2025” and defines Regional Ambassador zones as four US regions. Treat current intake as stale/unclear and India fit as poor absent a new international role. The club-partnership track is separate and not an individual ambassador role.
- **AI Youth Alliance:** useful AI literacy / student-chapter adjacent lead, but not a named ambassador program. Its official 2025–26 season has ended and the page says 2026–27 details are forthcoming; its documented network is school-based, so university chapter eligibility is unknown.
- **JA Worldwide AI Ambassador Program 2026:** a real AI education ambassador cohort but the global group required JA staff, and the deadline was 2026-03-20. This does not establish undergraduate student eligibility; 16–25 refers to youth reached by the program, not applicants.

## Coverage and limitations

This was a bounded discovery pass, not a complete worldwide census. It emphasized English-language Exa discovery and organizer sources surfaced by the selected search angles; regional, non-English, invitation-only, and poorly indexed programs may be missed. Search results are similarity-ranked and the 115 count includes repeats/irrelevant pages. Current application page wording is not proof that a form will accept an applicant from every country; no forms were entered or submitted and no organizers were contacted. Dala's page content is partially inaccessible to text extraction, and the AIYA join flow's exact form destination was not verified. AI Student Club and JA applications were not tested; their dates/status are inferred only from organizer-published pages.
