**Cutoff:** 2026-11-09. Research and page checks were performed on 2026-11-10. A page that is live today is not, by itself, proof that an intake was open yesterday.

## Result

No new, distinct China-ecosystem program with both a first-party actionable route and verified India eligibility was established in this bounded pass. The useful result is a source recheck of already-tracked OpenBMB MiniCPM Global Ambassador, Qwen Ambassador, Kimi Global Ambassador, and ModelScope Co-Builder. Those are supplemental updates only; the CSV explicitly marks them as existing candidates so they are not counted twice.

The strongest cutoff evidence is OpenBMB’s official English community-events list, which marked its MiniCPM Global Ambassador Program “Open call” and “Ongoing program.” The page was indexed about three days before the 2026-11-10 check, placing the observed page state near the Nov 9 cutoff. The official Chinese MiniCPM page also labels a 2026 cohort, shows an India-based global ambassador, and directly says there are no regional limits. It does not say that applications guarantee selection into that already-listed cohort, nor does it show age, student-year, hours, stipend, or an application form in the extracted text. [Official MiniCPM ambassador page](https://openbmb.cn/docs/community/ambassador) · [Official OpenBMB community-events list](https://openbmb.cn/docs/en/community)

Official rechecks for Qwen, Kimi and ModelScope reaffirm program activities and routes but do not create dated Nov 9 evidence. Kimi’s page currently states worldwide intent, includes students among target community participants, and links an online application; no explicit country rule, age, or student-year requirement is given. Qwen’s official indexed page adds material contribution and benefit terms (including 4 monthly content outputs or 1 event plus 2 posts for $100 monthly API credits), but its page is undated and does not confirm Nov 9 status. ModelScope’s page presents the Co-Builder route, but cross-border eligibility remains unresolved.
