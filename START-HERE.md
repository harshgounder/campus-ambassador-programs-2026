## Rules that save you from wasting a slot

- OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor/SpaceXAI and Solana restrict holding another ambassador role at the
  same time. Pick one AI flagship. Sarvam and Gemini are the two best for you.
- Never pay to join anything. Every row here is free.
- Apply from your college email and keep the confirmation mail.
- Do not list a program as applied until the form returns a confirmation screen.

## Do not apply here

- **Unstop Campus Champions**: application page says closed, deadline was 16 Sep 2026.
- **IncuXai**: campus ambassador subdomain is down as of 5 Nov.
- **Sarvam Next**: page is 404, and it was IIT/IISc/ISB/NIT only anyway.
- **Microsoft Copilot, Adobe, Canva Campus, Simplyr**: US or UK or Australia only.
- **Huawei, Baidu, Tencent, ByteDance, Zhipu, MiniMax, Xiaomi**: China only.
