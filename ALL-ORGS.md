## What NOT CHECKED means

It means no ambassador program row exists for that organisation yet. It does not mean the
organisation has no program. This is the honest gap: 444 of 486 curated organisations still
need an individual check.

## The long tail

`data/ai-universe.csv` has 13,981 names from Hugging Face (9,195), GitHub (4,567) and the
curated layer (486). The notable 1,097 were probed for ambassador pages. The remaining
~12,884 are mostly individual model uploaders and small repo owners, not companies.
