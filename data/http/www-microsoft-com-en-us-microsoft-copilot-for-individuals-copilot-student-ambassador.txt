Networking You’ll be connected to an ambassador cohort network across 100+ college campuses, collaborating in both individual and partner-led events. Impact As part of an active feedback loop with the Copilot team at Microsoft, you’ll help influence how the Copilot experience meets the needs of students. You could be a perfect fit We’re looking for creative , influential individuals who are naturals at connecting with people at events, through clubs and organizations, and through social media. You’re passionate about AI , eager to share and communicate , and excited to help lead campus initiatives to represent a global brand . Every class has a syllabus. Here’s ours To become a Copilot Ambassador, you need to be a full-time student and a Copilot user enrolled at one of our affiliated four-year universities . Students from all majors and backgrounds are welcome. The program is designed to complement a full-time academic schedule. Expect to dedicate around 3-5 hours per week including training , campus events , and content creation . In return, you’ll receive compensation of up to $1,500 upon completion of the program if all responsibilities are met. And there may be opportunities to earn additional cash bonuses for exemplary work. Ready to come aboard? Start your application on the official portal. Applications open in June 2026 and will fill up on a rolling basis. Be prepared to provide supporting materials, such as your resume, socials, and other relevant materials.
