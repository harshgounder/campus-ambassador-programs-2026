Hedera is seeking community members across different regions to act as a bridge between the Hedera team and the broader community, helping with outreach, education, content creation, event organization, and onboarding new community members. Whether you are a developer with technical expertise or a content creator and community leader, there is an opportunity for you to contribute and help grow the Hedera ecosystem. Apply Now a global network of community leaders Overview The Hedera Ambassador Program identifies, trains, and empowers both technical and community leaders worldwide. Developer Ambassadors bring programming expertise in blockchain, coding, and smart contracts, while Community &amp; Events Ambassadors excel in organizing, marketing, and community building. Global expansion The Hedera Ambassador Program is growing globally, empowering local leaders to build and strengthen Hedera communities around the world. With ambassadors across North America, Latin America, Africa, and Asia, the program continues to expand into new regions, connecting communities and creating more opportunities to learn, build, and engage with the Hedera ecosystem. Benefits and opportunities As an ambassador, you’ll gain access to ongoing learning opportunities, network with teams contributing to Hedera, and gain professional recognition.
