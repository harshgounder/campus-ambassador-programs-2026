Host local events, bring builders together, and partner with Anthropic to shape the future of Claude. Apply now (opens in new tab) What you get The Claude Community Ambassadors program gives you everything you need to create a thriving community of builders in your city. Grow your local community Host meetups, workshops, and hackathons that bring builders in your city together. We cover event funding, ready-to-use content, swag, and promotion through Anthropic&#x27;s channels. Build and experiment Stay sharp and teach from experience. Run live demos, prototype ideas, and share what you know with your community. Get monthly API credits from Anthropic to power your demos. Shape the product Bring your community&#x27;s perspective to Anthropic’s product teams. Access pre-release features and Builders Council sessions to influence what gets built. Connect and contribute Share what&#x27;s working in your city and help shape how this program grows through a private Slack with fellow Ambassadors and the Anthropic team. Our growing community Claude Community Events are hosted globally by Claude Community Ambassadors. Meet others building with Claude, and keep thinking. Learn more Our ambassadors If you’re active in your community and want to go further with Anthropic&#x27;s support, we encourage you to apply. No developer title required. Community builders You bring people together. You organize meetups, lead local groups, or hold a visible presence on Discord, Reddit, or X. Technical users You have hands-on experience with Claude Code or Claude Cowork and can help others get started and go deeper. Claude advocates You&#x27;re genuinely excited about Claude and Anthropic&#x27;s mission. You help others learn naturally and give honest feedback. How it works Ambassadors can be from any background and from anywhere in the world. Multiple ambassadors from the same city are welcome. Apply now (opens in new tab) Applications Complete the application. Tell us about your background, your experience, and why you want to build your local Claude Community. Selections We review every application. Qualified candidates are invited to interview in a short screening call. Onboarding Sign our Ambassador Agreement, join the private Slack, and start planning your first event. FAQ Is there a cost to join? No. Event sponsorship, API credits, swag, and resources and more are all provided at no cost to you. Do I need to be a professional developer? No. You should have meaningful experience with Claude Code or Claude Cowork and a track record of community involvement, but a developer title isn&#x27;t required. Do I need to live in a specific location? No. The program is global and we actively prioritize geographic diversity. Multiple Ambassadors can operate in the same city. Will I receive compensation? The program doesn&#x27;t include direct payment. Benefits include event sponsorship, API credits, networking, swag, and recognition. How long does membership last? Membership is ongoing as long as you&#x27;re hosting events and staying engaged. If circumstances change, you can step away and reapply when you&#x27;re ready. Can I be an ambassador for other companies? Yes, in most cases. We ask that you&#x27;re not also an ambassador for a company whose core business competes with Anthropic&#x27;s. If unsure, mention it in your application and we&#x27;ll follow up. Can I talk about being an Ambassador publicly? Yes! We encourage you to share it on social media, your resume, and LinkedIn. Ready to grow your city’s Claude Community? Applications are open. If you’re a builder who brings people together, we’d love to hear from you.
