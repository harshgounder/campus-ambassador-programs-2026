So far this year, Women Techmakers have: 412 events held with 83K attendees 198 unique pieces of written work with a combined 7M views 311 speaking engagements in front of 54K attendees 71K minutes of collective mentoring For International Women’s Day alone, Ambassadors ran and contributed to 300 events that impacted 27,000 people and published content that reached 6 million viewers, globally in March and April 2026.
