Questions &amp; Answers What are the criteria for acceptance into this program? ↓ ↑ Here&#x27;s what we&#x27;re looking for: You must be at least 18 years of age You must complete the application form, including reviewing our Code of Conduct You can commit to contributing to the Cursor community through forum moderation, hosting community meetups and hackathons (and whatever ideas you&#x27;re excited about!) We&#x27;re looking for people who want to make our community vibrant, welcoming, and collaborative. When will I know if I&#x27;m a Cursor Ambassador? ↓ ↑ We review applications once a week. We&#x27;ll do our best to get back to you within 2-3 weeks of filling out the application. We&#x27;re excited to get more great people on board! If I get accepted into the program, what happens next? ↓ ↑ Look out for an email from the Cursor team with next steps. We&#x27;ll set up a chat with you, add you in our Slack group and help you get started! How long is the commitment? ↓ ↑ You choose, totally up to you! We&#x27;re excited to have you on board as long as you&#x27;d like. After a year, you can choose to renew your participation if you&#x27;d like to continue on as a Cursor Ambassador. If you&#x27;re not involved on a regular basis, we&#x27;ll reach out to check in and see if still relevant for you. If at any point you are no longer able to participate in the program, message us. We appreciate you! Can I participate in other advocacy programs? ↓ ↑ Our preference is that you not participate in other advocacy programs while you&#x27;re in Cursor&#x27;s Ambassador program. Do I have to be fluent in English to be an Ambassador? ↓ ↑ No need! We&#x27;re excited to partner with Ambassadors who speak different languages. Where can I reach out with questions? ↓ ↑ Feel free to email blang@cursor.com with any questions. Where can I find the Cursor community code of conduct? ↓ ↑ See our Cursor Community Code of Conduct .
