They host meetups, highlight what their city is creating, and bring Lovable moments to life on the ground alongside our team. They&#x27;re organizers with a track record: people who already run meetups, communities and events, and know how to fill a room. Campus Leads Campus Leads bring Lovable to their campus through meetups, workshops and activations, so classmates ship real projects and a campus learns what&#x27;s possible. They sit at the centre of campus life: club founders, group leaders, and faculty who want their students to dream bigger. Industry Leads Industry Leads are respected in their field and quick to see where Lovable fits before others do. They drive adoption through their network and their platform, and they surface new use cases for Lovable from inside their domain. Educator Leads Educator Leads are teachers at heart, with a deep feel for what Lovable can do. They guide people from curious to capable through content, instruction, office hours, livestreams and webinars. Community Champions Community Champions make the Lovable Discord a place worth returning to. They answer questions, welcome newcomers, and turn a stuck builder into a shipping one. They lead online rather than on stage: recognised for showing up every day, sharing what they know, and setting the tone others follow. Want to be an ambassador? Applications are closed right now.
