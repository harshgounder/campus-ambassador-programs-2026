The IEEE Communications Society (ComSoc) proudly launches the IEEE ComSoc Student Ambassador Program 2026, a global leadership initiative designed to empower passionate students and young professionals to champion innovation in communications technology. This prestigious program identifies outstanding ComSoc student leaders worldwide who are eager to strengthen the global IEEE community, promote emerging technologies, and inspire fellow students through impactful initiatives. ComSoc Student Ambassadors serve as global representatives of the Communications Society, helping expand IEEE engagement through leadership, collaboration, outreach, and innovation. What ComSoc Student Ambassadors Do Selected ambassadors will play an important role in advancing IEEE ComSoc initiatives through leadership and community engagement. Lead Innovative Initiatives Design and organize impactful technical events, workshops, hackathons, webinars, and awareness campaigns focused on communications technologies and emerging innovations. Collaborate Globally Work closely with IEEE Student Branches, ComSoc Chapters, Young Professionals (YP), and global volunteers to build a vibrant international community. Promote IEEE ComSoc Amplify ComSoc programs, research activities, conferences, and initiatives through social media, digital platforms, and community outreach. Foster Community Engagement Mentor students, support peer learning, and help bridge academia, research, and industry collaborations. Share Impact and Achievements Document and report program activities by submitting progress reports, event summaries, and outreach achievements throughout the program year. Program Timeline – 2026 Phase 1: Application &amp; Selection This phase focuses on identifying the most motivated and capable student leaders from across the globe. Application Period: 4 May 2026 – 30 May 2026 Application Review &amp; Evaluation: 31 May – 10 June 2026 Selection Notifications: 20–25 July 2026 Phase 2: Onboarding &amp; Training Selected ambassadors will undergo orientation and leadership preparation. Vetting, Onboarding &amp; Orientation: 25 July–10 August 2026 Key Activities • Orientation and onboarding webinar • Ambassador role briefing • Goal-setting sessions • Mentor assignment and networking Phase 3: Execution &amp; Engagement Ambassadors will actively organize events, outreach programs, and technical initiatives. Student Ambassador Activities Begin: 16 July 2026 Key Activities • Technical workshops and seminars • Student engagement programs • Social media outreach campaigns • Collaborations with IEEE chapters and societies Mid-Year Report Due and Announcement: 15 September 2026 Phase 4: Recognition &amp; Graduation Evaluation and recognition of ambassador achievements. Timeline: October 2026 – December 2026 Final Reports Due: End of October 2026 Awards, Certificates &amp; Closing Ceremony: December 2026 Eligibility Requirements Applicants must meet the following criteria: Must be an active IEEE Communications Society (ComSoc) student member at the time of application and throughout the program duration Must demonstrate leadership, initiative, and strong communication skills Must have a passion for communications technologies, research, innovation, and community development Must be willing to organize and participate in technical and outreach activities Rules &amp; Code of Conduct To maintain a professional and productive ambassador community, all participants must follow these guidelines: Professional Conduct Ambassadors must represent IEEE ComSoc with integrity, professionalism, and respect in all activities. Attendance &amp; Participation Participation in orientation sessions, training webinars, and progress meetings is mandatory. Communication &amp; Reporting Ambassadors must maintain regular communication with mentors and program coordinators and submit required reports on time. Ethics &amp; Compliance Academic dishonesty, plagiarism, misuse of IEEE platforms, or violation of IEEE policies is strictly prohibited. Evaluation Criteria Applications and ambassador performance will be evaluated based on the following categories: Leadership &amp; Initiative — 25% Demonstrated leadership in organizing events, managing teams, and driving innovative initiatives. Communication &amp; Outreach — 20% Public speaking, workshop facilitation, storytelling, and digital engagement. Technical &amp; Innovation Mindset — 20% Interest in emerging technologies, research contributions, and IEEE participation. Community Engagement &amp; Collaboration — 15% Mentoring students, networking with IEEE chapters, and building collaborations. Vision &amp; Motivation — 10% Alignment with the IEEE ComSoc mission and clear personal goals. Digital &amp; Social Media Engagement — 10% Creative promotion of ComSoc activities through online platforms. Ongoing Evaluation &amp; Performance Monitoring Ambassadors will be assessed throughout the program using structured milestones. Milestone Reports Mid-Year Report Due and Announcement: 15 September 2026 Final Reports Due: End of October 2026 Key Performance Indicators • Quality and scale of events organized • Level of engagement with IEEE chapters and student branches • Creativity and reach of social media campaigns • Leadership demonstrated through project coordination Recognition &amp; Awards Outstanding ambassadors will receive recognition for their contributions to the IEEE ComSoc community. Program Benefits Certificate of Recognition for all successful ambassadors IEEE ComSoc Ambassador Excellence Award for top performers Opportunities for leadership roles in global IEEE ComSoc initiatives Travel grants for international IEEE conferences Travel Grant Opportunity Ambassadors may receive travel grants of up to US$1,500 to attend the prestigious IEEE Global Communications Conference (GLOBECOM 2026) or other IEEE ComSoc conferences, subject to eligibility and available funding. Ambassadors are responsible for first going through and clearing the IEEE vetting process before they book any travel. Vetting can take anywhere from 3-10 weeks. All ambassadors will then book all travel and follow the approved IEEE travel reimbursement process, which will be documented and distributed to all ambassadors when they are notified of their selection as one of the top 10 ambassadors who have been awarded a travel grant.
