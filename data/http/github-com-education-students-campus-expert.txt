Become a campus expert Students guiding students Students guiding students Campus Experts are bridging the gap between industry and academia for their local communities, and doing so by recognizing the inherent struggles many students face in learning industry best practices. Campus Experts are students that are passionate about learning opportunities beyond just a classroom; that’s why they work together to bring tech community events to as many of their peers as possible. Hackathons, meetups, and more Whether online, in person, or hybrid - through workshops, guest lectures, webinars, hackathons, and more - GitHub Campus Experts are architecting multiple ways for students to learn what is too often left out of the modern higher learning curriculum. Campus Experts receive training in how to deliver these events and work together to create the best experience for every attendee. Growing skills by teaching Every Campus Expert is dedicated to a growth mindset and a belief that one learns best by teaching. By creating engaging events, with a little help from GitHub staff, Campus Experts are consistently growing their own public speaking, organizational, and tech skills while they help others to build their own necessary career skills.
