Students come together to explore cloud technology, build innovative projects, and grow their technical skills. Membership is open to any learner actively enrolled in a post-secondary institution aged 18+ who&#x27;s ready to build their future. Every group is led by Student Builder Group Leaders (formerly AWS Cloud Club Captains) who are passionate about bringing their communities to life. With support from AWS, these leaders are responsible for growing their groups, organizing inspiring events, and championing local initiatives that matter to their peers. Who can join an AWS Student Builder Group? Students must be 18+ and should have an interest in building on AWS, connecting with other developers, and being part of their local campus tech community. Why should I join an AWS Student Builder Group? Why should I join an AWS Student Builder Group? Joining an AWS Student Builder Group connects you with a community of student builders on your campus who share a passion for cloud and AI technology. As a member, you gain access to a collaborative, peer-driven environment that offers hands-on learning experiences, technical events, and resources that help you develop in-demand cloud skills. Can I start a local AWS Student Builder Group? Can I start a local AWS Student Builder Group? Yes! If there is no Student Builder Group on your campus, you can apply now to start a new Student Builder Group. If a Student Builder Group already exists on your campus, we encourage you to join and begin learning together. What is an AWS Student Builder Group Leader? What is an AWS Student Builder Group Leader? Student Builder Group Leaders are post-secondary students who guide and manage their campus Student Builder Groups with support from AWS. Group Leaders are responsible for organizing technical events, supporting local community initiatives, and growing their Group&#x27;s membership. How do AWS Student Builder Group Leaders bring cloud innovation to their campus communities? How do AWS Student Builder Group Leaders bring cloud innovation to their campus communities? Student Builder Group Leaders organize technical events, workshops, and hands-on building sessions that give students real-world experience with AWS services and AI technologies. Through community initiatives and collaborative projects, they empower fellow students to develop the skills needed to build, innovate, and make an impact. What are the benefits of being an AWS Student Builder Group Leader? What are the benefits of being an AWS Student Builder Group Leader? Student Builder Group Leaders unlock valuable benefits for themselves and their community, including receiving AWS Credits, AWS Certification exam vouchers, swag, direct communication with AWS professionals, and more. Becoming a Student Builder Group Leader helps build leadership skills while teaching others and building a thriving tech community on campus. Why did AWS Cloud Clubs evolve to AWS Student Builder Groups? Why did AWS Cloud Clubs evolve to AWS Student Builder Groups? AWS Cloud Clubs has evolved into AWS Student Builder Groups to reflect the incredible growth and impact our student communities have built over the past three years. The change also showcases student builders&#x27; continuing expansion into AI and cloud-native learning spaces. All member progress, event history, and achievements carry forward — nothing is lost.
