Learn More Student Benefit FAQs Find answers to FAQs or ask a question in our student Community Forum Student Community Forums Who is eligible for MongoDB Student benefits? MongoDB student benefits, including free MongoDB certification and $50 in MongoDB Atlas credits, are available through the GitHub Student Developer Pack. To qualify for the GitHub Student Developer Pack, you must meet the following criteria: Be enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting program. Have a verifiable school-issued email or documentation confirming your student status. Own a GitHub personal account. Be at least 13 years old. You can find additional instructions for applying to the GitHub Student Developer Pack on GitHub here. Why wasn&#x27;t my application approved by GitHub? Additional troubleshooting resources for GitHub Education access are available here. If you have specific questions regarding the GitHub Student Developer Pack or the status of your GitHub Student Developer Pack application, please contact GitHub support. How do I access free certification through MongoDB for Students? Our student benefits, including free certification, are available through the GitHub Student Developer Pack. To be eligible for free certification, students must: Be enrolled in the GitHub Student Developer Pack Verify their student status by entering their GitHub information at mongodb.com/students Complete and pass all courses associated with a certification learning path in MongoDB University. For additional details on MongoDB student benefits, please refer to the Student Resource Guide. Do my GitHub email address and MongoDB University email address need to match to qualify for a free certification voucher? No, your GitHub email address and your MongoDB University email address do not need to match to receive a free certification voucher. If the email addresses differ, please follow the instructions on this page after successfully verifying your GitHub Student Developer Pack benefits to receive your free certification voucher. I have a question related to the MongoDB certification exam. Can I reschedule my certification exam? Yes, test takers may reschedule or cancel a certification exam up to 24 hrs before the scheduled test time without penalty. To make changes, visit your learner dashboard on MongoDB University. ProctorU Support If you&#x27;re having trouble scheduling your exam or you are experiencing a technical issue, contact our proctoring partner using one of these methods: LiveChat Submit a request I missed my scheduled certification exam. Can I request another free certification voucher code? MongoDB for Students does not offer additional free certification vouchers to students who miss their scheduled certification exam. Can I take more than one certification exam? Yes, students are eligible for free certification vouchers for each related learning path they complete. To unlock additional certification benefits, you must finish the learning path associated with your first voucher before claiming another. Are there any resources to help me prepare for the certification exam? To prepare for your certification exam, you can access free exam preparation materials available on MongoDB University, including: Study Guides Practice Questions Additionally, check out the Certification Success for Students guide, specifically created to support students preparing for their MongoDB certification exam. What is the passing criteria for the certification exam? MongoDB certification exams have a pass/fail grading system, with the required passing percentage varying by exam. Candidates must meet the overall passing score, not individual domain scores, and domain weightings are detailed in the Exam Study Guide. After the exam, candidates receive a score report showing their result and areas for improvement. For more details see the Certification Program Guide. Why should I earn a MongoDB Certification? Earning a MongoDB Certification validates your skills and knowledge, helping you stand out in the tech industry. MongoDB Certified Professionals are hired by organizations worldwide, making this certification a valuable addition to your resume. It boosts your credibility, keeps you competitive, and enhances your employability in today&#x27;s job market. I&#x27;m new to MongoDB, how should I get started learning? We recommend beginning with the MongoDB Basics for Students Skill Badge. It&#x27;s an interactive way to learn MongoDB essentials while earning a Credly badge for your resume or LinkedIn profile. MongoDB Atlas is billed hourly based on usage. As part of the GitHub Student Developer Pack, we provide $50 in Atlas credits to apply to your Atlas organization. To redeem this, you&#x27;ll need a valid credit card or a PayPal account linked to your Atlas account. How do I apply the Atlas promotional code to claim free credits? Once you&#x27;ve received your Atlas promotional code, you can apply the credits to the selected Atlas organization in your account. Please note, promotional codes are redeemed at the organization level and each promotional code can only be redeemed once to an organization of your choice. Do the Atlas promotional codes expire?
