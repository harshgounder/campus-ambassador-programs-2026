Typical ambassador activities include: Spread the word Share upcoming CTF7 events with classmates, clubs and WhatsApp groups, and help first-timers register. How it works From application to onboarding Apply with the form, hear back by email, then get onboarded. Start your application Apply in five minutes Fill in the form below with your college, year, city and why you want to join. Links to your LinkedIn or Instagram help us get to know you. We review and reply The CTF7 team reviews your application and replies by email. Get onboarded Selected ambassadors are onboarded with program details, event updates and a direct line to the CTF7 team. Lead on campus Promote events (our brand guidelines cover logo and color use), help your club compete (our guide to building a CTF team is a good start) and help your college run its own events. Your contributions earn shoutouts, swag, certificates and internship opportunities. Expectations Time, give and get, and how selection works Time commitment The role is part-time and fits around classes and exams. We don't set a weekly hours quota on this page; what you take on, and when, is agreed with you at onboarding. What you give Your time and reach: sharing events, helping your club run practice rounds, introducing fest organizers to CTF7, and telling us what students want. What you get Free event access, shoutouts, recognition and CTF7 merchandise for active ambassadors, internship opportunities, and a certificate and letter of recommendation for your contribution. Selection and reply We read each application for interest in technology, activity in college life and how well you communicate. Links to your socials help.
