Frequently Asked Questions Is there any registration or program fee? No, the Campus Unite Campus Ambassador Program is 100% free for all eligible students. What is the Program Duration &amp; Activity Policy? Program Duration: 6 Months (subject to performance and active participation). Activity Policy: Inactive ambassadors may be removed from the program at any time based on performance and participation. Can first-year or diploma students apply? Yes! Students from any year (1st to 4th year UG, PG, or Diploma programs) are welcome to apply. Can multiple ambassadors belong to the same college? Yes. Selection is based on individual motivation and merit rather than arbitrary college limits. Multiple ambassadors from one campus can also collaborate as a campus team! What happens if I fail to meet the monthly target? If you do not meet the minimum monthly requirements, your account will simply continue as a regular Community Member without any penalty. You can re-apply for Ambassador status whenever you are ready. How do I track my monthly referral progress &amp; admin tasks? Once selected, you get access to a live Ambassador Portal on your Campus Unite dashboard, displaying real-time referral numbers, task submissions, and certificate claim statuses. Will I receive an official certificate? Yes! Active ambassadors who complete monthly requirements receive verified digital certificates, public profile badges, and letters of recommendation.
