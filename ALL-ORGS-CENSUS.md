## Method

1. Hugging Face: 40 pipeline tags, top 1,000 models each by downloads, likes and creation date. Author parsed from the model id. 23,986 unique authors.
2. GitHub: 50 AI topics, up to 1,000 repos each. Owner parsed from the repo. 4,568 owners in the first pass, more running.
3. Curated: 486 named companies with a country.
4. Tier by downloads and stars. Category by the highest-priority pipeline tag.

## Limits

- Tier is a proxy for 'is this a company'. It is not a verification.
- Country is only present for the curated 486.
- The individual tier is mostly people, not organisations.
